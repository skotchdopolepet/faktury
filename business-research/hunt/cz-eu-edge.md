### 1. Vintage Czechoslovak glass, Christmas glass and porcelain "picker" shop (Etsy Vintage + eBay)
- **What you sell / type:** Physical resale. You buy mid-century Czechoslovak pressed and art glass (Sklo Union, Rosice, Libochovice, Harrachov), vintage Czech blown-glass Christmas ornaments and "cibulák" (blue onion) porcelain. Sources are Czech flea markets, bazos.cz, aukro.cz and house clearances. You then clean, photograph and ship the items. Only items at least 20 years old qualify for Etsy's vintage category.
- **Buyer & channel:** Collectors and interior buyers in the US, UK, DE and NL. The main channel is Etsy (vintage category), with eBay.de and eBay.com as a second channel.
- **Price (€):** €25–120 per item. **Estimate:** the typical range of the Sklo Union and Czech-vase listings I found; I could not fetch the actual prices.
- **Unit economics (estimate):**
  - Buy-in: €3–15 per item at CZ flea markets or on bazos. This is background knowledge, UNVERIFIED.
  - Average sale: €45. The buyer pays shipping separately.
  - Etsy fees: about 6.5% transaction fee, plus about 4% + €0.30 payment processing, plus a $0.20 listing fee. That comes to roughly €5.
  - Packaging for fragile items: €2–3.
  - Occasional breakage or returns: about 5%, so roughly €2 per item.
  - **Net: about €22–30 per item before the founder's time.**
  - Time per item is about 35–45 minutes: sourcing share, cleaning, photos, listing and packing. That works out to roughly €30–40 per hour.
- **Demand evidence:**
  - The Czech-based Etsy shop **VintageRetroEu** ships from CZ, sells Sklo Union among other things, and has **1,290 reviews at 5.0★**. Reviewers praise the "most BEAUTIFUL glass pieces" and the careful packaging. ([pinterest mirror of the shop](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/vintageretroeu-etsy--354940014393416154/), [etsy sklo_union market](https://www.etsy.com/market/sklo_union))
    - Etsy review rates usually sit around 10–30% of orders. **Estimate:** that implies roughly 4,000–13,000 lifetime sales.
  - **BohemianStarsltd** has **1,047 reviews**. It sells new and vintage Czech/Czechoslovak glass ornaments and jewelry. ([etsy shop](https://www.etsy.com/shop/BohemianStarsltd))
  - Etsy runs dedicated market pages for these niches: [Czech Sklo Union](https://www.etsy.com/market/czech_sklo_union), [Sklo Union glass](https://www.etsy.com/market/sklo_union_glass), [vintage Czech glass](https://www.etsy.com/market/vintage_czech_glass) and [vintage Czech blown glass ornaments](https://www.etsy.com/market/vintage_czech_blown_glass_ornaments).
  - Named-designer items get individual listings, for example a [Pavel Pánek Sklo Union set](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1798876776/sklo-union-pavel-panek-vintage-bohemian) and a [Vladislav Urban Rosice vase](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1772053429/vintage-vase-glas-sklo-union-bohemia).
- **Supply gap / asymmetry evidence:**
  - Vintage supply is finite and physically located in CZ. Large Czech flea markets are one source: U Elektry in Prague is described as 50,000 m² and open on weekends, with Bohemian glass and porcelain ([fleamapket](https://www.fleamapket.com/listing-region/czech-republic/), [culturetrip](https://theculturetrip.com/europe/czech-republic/articles/the-best-flea-markets-in-prague)).
  - The asymmetry is sourcing price against collector price. **UNVERIFIED:** I could not pull bazos or aukro prices to compare with Etsy sold prices.
- **Edge for a CZ-based solo founder:** Physical access to the source (weekend markets, bazos pickup, house clearances) at Czech price levels. Reading Czech listings lets you find underpriced lots that foreign pickers cannot. EU buyers pay no customs.
- **Startup cost (€) & hours/week:** €500–1,500 for the first 60–100 items, packaging and a light tent. Plan on 10–12 hours a week: one sourcing trip and two photo/listing evenings.
- **Realistic monthly profit after 12 months (estimate):**
  - Pessimistic: €150.
  - Realistic: €500–700, from about 20–25 sales a month × €25 net.
  - Optimistic: €1,500, from about 50 sales a month plus a few high-value designer pieces.
- **CZ/EU legal/regulatory notes:**
  - A volná živnost for retail is enough as a side activity (vedlejší činnost).
  - Second-hand sales to consumers carry the 14-day withdrawal right in the EU.
  - GPSR broadly covers second-hand consumer products but excludes antiques. Keep the listings factual.
  - The CZ VAT registration threshold was CZK 2M in 2025 (UNVERIFIED for 2026). EU distance sales above €10k go to OSS or destination VAT, and the margin scheme is possible for second-hand goods (verify with an accountant).
  - **US risk (background knowledge, UNVERIFIED in this run):** the US ended the de-minimis duty exemption for all countries in Aug 2025. US buyers may now face duties or brokerage fees on postal parcels, so prioritise EU and UK buyers.
- **Key risks:** Breakage in transit. Sourcing competition from other Czech pickers, which is already visible in shops like the two above. Time per item does not scale. Seasonality in the ornament stock.
- **Confidence: 3/5.** There is real, verified evidence that a Czech-based shop has sold at scale (1,290 reviews). The margins depend on price data I could not fetch.
- **Verify next:**
  1. Etsy sold/review data for VintageRetroEu and 3 other CZ vintage shops.
  2. eBay.com and eBay.de *sold* prices for "Sklo Union", "Rosice vase" and "Czech blown glass ornament vintage".
  3. bazos.cz and aukro.cz asking prices for the same items.
  4. Whether Czech Post or Packeta currently ships to the US, and the DDP options.

### 2. Personalised hand-lettered Czech mouth-blown glass baubles (name/date/"first Christmas")
- **What you sell / type:** Physical, hybrid of sourcing and light handwork. You buy plain or semi-decorated **mouth-blown Czech glass baubles** wholesale from a Liberec/Jablonec-area workshop, then hand-letter the buyer's name, date or short text on each (5–10 minutes with enamel or paint pen). Each ships in a branded box with a "mouth-blown in Bohemia" card.
- **Buyer & channel:** Gift buyers in DE, AT, NL, UK and US. Channels are Etsy personalised-ornament search, plus a German-language Etsy/own-shop listing ("Personalisierte Weihnachtskugel mundgeblasen Tschechien").
- **Price (€):** €24–39 per ornament and €60–90 for sets of 3. **Estimate:** typical personalised-ornament Etsy pricing, not fetched.
- **Unit economics (estimate):**
  - Blank mouth-blown bauble: about €2–6 wholesale (UNVERIFIED; to be quoted by the workshop).
  - Paint and pen: €0.30. Gift box and padding: €1.50.
  - Etsy fees: about 11% plus €0.30 plus listing, so about €3.50 on €29. Offsite ads, when triggered, take a further 15%.
  - Shipping is charged to the buyer.
  - **Net: about €18–22 per ornament** for about 15 minutes of work including packing.
- **Demand evidence:**
  - Czech glass ornaments sell on Etsy through several shops. **BohemianStarsltd (1,047 reviews)** is one ([shop](https://www.etsy.com/shop/BohemianStarsltd)).
  - The manufacturer **Glassor** (Liberec) sells direct as [DecorbyGlassorCzech](https://www.etsy.com/shop/DecorbyGlassorCzech).
  - Several shops resell **Astralex** ornaments, blown from renovated 1930s clay moulds ([listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1295808257/czech-republic-glass-blown-hand-painted), [listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1535910727/czech-republic-glass-blown-hand-painted)).
  - Etsy has a [Czech glass Christmas ornaments market page](https://www.etsy.com/market/czech_glass_christmas_ornaments).
  - The Czech bead ornaments from Poniklá (Rautis) were inscribed on the UNESCO intangible heritage list in 2020, which gives the "Czech Christmas glass" story free PR ([radio.cz](https://english.radio.cz/unesco-adds-czech-glass-blown-christmas-beads-intangible-cultural-heritage-list-8703365), [VisitCzechia 2025](https://www.visitczechia.com/en-us/news/2025/12/n-do-you-know-traditional-czech-christmas-ornament)).
  - **UNVERIFIED:** the actual sales volume of *personalised* Czech glass ornaments.
- **Supply gap / asymmetry evidence:**
  - The manufacturers sell finished, generic designs. Personalisation per order is a labour step that factories do not want, and it fits the brief's "light manual work".
  - **UNVERIFIED:** whether personalised *Czech mouth-blown* baubles are thin on Etsy. The personalised-ornament category overall is huge and crowded with cheap machine-made or plastic baubles, so "mouth-blown in Bohemia" is the differentiator.
- **Edge for a CZ-based solo founder:** You can drive to the workshops to buy seconds or blanks at factory prices and inspect quality. The "made in Bohemia" origin is genuine. You can ship within the EU with no customs.
- **Startup cost (€) & hours/week:** €800–1,500 for 300–500 blanks, paints, boxes and a photo setup. The work is highly seasonal: 3–5 hours a week in Jan–Aug, then 20+ hours a week in Oct–Dec, which strains the 15-hour cap.
- **Realistic monthly profit after 12 months (estimate):** Pessimistic €50, realistic €300, optimistic €900 as a *12-month average*. About 70–80% of the profit lands in Oct–Dec.
- **CZ/EU legal/regulatory notes:**
  - Etsy requires disclosing the blank maker as a **production partner**. Personalisation by the seller fits Etsy's "made by" rules, but reselling unpersonalised factory ornaments on Etsy would breach the handmade policy.
  - GPSR requires manufacturer, importer and contact info plus a warning that glass is breakable and not a toy.
  - Paints must be non-toxic.
- **Key risks:**
  - Seasonality and the time crunch in December.
  - Breakage.
  - The workshop may refuse to sell blanks, or may sell personalised pieces itself.
  - The manufacturer's own Etsy presence (Glassor) competes directly.
  - US tariffs and loss of de-minimis (UNVERIFIED).
- **Confidence: 2/5.** The category is proven. The personalisation gap and the blank pricing are not verified.
- **Verify next:**
  1. Written price quotes for blanks from 3 workshops (Glassor, Irisa/Vánoční ozdoby Dvůr Králové, Astralex).
  2. Etsy search counts and reviews for "personalized glass ornament mouth blown", and whether any top-100 listings are Czech.
  3. Etsy DE demand for "Weihnachtskugel personalisiert Glas".

### 4. Authenticated Czech moldavite (vltavín), sourced with documented origin
- **What you sell / type:** Physical resale with light work. Raw moldavite pieces graded by weight and shape, each with photo-documented origin: a purchase from a licensed Czech source, the locality, and a UV and inclusion (bubbles/lechatelierite) photo. You add simple wire-wrap or silver-bail pendants. The niche is "Genuine moldavite from Czech Republic" for crystal and collector buyers.
- **Buyer & channel:** US/UK/DE crystal community via Etsy, plus an own shop and Instagram/TikTok.
- **Price (€):** UNVERIFIED. Per-gram prices must be checked, because the 2021 TikTok boom inflated them and they may since have collapsed.
- **Unit economics:** UNVERIFIED. The model is a gram-price spread between Czech buy-in from licensed dealers and Etsy retail, minus about 11% Etsy fees, €5–10 tracked shipping, and authenticity documentation costs.
- **Demand evidence:** **Not verified in this run.** Search budget ran out before any moldavite query executed. Background knowledge (UNVERIFIED): moldavite became a mass-market crystal-community item in 2020–2022, fakes (Chinese green glass) are widespread, and buyers actively seek "Czech-sourced" sellers.
- **Supply gap / asymmetry evidence:** Structural, UNVERIFIED. Moldavite comes essentially only from South Bohemia and Moravia, deposits are finite, and authenticity is the scarce good. A Czech seller with verifiable sourcing has a trust edge that no foreign seller can copy.
- **Edge for a CZ-based solo founder:** Location (only CZ produces it), language (Czech dealer networks and localities) and authenticity credibility.
- **Startup cost (€) & hours/week:** €1,000–3,000 of inventory. About 6–8 hours a week.
- **Realistic monthly profit after 12 months:** Not estimable without price data. Placeholder range: €0–1,000.
- **CZ/EU legal/regulatory notes:**
  - Collecting in fields needs the landowner's permission, and the mining-law status of moldavite must be checked (UNVERIFIED).
  - **Buy only from licensed sources with invoices.**
  - Avoid making health claims (EU consumer law and Etsy policy).
- **Key risks:**
  - A decaying fad after 2022 and a possible price collapse, which is **the key question**.
  - Many Czech sellers are likely already on Etsy.
  - Counterfeit-accusation disputes.
- **Confidence: 2/5.** The Czech-only structural edge is excellent, but demand and prices are completely unverified and could be post-hype.
- **Verify next:**
  1. Etsy/eBay *sold* prices per gram (2025–2026) against 2021–22.
  2. Czech dealer buy-in prices (vltavin.cz-type shops, bazos).
  3. The number of CZ-based moldavite Etsy shops and their review counts.

## Rejected
- **Balcony solar kits or mounting brackets (CZ and DE).**
  - In **CZ**, plugging in is still legally messy: licence-free status still requires a DSO notification, ČEZ demands a revision report with measured impedance plus an approved inverter since 1.1.2025, and only EG.D has a simplified ≤800 W category ([mypower forum](https://forum.mypower.cz/viewtopic.php?t=15058), [mikrofve.cz](https://mikrofve.cz/blogs/novinky/balkonova-fotovoltaika-a-distributori-v-cr)).
  - The simplifying amendment (800 W inverter, panels up to 2 kWp, e-notification only) is still going through the Sněmovna ([energozrouti](https://energozrouti.cz/clanek/nova-pravidla-pro-balkonovou-fotovoltaiku), [tiscali](https://www.tiscali.cz/panel-z-obchodaku-zasuvka-na-balkone-a-vlastni-elektrina-cesko-chce-zrusit-byrokracii-ktera-brzdi-tisice-domacnosti-703450)).
  - Even if it passes, the product is already commoditised. Lidl sells an 800 W EcoFlow kit ([arecenze](https://www.arecenze.cz/clanky/balkonova-fotovoltaika-z-lidlu-2026-cesi-si-mysleli-ze-ji-proste-zapoji-do-zasuvky-narazili-na-zakon-novela-to-ma-zmenit/)), kits run CZK 14,900–25,000 with CZK 5,000 for 400 W, and railing brackets are sold by many CZ e-shops and Kaufland.cz ([eshopelektronika](https://www.eshopelektronika.cz/sada-pro-kotveni-panelu-na-balkonove-zabradli), [e-altamira](https://e-altamira.cz/Fotovoltaicke-Balkonove-Drzaky), [abctech](https://www.abctech.cz/drzak-pro-solarni-panely-na-balkon-a-zed-cerny-nastavitelny-uhel-10-30-_d40118.html), [kaufland.cz](https://www.kaufland.cz/product/448923733/)).
  - In **DE** the market is huge (≈430k new units in 2025; 1.33M registered by May 2026), but prices have fallen through mass production ([gfk-solar](https://www.gfk-solar.de/solaranlagen-photovoltaik-berlin-brandenburg/balkonkraftwerk-karte-deutschland-2026/), [strom-report](https://strom-report.com/balkonkraftwerk/)).
  - There is no edge for a solo founder, and the margins belong to volume distributors.
- **Smart-thermostat / TRV valve adapters (3D-printed, including the CZ building-energy angle).**
  - Free STL files exist for the common conversions, such as Danfoss RA/RAV to M30×1.5 ([stlfinder](https://www.stlfinder.com/3dmodels/danfoss-rav-adapter/), [3dmdb](https://3dmdb.com/en/3d-model/danfoss-ra-type-valve-to-m30x15-thermostats-adapter-by-antonveretenenko/8868655/)).
  - Branded adapter packs such as Honeywell EVA1 are cheap and sold on eBay.de ([ebay.de](https://www.ebay.de/p/1645623104), [resideo](https://www.resideo.com/emea/en/products/comfort/radiator-valves-and-thermostats/radiator-valve-and-thermostat-accessories/eva1-danfoss-valve-adapter-danfoss-rav-ravl-ra-eva1-danfoss/)).
  - This is a low-price commodity with no supply gap. Czech heat-metering rules create demand among B2B building managers, not consumers.
- **Czech glass beads (Jablonec seed or fire-polished) resale.**
  - The field is saturated by Czech-based wholesalers already selling direct online and on Etsy, such as czechandglassbeads with hundreds of SKUs per bead type ([etsy shop](https://www.etsy.com/shop/czechandglassbeads)).
  - Other examples are [czechbeads.cz](https://www.czechbeads.cz/), [beadsczech.com](https://www.beadsczech.com/) and [czech-glassbeads.com](https://www.czech-glassbeads.com/). Reviews emphasise "so many beads for so little", which signals thin margins.
  - A Czech location is no edge when the manufacturers sell direct.
- **New Bohemian crystal (cut stemware/vases) resale.**
  - There is no supply gap: manufacturers export directly and the product is not scarce.
  - It is fragile and heavy, which raises shipping, breakage and 14-day-return costs.
  - Etsy does not allow reselling new factory goods as handmade, so the channel would be eBay or Amazon against the factories themselves. This is reasoning; no price data was fetched.
- **Compliance products for EU rules (GPSR, EPR, EUDR, Ecodesign/DPP, e-invoicing).**
