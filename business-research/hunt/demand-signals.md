### 1. Flash-modded iPod Classic / iPod Video, built from broken CZ donors ("dead-HDD rescue")
- **What you sell / type (physical):** A refurbished Apple iPod Classic (6th/7th gen) or iPod Video (5th/5.5th gen). The failed hard drive is swapped for an SD/microSD flash adapter (128–512 GB card), and the unit gets a new 2000–3000 mAh battery, an optional new face/back plate, and is reset and tested. It is sold empty: no preloaded music, for copyright reasons.
- **Buyer & channel:** Gen Z and 25–40 nostalgia/"digital detox" buyers in DE, AT, FR, NL, IT and CZ. Channels: Vinted (no seller fee), eBay.de, and Etsy with shipping restricted to the EU and UK at first (see the US caveat under risks).
- **Price (€):** €190–260 for a standard build and €280–350 for premium builds (colour shell, 3000 mAh, 512 GB). Reference points:
  - PlayerMods custom builds "from $259" (≈€220). https://player-mods.com/
  - An Etsy 256 GB / 3000 mAh 7th-gen listing at $358.33 (≈€305), per search snippet. https://www.etsy.com/listing/1673597987/apple-ipod-classic-7th6th-gen-grey
  - The low end: an eBay.de modded unit at ≈$88.75. https://www.ebay.de/itm/205538637277
- **Unit economics (cost, fees, net per unit). All figures are estimates. Parts prices come from general knowledge of AliExpress/iFlash-type pricing and were not checked this session.**
  - Donor: €4–19 list price for "nefunkční/na díly" units. Averaged to €25 per sellable unit to cover duds, assuming about 30% of donors are unrecoverable (dead logic board rather than dead HDD).
  - Parts: €70–100 in total. SD adapter €15–30, SD card €20–30, battery €12–20, shell €10–25, thermal/adhesive/clickwheel sundries €5.
  - Packaging: €2.
  - Marketplace fees on a €230 sale:
    - Etsy: 6.5% transaction fee plus about 4% payment fee plus listing fee, roughly €25. Occasional 12–15% offsite-ads fee on top.
    - eBay.de: roughly 11–13%.
    - Vinted: €0 to the seller, because the buyer pays the fee.
  - Shipping to DE via Packeta/post: €6–9, mostly charged to the buyer.
  - Reserve for 14-day withdrawals and warranty claims: about €10 per unit.
  - **Net ≈ €80 per unit at a €230 sale price (estimate)**, or about €100 when sold on Vinted.
  - Labour: about 1.5–2 h per unit (disassemble, swap, restore the firmware, test for 1 h, photograph, list, pack). That fits the brief's limit of 1–2 h refurb per unit.
- **Demand evidence (numbers + URLs):**
  - Searches for iPod Classic rose 25% and for iPod Nano 20% (Jan–Oct 2025 vs the same period in 2024). Refurbished iPod sales have grown an average of 15.6% per year since 2022. https://www.rd.com/article/ipods-back-in-style/
  - Mainstream coverage of a Gen Z iPod revival in 2025–26:
    - Morning Brew, 2026-02-24: https://www.morningbrew.com/stories/2026/02/24/gen-z-is-putting-down-phones-and-picking-up-ipods
    - https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-gen-z-ipod-revival/
    - https://www.engadget.com/2196894/ipods-comeback-gen-z/
  - The snippet also cites an AP report of a "steady climb in resale demand".
  - Active Etsy category pages exist for this product: https://www.etsy.com/market/ipod_classic_mod, https://www.etsy.com/market/ipod_classic_modded_usb_c
- **Supply gap / asymmetry evidence (numbers + URLs):**
  - A modded-iPod eBay seller's listing text (surfaced by search; I could not open it to confirm which listing, likely https://www.ebay.com/itm/206475315583 or https://www.ebay.com/itm/167946363107) says: "as of September 2025, there is currently a persistent queue of orders… orders made in December [cannot be] guaranteed delivery by Christmas". That is a builder who cannot keep up.
  - Several PlayerMods variants show "sold out or unavailable": https://player-mods.com/products/black-ipod-classic-7th-gen-upgraded-sdxc-personalised-media-player
  - **The asymmetry is in donors.** Broken iPod Classics on Aukro list at **99–469 Kč (€4–19)**, including a 5th-gen 80 GB "na díly" at 99 Kč buy-now, per search snippet. These may be auction start prices, not final prices. https://aukro.cz/apple-ipod-classic-5-generace-80gb-7092200617, https://aukro.cz/lp/apple-ipod
  - Working units on Bazoš ask **3,500–7,490 Kč (€140–300)**. https://elektro.bazos.cz/inzeraty/ipod-classic/
  - The most common failure, a dead HDD, is exactly the part the mod replaces. So a "broken" unit is worth roughly €200 more once modded.
- **Edge for a CZ-based solo founder:**
  - Cheap local donor supply (Aukro, Bazoš, Vinted CZ), in Czech, with personal pickup.
  - Packeta gives cheap EU shipping.
  - Selling from the EU avoids the customs and returns friction that UK and US builders face for EU buyers.
  - The skill is learnable in weeks, and most flash-adapter swaps need no soldering. USB-C and Bluetooth mods do.
- **Startup cost (€) & hours/week:**
  - About €1,300 (estimate): tools €60, 15 donors ≈ €300, parts for 10 units ≈ €850, light box and props €40, test cards €50.
  - Under €2,000 at risk before traction.
  - 10–15 h/week gives about 8–12 units/month.
- **Realistic monthly profit after 12 months (estimates):**
  - Pessimistic: €250 (5 units × €50 after price pressure).
  - Realistic: €800 (10 units × €80).
  - Optimistic: €1,800 (18 units × €100, premium builds, Vinted-heavy).
- **CZ/EU legal/regulatory notes:**
  - Trade licence: volná živnost (retail plus repair of consumer electronics; verify the exact obor) as vedlejší činnost. Check the §304 ZP non-compete clause in the employment contract.
  - GPSR (EU 2023/988) covers second-hand and refurbished consumer products. A refurbisher who substantially modifies a product can be treated as its manufacturer: give contact details and safety info.
  - Fitting new Li-ion cells may make the founder a battery "producer" under the Battery Regulation (EU) 2023/1542. That means joining a CZ take-back scheme (e.g., ECOBAT). Verify.
  - Check whether selling refurbished EEE cross-border triggers WEEE/ElektroG registration in DE. The rules mostly target new EEE; verify.
  - B2C warranty is 24 months. The Czech civil code allows shortening it to 12 months for used goods by agreement; verify the wording. The 14-day withdrawal right applies.
  - Apple trademark: reselling genuine goods is fine, but no Apple logo in branding, and aftermarket parts must be disclosed.
  - Li-ion batteries inside equipment (UN3481) can be shipped under carrier rules.
- **Key risks:**
  - Donor supply is finite and prices rise as the trend spreads.
  - Competition keeps growing (Etsy is full of "ipod classic mod" sellers).
  - Cheap SD cards are sometimes counterfeit. Clone adapters vary in quality.
  - Donor yield is uncertain.
  - Fad risk.
  - Selling to the US is risky. It is my understanding, not verified this session, that the US ended de minimis for all countries in Aug 2025 and that some EU posts paused US parcels. Stay EU-first.
- **Confidence: 3/5.**
  - For: demand is multi-sourced and current (2025–26), and the donor price asymmetry is concrete.
  - Against: the supply-gap evidence is one queue statement plus sold-out variants. I have no eBay sold-volume data (fetch blocked). Parts costs and yields are estimates.

### 2. New-format (from 11 April 2026) Czech A2 permanent-residence exam prep pack, in UA/RU/EN/VI
- **What you sell / type (digital):**
  - A self-study pack (PDF plus MP3, or a small login-protected web app) for the **new** A2 exam format that took effect 11 April 2026.
  - Contents: 6–10 original full mock tests with native-speaker audio, answer keys, model written answers and speaking prompts with sample answers, and a topic vocabulary list.
  - Instructions and explanations in Ukrainian, Russian, English and Vietnamese.
  - The content is original, never copied from official tests.
- **Buyer & channel:**
  - Buyers: foreigners in CZ applying for permanent residence.
  - Channels: Facebook groups and Telegram channels of the Ukrainian, Vietnamese and expat communities, Google Ads and SEO on "zkouška A2 trvalý pobyt" / "Czech A2 exam", and affiliate deals with language schools and visa agencies.
  - Sold through your own site with Stripe/GoPay, or Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record.
- **Price (€):**
  - 690 Kč (≈€28) for the pack.
  - 990 Kč (≈€40) bundled with candidate 3.
  - Price anchors (search snippets):
    - UJOP individual consultation: 1,580 Kč per 90 min. https://ujop.cuni.cz/UJOPEN-74.html?ujopcmsid=68:individual-preparation-for-the-exam-for-permanent-residence-applicants
    - ICJ course for the new 2026 format: 5,400 Kč per 40 h. https://icj.cz/en/preparation-for-the-a2-exam-for-permanent-residence-new-format-2026/
- **Unit economics (estimates):**
  - Payment fees: about 20 Kč via Stripe/GoPay, or 10% + $0.50 via Gumroad (which handles VAT).
  - Paid acquisition: roughly 150–250 Kč per sale. Estimate: CPC 3–6 Kč at 2–3% conversion.
  - Net: **about 450–650 Kč (€18–26) per sale.**
  - The marginal cost of delivery is zero.
  - As a non-VAT payer (the CZ threshold is 2,000,000 Kč; verify), there is no VAT on domestic sales.
- **Demand evidence (numbers + URLs):**
  - The A2 exam is **mandatory** for permanent residence. Figures from the MŠMT press release, per search snippet: https://msmt.gov.cz/zajem-o-zkousku-z-cestiny-pro-trvaly-pobyt-roste-stat-jiz
    - **More than 12,000 people passed it in 2024.**
    - The Interior Ministry estimated **up to 30,000 permanent-residence applications** "this year" (2025).
    - About 2,500 passed in 2020.
    - The plan was to raise capacity by about 50% in 2025.
  - In 2023, 8,819 candidates sat the exam and 86.2% passed (search snippet from results including https://www.auccj.cz/zkouska-z-cestiny-pro-trvaly-pobyt-aktualni-podoba-a-moznosti-pripravy-uchazecu/).
  - Paying demand already exists: schools sell dedicated courses (ICJ 5,400 Kč; Solaris https://www.solarisschool.cz/group-language-courses/czech-a2-exam-course/; Language Atelier https://languageatelier.eu/language-courses/czech-exam-for-permanent-residence/; UJOP 1,580 Kč consultations).
- **Supply gap / asymmetry evidence (numbers + URLs):**
  - **Regulation-forced obsolescence.** The format changed on 11 April 2026, the first update in 5 years. Sources:
    - https://www.expats.cz/czech-news/article/czechia-s-permanent-residence-language-exam-getting-an-update-tomorrow
    - https://blog.foreigners.cz/changes-to-the-czech-a2-language-exam-in-2026/
    - https://edujoy.cz/en/about-us/blog/czech-language-a2-exam-for-permanent-residency-in-the-czech-republic-changes-from-april-2026-and-how-to-prepare/
  - The main official handbook is the **2022** NPI edition, which covers the old format: https://www.npi.cz/images/isbn/978-80-7578-176-5.pdf
  - **Waiting lists:**
    - About 5 months for A2, per search snippet.
    - The official registration page says that with more than 500 people ahead of you the wait "can take several weeks": https://registrace.cestina-pro-cizince.cz/en/
    - Candidates therefore have months of self-study time and a strong reason to pass on the first attempt.
  - **Counter-evidence (competition):**
    - https://a2zkouska.cz/en: online prep with a "Smart Review System"; price not seen.
    - https://czechready.cz/: **free** Reading and Listening practice plus AI-graded Writing and Speaking "in the official UJOP format".
    - The official site's preparation page may host new model tests: https://cestina-pro-cizince.cz/trvaly-pobyt/a2/en/priprava-na-zkousku/ (not verifiable, fetch blocked).
  - So the gap is **native-language (UA/VI) explanation plus volume of new-format mock tests**, not the absence of any material.
- **Edge for a CZ-based solo founder:**
  - Native Czech for writing items and recording audio.
  - Local knowledge of the exam and offices.
  - The ability to reach communities directly.
  - A regulation-driven, recurring buyer flow of thousands per year, with no inventory.
- **Startup cost (€) & hours/week:** estimates.
  - About €1,000–1,500: translations of instructions and explanations (~8k words) €300–600, microphone €80, site €100–200, ad test €500.
  - 80–120 h one-off build, about 8 weeks at 10–15 h/week. Then 3–5 h/week.
- **Realistic monthly profit after 12 months (estimates):**
  - Market reasoning: about 20,000 exam takers a year is roughly 1,700 a month.
  - Pessimistic: €200 (10 sales).
  - Realistic: €700 (about 35 sales × €20, a 2% capture).
  - Optimistic: €2,000 or more (about 90 sales plus school affiliate bundles).
- **CZ/EU legal/regulatory notes:**
  - Trade licence: volná živnost (publishing / educational materials).
  - Use original content only. Do not call it "official" or use UJOP or MŠMT marks.
  - The 14-day withdrawal right for digital content can be waived only with the buyer's explicit consent to immediate delivery (§1837 l OZ).
  - GDPR applies to the email list.
  - OSS VAT applies if cross-border B2C digital sales in the EU exceed €10,000 a year.
- **Key risks:**
  - Free or AI competitors (czechready) cap willingness to pay.
  - The ministry may publish plenty of free new-format model tests.
  - Piracy of PDFs; mitigate with a web app.
  - Policy change under a new government.
  - Marketing in UA/VI needs trusted translators.
- **Confidence: 3/5.**
  - For: government numbers and a regulatory format change prove the buyer volume and the timing.
  - Against: willingness to pay for self-study against free alternatives is unproven, and I could not open competitors' prices.
