### Cross-cutting findings on AI saturation (2023–2026)
- Etsy sellers are leaving because AI-generated listings are flooding the site. Reported examples: an AI pet-portrait competitor cut one seller's sales by 30% and then 50%; an illustrator's sales fell 98% in a year; a knitwear designer's pattern sales fell at least 50% in six months. https://www.digitaltrends.com/computing/etsy-sellers-are-quitting-as-ai-slop-floods-the-handmade-marketplace/
- Etsy GMS is projected at about $11.2B for 2025, down from $12.5B in 2024. Source is a low-quality aggregator: https://www.accio.com/business/etsy-digital-downloads-sales-statistics-trend-2025
- AI-generated fake crochet patterns: NBC found more than a dozen Reddit threads with hundreds of comments since May 2023. https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/etsy-crochet-buyers-suspect-ai-made-images-used-sell-patterns-rcna145878 , https://tlycblog.com/how-to-spot-fake-crochet-patterns/
- AI-made material on TPT, the US teacher marketplace (Chalkbeat, 3 Aug 2026): https://www.chalkbeat.org/2026/08/03/ai-slop-on-teachers-curriculum-marketplace/
- **Takeaway:** the categories that still hold up are those where a file must *physically work* (a 3D print that fits a real object, modular terrain that prints without supports) and those in a *small language* where US AI sellers don't compete (Czech). Purely visual or English categories (wall art, SVG, Canva, Notion, planners, presets) and "looks-right" categories (crochet/knit patterns) have been hit hardest.

### 1. EU power-tool and storage-system mounts, organisers and adapters (Parkside X20V / Einhell Power X-Change / Eurobox / Systainer / L-Boxx), monetised on MakerWorld Exclusive and Printables Store/Club
- **What you sell / type (physical|digital|hybrid):** Digital. Parametric, functional 3D-print files: wall and pegboard mounts for Lidl **Parkside X20V** tools and batteries (sold only in Europe), Einhell PXC batteries, Gridfinity/IKEA-SKÅDIS/Multiboard bins sized to **Eurobox 600×400** containers, and inserts for Systainer and L-Boxx cases. The free base designs go on MakerWorld as "Exclusive" (the platform pays per download, print and boost). Premium packs (full-set bundles, customiser versions) are paid on **Printables Store**. A **Printables Club** tier at €3–5 per month gives early access and requests. No electrical battery-to-battery adapters, for liability reasons.
- **Buyer & channel:** EU hobbyists with a Bambu or Prusa printer. MakerWorld (Bambu Lab) for reach and platform rewards. Printables.com, run by Prague-based Prusa Research, for Store and Club.
- **Price (€):** MakerWorld is free to the downloader and paid by the platform. Printables Store models at €2–6 each. Club at €3–5 per month.
- **Unit economics (cost, fees, net per unit):**
  - MakerWorld: only "Exclusive" points can be cashed out, at **$0.066 per point** with a $100 minimum; regular points buy gift cards and filament only (https://bambuhub.net/guides/makerworld-points-rewards).
  - A forum rule of thumb puts 1 boost at about $1, 40 downloads at about $1 and 20 prints ("makes") at about $1, with gift cards from Exclusive models worth 25% more. That is roughly €0.02 per download, €0.04 per print and €0.85 per boost (https://forum.bambulab.com/t/how-much-money-do-you-earn-on-makerworld/119890, via search summary).
  - BOM commission is 3–15% of kit or parts orders placed from a model page (https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/makerworld/tutorials/creator-commission-incentives).
  - Printables: 10% platform fee plus payment processing; a $10 club tier nets the creator $8.41, about €7.15 (https://3dprintingindustry.com/news/prusa-research-launches-paid-3d-printable-model-marketplace-227446/ , https://all3dp.com/4/prusa-research-launches-printables-clubs-subscription-service/).
  - Marginal cost per sale is €0. Prototype filament is about €1–2 per design (**estimate:** 50–100 g of PLA at about €20/kg).
- **Demand evidence (numbers + URLs):**
  - Per platform statistics as of 1 March 2026, some MakerWorld creators earn over $1,000/month; a company update mentions creators close to $20,000/month through BOM (https://sparx-worx.com/makerworld-creator-economy/ , https://stacksheriff.com/3d-printing/makerworld-guide/).
  - Printables Store (launched mid-December 2023) and Clubs (August 2023) are live, paid channels. Prusa doubled creator earnings up to $10,000 at the Store launch (https://blog.prusa3d.com/printables-store_87810/ , https://blog.prusa3d.com/printables-clubs-are-live-you-can-now-support-the-creators-you-love-on-printables_81063/).
  - Money is changing hands, but paid by the platforms, not by buyers.
- **Supply gap / asymmetry evidence (numbers + URLs):** Not measured this session (MakerWorld and Printables search pages were blocked). The asymmetry is a hypothesis. Parkside (Lidl), Einhell, Eurobox and Systainer/L-Boxx are European ecosystems, so the large US designer base that dominates MakerWorld has no physical access to them, and a mount that must fit a real battery cannot be AI-generated without holding the object. **Verify:** count MakerWorld/Printables results for "Parkside X20V", "Einhell PXC" and "Eurobox" against "Milwaukee M18" and "DeWalt 20V", and record downloads of the top 10.
- **Edge for a CZ-based solo founder:** Parkside tools and batteries are cheap in every Czech Lidl to buy and measure. Prusa and Printables are headquartered in Prague (community events, Czech-language support). Functional CAD (Fusion 360 personal / Onshape free) is learnable in weeks, unlike organic sculpting.
- **Startup cost (€) & hours/week:** **Estimate:** printer €600–1,200 (Bambu P1S/X1C or Prusa Core One class) [prior knowledge, not re-verified], calipers €20, filament €100, reference tools and batteries €150. Total **€900–1,500**. 10–12 h/week gives about 1.5 designs per week.
- **Realistic monthly profit after 12 months (pessimistic / realistic / optimistic, €):** **Estimate: €30 / €200–350 / €1,000–1,500.** Reasoning: about 75 published models after 50 weeks. At €2–5 per model per month in points, boosts and store sales, that gives the realistic range. The optimistic case needs 1–3 hit systems and is anchored to the ">$1,000/month" creators reported for March 2026.
- **CZ/EU legal/regulatory notes:**
  - Licensing income from copyrighted designs may fall under §7(2) of the Czech income tax act (ZDP), with a 40% flat-rate expense allowance [prior knowledge, not re-verified; confirm with a tax adviser]. Otherwise a free trade licence (volná živnost) as a side activity (vedlejší činnost).
  - Platform fees paid to foreign platforms can make the founder an **"identifikovaná osoba"** for VAT (reverse charge) even as a non-payer [prior knowledge, not re-verified].
  - Platforms selling e-services usually act as the deemed VAT supplier (Art. 9a of Implementing Regulation 282/2011); verify per platform.
  - Use "compatible with Parkside" wording (nominative use) and no logos.
- **Key risks:**
  - The platform changes its rules. MakerWorld has already reallocated points and "upgraded" its points system (https://makerworld.com/en/community/post/458727 , https://forum.bambulab.com/t/reallocation-of-makerworlds-points-reward-system/70756?page=8).
  - Regular points have no cash value.
  - Copycats remix popular designs.
  - Income is power-law distributed.
  - Lidl ranges change.
- **Confidence (1–5) and why:** **3.** Platform payouts are real and documented, the founder has a physical-access edge, and startup cost is low. The supply gap in EU ecosystems is unmeasured and income is platform-dependent.

### 2. Czech-language pre-school and first-grade printable packs (předškolák / grafomotorika / Montessori three-part cards, "příprava na zápis") for parents, homeschoolers and nursery-school (MŠ) teachers
- **What you sell / type (physical|digital|hybrid):** Digital PDF packs: themed 20–40-page workbooks (fine-motor drills, halves matching, first letters and numbers, seasonal themes) and Montessori three-part card sets in Czech. MŠ teachers also get weekly theme blocks aligned with RVP PV, the national pre-school curriculum.
- **Buyer & channel:** Czech and Slovak parents of 4–7-year-olds (the Slovak market is reachable in Czech), homeschooling families and MŠ teachers. Channels are an own shop (Shoptet or WooCommerce with automatic PDF e-mail), Instagram and Facebook parent groups, and the marketplaces **UčiteléUčitelům.cz** (serves MŠ, ZŠ and SŠ teachers, speech therapists, parents and homeschoolers), **Učitelnice.cz** and **eduki.com/cz**.
- **Price (€):** **Estimate:** CZK 99–249 (€4–10) per pack; CZK 390–590 (€16–24) bundles. Czech competitor prices were not visible (fetch blocked).
- **Unit economics (cost, fees, net per unit):**
  - **Estimate** for an own shop: gateway about 1.5–2.5%, platform €10–30/month fixed, marginal cost €0. Net about €3.8–9.5 per pack before fixed costs.
  - Marketplaces: eduki keeps 50% (60% share for the seller after 20 materials), with new per-type transaction fees since 1 July 2025 once a seller passes €1,000 in sales (https://www.carosclass.com/wie-viel-verdient-man-bei-eduki-wirklich-2026-ehrlich-erklart/ , https://www.betterteachingresources.com/post/10-fragen-zu-eduki-geld-verdienen-unterrichtsmaterialien).
- **Demand evidence (numbers + URLs):**
  - Paid Czech shops exist and sell exactly this. Mama do Reste runs dedicated "pracovní listy v PDF" and "Montessori pomůcky v PDF" categories, for example a 26-page Montessori halves workbook (https://eshop.mamadodeste.cz/pracovni-listy/ , https://eshop.mamadodeste.cz/produkt/pracovni-sesit-prirazovani-polovin-montessori-26-stran-pdf/). Učení s nápadem sells PDF teaching sets (https://www.ucenisnapadem.cz/on-line-vyukove-materialy). Paid marketplaces exist (https://uciteleucitelum.cz/ , https://www.ucitelnice.cz/ , https://eduki.com/cz).
  - Comparable model abroad: eduki (DE) has 2M+ teachers, and one seller reports at least €500/month with good months at €1,200–1,500 (search summaries of https://www.carosclass.com/wie-viel-verdient-man-bei-eduki-wirklich-2026-ehrlich-erklart/ and https://app.dealroom.co/companies/lehrermarkplatz).
  - No Czech sales counts were obtained.
- **Supply gap / asymmetry evidence (numbers + URLs):**
  - Language asymmetry: English Montessori and pre-school printables are abundant on Etsy and TPT, but Czech ones come from a handful of small shops (above). US AI sellers do not produce Czech.
  - Counter-evidence: a lot of free Czech supply (Asociace Montessori https://asociacemontessori.cz/ke-stazeni/ , MŠ websites https://www.msmerklin-kv.cz/ke-stazeni-pro-deti/ , https://www.zodpovednavyuka.cz/materialy-zdarma/ , https://www.edupraxe.cz/materialy , MonTeDu free samples https://montedu.cz/kategorie-produktu/pdf-zdarma/).
  - The gap is in polished, themed, print-ready sets, not in availability.
- **Edge for a CZ-based solo founder:** Native Czech; knows the zápis (school enrolment) calendar and the RVP; no foreign competition. AI helps with illustration and layout, but Czech pedagogy and wording stay a human moat.
- **Startup cost (€) & hours/week:** **Estimate:** €200–600 (Shoptet/Woo first year, fonts and illustration licences, a small ads test). 8–12 h/week to build about 30 packs in year 1.
- **Realistic monthly profit after 12 months (pessimistic / realistic / optimistic, €):** **Estimate: €20 / €150–350 / €800.** Reasoning: realistic is 40–70 sales/month at about €6 net, minus €30–60 fixed. Sales will peak around zápis (April) and September.
- **CZ/EU legal/regulatory notes:**
  - The 14-day withdrawal right can be waived for digital content if the buyer consents and acknowledges it before download (Directive 2011/83/EU Art. 16(m); Czech civil code §1837) [prior knowledge, not re-verified]. Build the checkbox into checkout.
  - Author income under §7; VAT threshold CZK 2M [prior knowledge, not re-verified].
  - Illustrations must be licensed for commercial resale.
- **Key risks:** Free substitutes cap prices. The market is small (CZ+SK). Willingness to pay is unproven beyond a few shops. Traffic depends on Facebook and Instagram.
- **Confidence (1–5) and why:** **2.** Paid Czech sellers exist and the language moat is real, but there are no sales counts and free supply is heavy. **Verify:** Mama do Reste product review counts, UčiteléUčitelům bestseller lists, and a CZK 99 test pack with a Facebook group post.

### 3. Central-European historical wargaming terrain STL club (Thirty Years' War / Napoleonic "Austerlitz" / 1866 / WWII Bohemia-Moravia) for 15 mm and 28 mm
- **What you sell / type (physical|digital|hybrid):** Digital, as a subscription. Each month a modular, support-free, hard-surface terrain set: Bohemian and Moravian village buildings (log cottages, Baroque chapels, farmsteads), bridges, field works and fortifications. Past months are sold à la carte, and a "merchant" tier lets print sellers resell the prints.
- **Buyer & channel:** Historical wargamers (Bolt Action, Pike & Shotte, Black Powder and similar) in the UK, DE, US and CZ. Channels are Patreon and Printables Club (up to 5 tiers; https://all3dp.com/4/prusa-research-launches-printables-clubs-subscription-service/), plus MyMiniFactory and Cults3D for one-off sets.
- **Price (€):** About $10/month is the norm for STL Patreons (https://miniset.net/patreon/rating, via search summary). Proposed: €6 personal tier, €15 merchant tier, and €10–20 for back-catalogue sets (**estimate**).
- **Unit economics (cost, fees, net per unit):**
  - On Printables, a $10 tier nets $8.41 (≈€7.15) after the 10% fee and payment processing (https://3dprintingindustry.com/news/prusa-research-launches-paid-3d-printable-model-marketplace-227446/).
  - Patreon fees for new creators have been about 10% plus payment processing since 2025 [prior knowledge, not re-verified].
  - Net ≈ 83–86% of price. Marginal cost €0. Test prints about €5–15 per set (**estimate**).
- **Demand evidence (numbers + URLs):** The STL-subscription model is proven, with hundreds of paid terrain and miniature Patreons indexed (https://miniset.net/patreon , https://makerfun3d.com/patreon-game-terrain-miniatures) and Graphtreon keeping a 3D-printing earnings ranking (https://graphtreon.com/top-patreon-earners/3d-printing; blocked, figures not read). The historical sub-niche was **not** measured.
- **Supply gap / asymmetry evidence (numbers + URLs):** Hypothesis only. The big STL clubs are fantasy and sci-fi (D&D/Warhammer-adjacent, per the indexes above), and Central-European-specific architecture for historical periods looks thin. **Verify:** count historical-terrain creators on miniset.net and Graphtreon, and search MyMiniFactory for "Thirty Years War terrain", "Napoleonic village 15mm" and "Czech/Bohemian".
- **Edge for a CZ-based solo founder:** On-site references: the Thirty Years' War began in Prague in 1618, Austerlitz (Slavkov) is in Moravia, and Königgrätz (Hradec Králové) was the 1866 battlefield. Open-air museums (e.g., Rožnov pod Radhoštěm) provide authentic vernacular architecture. Hard-surface modular terrain is CAD and Blender work, easier than organic figure sculpting, and harder for current AI 3D generators to do printably and at true scale.
- **Startup cost (€) & hours/week:** **Estimate:** €500–900 (a resin plus FDM test printer or a shared one with candidate 1, Blender free, render setup) and €100 for reference trips. 12–15 h/week for one set per month plus renders.
- **Realistic monthly profit after 12 months (pessimistic / realistic / optimistic, €):** **Estimate: €60 / €300–500 / €1,500.** Reasoning: 12 months of back catalogue. Realistic is 40–70 members at about €6 net plus €50–100 in store sales. Optimistic needs 200+ members plus merchant tiers.
- **CZ/EU legal/regulatory notes:** §7 income, or a free trade licence. Treat Patreon and Printables fees as foreign services for VAT identification (see candidate 1). Keep historical insignia off the models, because WWII German symbols are restricted in DE (StGB §86a) [prior knowledge, not re-verified].
- **Key risks:** A slow membership ramp (6–12 months of free marketing on Reddit and Facebook wargaming groups). Monthly deadlines conflict with a full-time job. Piracy of STLs. A historical niche is smaller than fantasy.
- **Confidence (1–5) and why:** **2.** The business model is clearly proven, but the specific niche's demand and gap are unmeasured and it needs sustained, skilled output.

## Rejected
- **Knitting and crochet patterns (Ravelry/Etsy), including Czech amigurumi patterns.** 72.3% of independent Ravelry designers earn ≤$50/month and about three-quarters earn ≤$600/year; $201+/month already puts a designer in the top ~10% (https://www.mediaperuana.com/blog1/designerincome). AI-generated fake patterns are eroding buyer trust (https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/etsy-crochet-buyers-suspect-ai-made-images-used-sell-patterns-rcna145878), and one knitwear designer's pattern sales fell at least 50% in six months (Digital Trends, above). The work is skill-heavy and pays little.
- **Czech PDF sewing patterns (střihy).** Already well served by at least 7 established Czech shops: Fazonetka, Šijeme hravě (200+ patterns in A4, A0 and paper), Tania Design, Šibabi, Dnes šiju, SHAPE-patterns and Šijte s námi (https://www.fazonetka.cz/ , https://www.sijemehrave.cz/ , https://www.taniadesign.cz/cs/digitalni-strihy , https://sibabi.cz/collections/pdf-digitalni-detske-strihy , https://www.dnessiju.cz/strihy/ , https://www.shape-patterns.cz/strihy-pdf/ , https://sijtesnami.cz/eshop-st/pdf-strihy/). It also requires pattern-drafting and grading skill. There is no gap.
- **US teaching resources on TPT.** Most sellers make $0–100/month and the top ~1% out-earn the bottom 99% (https://seolumina.com/blog/how-much-do-tpt-sellers-make-in-2026-real-data-income-breakdown). AI-made material is spreading on the platform (https://www.chalkbeat.org/2026/08/03/ai-slop-on-teachers-curriculum-marketplace/). It needs US-curriculum expertise, which the founder lacks.
- **Czech teacher marketplaces as a standalone business (UčiteléUčitelům / Učitelnice / eduki.cz for ZŠ/SŠ subjects).** UčiteléUčitelům itself describes author pay as a "symbolic" reward (https://uciteleucitelum.cz/faq, via search summary). There is heavy free, often publicly funded supply (https://www.edupraxe.cz/materialy , https://www.zodpovednavyuka.cz/materialy-zdarma/ , https://www.vesela-chaloupka.cz/ , https://vyukovematerialy.com/). eduki keeps 40–50% commission, and a non-teacher has no subject edge. These marketplaces are kept only as a secondary channel for candidate 2.
- **Generic Etsy digital products: printable wall art, SVG/Cricut cut files, Canva/Notion templates, digital planners, budget spreadsheets in English.** These are the categories hit hardest by the AI flood. Sellers report drops of 50–98% (https://www.digitaltrends.com/computing/etsy-sellers-are-quitting-as-ai-slop-floods-the-handmade-marketplace/), and Etsy GMS is projected to fall from $12.5B (2024) to about $11.2B (2025) (https://www.accio.com/business/etsy-digital-downloads-sales-statistics-trend-2025, low-quality source). A Czech founder has no edge here.
- **Generic fantasy/D&D miniatures Patreon.** Hundreds of competing STL Patreons at a standard ~$10/month (https://miniset.net/patreon/rating , https://makerfun3d.com/patreon-game-terrain-miniatures), earnings concentrated at the top (Graphtreon ranking, figures not read), and organic sculpting takes years to learn. AI 3D generation is also improving fastest on organic figures [prior knowledge, not re-verified]. Candidate 3 keeps the proven subscription model but moves to hard-surface historical terrain.
- **Machine embroidery files (Stickdateien).** AI auto-digitisers now turn images into stitch files "with minimal input" for beginners (https://absolutedigitizing.com/ai-embroidery-digitizer/), which erodes the old skill moat, and Etsy is full of digitising offers (https://www.etsy.com/market/embroidery_digitizing). Custom digitising is a service, which the brief excludes. No supply gap was found, and the German market could not be measured (search budget exhausted).
- **Fonts with Czech/CE diacritics.** A tempting language gap, but Google Fonts offers a large free catalogue with Latin Extended coverage, strong Czech foundries already sell to professionals, and a sellable family takes months to years of type-design skill [prior knowledge, not re-verified].
