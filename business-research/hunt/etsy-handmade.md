# HUNTER: etsy-handmade — physical handmade / personalized goods on Etsy + EU marketplaces

Date: 2026-09-29

## Method and evidence limits (read first)
- 23 WebSearch queries in EN/DE/CZ were run before the **shared session search budget ran out (200/200)**. Every WebFetch attempt was **blocked by the egress proxy**: etsy.com, ebay.de, aukro.cz, granat.cz, topvltaviny.cz, buymoldavite.com, milkies.de, innervisioncrystals.net and fajntip.cz all failed, and reddit was refused.
- As a result, **no Etsy shop sales counters could be read directly**. The figures below come from search-result snippets of the cited pages. Each figure is attributed to the URL it came from. Any value derived from my own reasoning is labelled **estimate**.
- Items marked *(knowledge, not re-verified this session)* are general legal or market facts I could not re-check with a live URL. The next phase should verify them.
- Assumptions used in all unit economics (estimates): €1 ≈ 25 CZK and $1 ≈ €0.85, taken from the brief's own conversion. Etsy fees follow the brief: 6.5% transaction, ~4% + €0.30 payment, €0.18 listing, and offsite ads of 15% on attributed orders, modelled as a **3% average** over all orders. That makes roughly **14% + €0.50 per order**.

## Cross-cutting notes for every candidate below
- **US shipments.** The US ended duty-free de minimis for all countries on 29 Aug 2025. Several EU posts, Česká pošta among them, paused or restricted goods shipments to the US for a while after that *(knowledge, not re-verified this session)*. Every candidate here should therefore be validated **EU-first** (DE/AT/FR/NL/PL via Etsy, eBay.de and Packeta). US buyers are upside, not the base case.
- **VAT.** A non-VAT-payer CZ sole trader who buys Etsy, Meta or Google services from other EU states becomes an *identifikovaná osoba* and must self-assess VAT on those fees *(knowledge, not re-verified)*. Budget about 1 hour per month of admin for this.
- **EU 14-day withdrawal right.** It applies to stock items such as moldavite and vintage pieces, so a 3–5% returns allowance is included. Personalized or made-to-spec goods such as breast-milk jewelry are exempt under Directive 2011/83/EU Art. 16(c) *(knowledge, not re-verified)*.
- **Product safety.** GPSR (EU 2023/988, in force since Dec 2024) applies to all consumer products: you need a manufacturer name and address on the product or packaging, plus a basic risk file. Jewelry must also meet the REACH limits for nickel, lead and cadmium *(knowledge)*.
- **Hallmarking (Czech puncovní zákon).** Silver items up to **3.49 g** are exempt from the state hallmark but must still carry a fineness mark and the seller's registered responsibility mark. Heavier items must be submitted to the Puncovní úřad. Sellers must be registered, and the rule also covers e-shops ([puncovnictvivpraxi.cz](https://www.puncovnictvivpraxi.cz/vyrobce-a-obchodnik/23-zakladni-povinnosti-vyrobcu-a-obchodniku-se-sperky), [style4.cz](https://www.style4.cz/povinne-znaceni-sperku/), [Puncovní úřad FAQ](http://www.puncovniurad.cz/cz/faq.aspx)).
- **Trade licence.** Retail and simple production fall under the volná živnost "Výroba, obchod a služby neuvedené v přílohách 1 až 3" and can be run as vedlejší činnost *(knowledge)*.

### 1. Genuine Czech moldavite — graded raw specimens with locality provenance, shipped from CZ
- **What you sell / type:** Physical. Individually photographed "the stone you get" raw moldavite pieces of 0.3–5 g, each with weight, locality (e.g. Chlum, Besednice) and a signed authenticity card. Sold on Etsy in the Craft Supplies / crystals category (reselling supplies is allowed there) and on eBay.de.
- **Buyer & channel:** Crystal and metaphysical buyers and jewelry makers in DE/EU, plus US and UK buyers once post and tariff conditions allow. Channels: Etsy, eBay.de, and later an own Shopify store.
- **Price (€):** €25–60 for 0.3–1 g pieces and €80–300 for 2–5 g grade-A pieces.
- **Unit economics (estimate, 1 g grade B/A piece sold at €50 incl. EU shipping):**
  - Stone cost €12–24. Reasoning: the Czech buyout price is 100–200 CZK/g (€4–8/g). A small buyer pays dealers or finders roughly 2–3× that, i.e. 300–600 CZK/g.
  - Etsy fees ~€7. Card, gem bag and box €2. Tracked EU letter €6–7 (estimate). Returns allowance €1.50.
  - **Net ≈ €9–21 per 1 g sale.** A 3 g grade-A stone at €180 nets ≈ €80–110.
- **Demand evidence:**
  - The median US price paid is **$73/g from 898 real sales (2022–2026)**, up from $20/g in 2020 and $48/g in 2021 ([buymoldavite.com](https://buymoldavite.com/what-is-moldavite-worth/)).
  - 2026 retail typically runs $30–150/g ([gemslore.com](https://gemslore.com/moldavite-price/)).
  - InnerVision reports that prices doubled in the 2021 TikTok wave and stabilised at $18–25/g for most stones in 2024, against $10–16/g before the wave ([innervisioncrystals.net](https://www.innervisioncrystals.net/blogs/news/moldavite-update-for-2022)).
  - Search results report a raw moldavite Etsy listing with "high sales volume over the past 6 months" ([Etsy listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1884309085/raw-moldavite-crystals-genuine-czech), [Etsy market page](https://www.etsy.com/market/czech_moldavite)).
  - Etsy also carries 50 g wholesale lots ([listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1582005716/50g-genuine-moldavite-wholesale-czech)).
  - eBay.de has an active "ECHT aus Tschechien" market, for example 0.2 g and 1.2 g pieces ([ebay.de/itm/156610540422](https://www.ebay.de/itm/156610540422), [ebay.de/itm/156924783794](https://www.ebay.de/itm/156924783794)).
- **Supply gap / asymmetry evidence:**
  - **Price asymmetry:** the Czech buyout is 100–200 CZK/g, about $5–9/g ([fondik.cz, citing Granát Turnov](https://www.fondik.cz/clanky/vltavin-cena)). One CZ dealer guarantees buyout at 100–3,000 CZK/g ([vltaviny-obchod.cz](https://vltaviny-obchod.cz/vykup/)). The median US retail price is $73/g, as cited above.
  - **Supply is constrained.** Etsy seller text surfaced in search says quality and quantity have been "increasingly limited due to government regulations, unauthorized mining, and seasonal mining limitations" since late 2020 ([Etsy market page](https://www.etsy.com/market/moldavite_from_czech)). Deposits are running dry ([aboutmoldavites.com](https://www.aboutmoldavites.com/moldavites-are-running-out/)).
  - **Trust gap.** Chinese, Thai and Indian fakes with fake certificates flood the marketplaces, and platforms barely police them ([ancientenergy.com](https://ancientenergy.com/blogs/knowledge-center/real-vs-fake-moldavite-complete-guide)). US resellers advertise "American Seller" in their listings ([Etsy listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1340419357/genuine-moldavite-czech-republic)). Genuine CZ-resident Etsy sellers look few and small: Moldavitestones in the Plzeň region is a Star Seller with only **533 sales** ([moldavitestones.etsy.com](https://moldavitestones.etsy.com/)), and MoldaviteMiner is in CZ ([Etsy shop](https://www.etsy.com/shop/MoldaviteMiner)).
- **Edge for a CZ-based solo founder:**
  - You are physically in the only source country. You can buy in CZK from finders and dealers on bazos, sbazar and Aukro and at mineral fairs, and inspect the stones in person.
  - You can offer a credible provenance story: a locality photo, shipping from Czechia, and a Czech-language invoice from the dealer.
  - Language access to Czech dealer and finder networks that foreign sellers lack.
- **Startup cost (€) & hours/week:**
  - About €1,800. That covers ~100 g of mixed inventory at ~€15/g (estimate), a 0.01 g scale, loupe and light box (€150), cards and packaging (€100) and Etsy setup.
  - About 5–8 h per week for photographing each stone, listing and packing.
- **Realistic monthly profit after 12 months (estimate):**
  - Pessimistic €50.
  - Realistic €400: about 25 orders per month at ~€16 net each.
  - Optimistic €1,500: grade-A pieces plus repeat jewelry-maker buyers.
  - The low ceiling reflects that the leading CZ shop found has only 533 lifetime sales.
- **CZ/EU legal/regulatory notes:**
  - Buy only from sellers with a traceable legal origin. Collecting requires landowner consent, and illegal digging is the "unauthorized mining" problem cited above.
  - Avoid health or metaphysical efficacy claims (Etsy policy and EU consumer law).
  - Keep an invoice trail for authenticity disputes.
  - The 14-day withdrawal right applies.
- **Key risks:**
  - Unknowingly buying fakes, especially through bazaars. Mitigate with reputable dealers only, UV and inclusion checks, and a lab test for large stones.
  - Fad decay: demand is TikTok-driven, although prices held through 2024–2026 per the sources above.
  - Small absolute margin on sub-1 g stones, and loss or theft in the post.
  - The US channel is currently impaired.
- **Confidence: 3/5.**
  - For: a real price asymmetry, genuine supply scarcity, a trust gap that favours a CZ seller, and cited paid demand.
  - Against: I could not read Etsy sales counts directly, the leading CZ shop's small volume caps the upside, and my cost-per-gram figure for small buyers is an estimate.

  - Against: I have no direct Etsy sales count for moldavite jewelry specifically.

### 3. Vintage and antique Bohemian garnet jewelry sourced in CZ, sold abroad on Etsy Vintage and eBay

- **Realistic monthly profit after 12 months (estimate):**
  - Pessimistic €50.
  - Realistic €450: about 22 kits per month.
  - Optimistic €1,500.
- **CZ/EU legal/regulatory notes:**
  - Epoxy and UV resins are typically CLP-classified skin sensitisers. They need a CLP label in the buyer's language, an SDS, and possibly a UFI/poison-centre notification for mixtures you re-pack *(knowledge, not re-verified)*.
  - Postal dangerous-goods limits may apply to resins, so check the Packeta and Česká pošta rules.
  - GPSR applies.
- **Key risks:**
  - Chinese kit sellers undercutting on price.
  - Chemical compliance if you re-pack resin. Mitigate by buying a pre-labelled branded resin and including it unopened.
  - Poor customer results leading to bad reviews.
- **Confidence: 2/5.** Demand is plausible and cheap to test. However, there is no price or sales data, and the chemical compliance adds friction.

### 6. Modern minimalist jewelry with genuine Bohemian pyrope (Czech garnet), origin-certified, in 925 or gold-filled

## Rejected
- **Wedding-bouquet resin preservation.** Demand is real: artists limit monthly slots, take deposits, and quote timelines of 35–50 weeks or 6–12 months ([Etsy "booking deposit only" listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1493771905/wedding-bouquet-resin-preservation), [floristcheck.com](https://floristcheck.com/complete-guide-to-wedding-bouquet-preservation-pricing/), [bloomandmake.com](https://www.bloomandmake.com/blog/wedding-bouquet-preservation-cost)). Rejected for four reasons:
  1. It is quasi-service work on client material.
  2. Hands-on time runs well over 2 h per piece (drying, arranging, multiple pours, sanding), and each order takes months.
  3. Fresh flowers limit intake to local buyers.
  4. The CZ market is small: the Prague leader Nature ID reports only 250+ brides since 2017 ([natureid.cz](https://eshop.natureid.cz/objekty)).
- **Pet or human ashes fused into glass pendants.** It is not supply-constrained. DE sellers produce in 7 working days, with 20–30 days total delivery ([urnengeschaft.de](https://www.urnengeschaft.de/asche-anhaenger-aus-glas.html)). Etsy lampworkers quote 1–2 weeks ([Etsy listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/565728825/one-handmade-in-usa-borosilicate-glass)). Many DE shops and even pet crematoria already sell it ([seelenglas.de](https://seelenglas.de/products/memorial-kette-asche-schwarz-andenken-an-verstorbenes-haustier-urne-glas-personalisiert), [klapperglas.de](https://www.klapperglas.de/products/gedenkschmuck-ascheschmuck-kette-asche-trauerschmuck-schwarz-andenken-an-verstorbenes-haustier-urne-glas-personalisiert), [ascheschmuck24.de](https://ascheschmuck24.de/Fuer-Tiere), [humania-tierbestattungen.de](https://www.humania-tierbestattungen.de/asche-glasschmuck.php)). It also needs lampwork or kiln skill and involves handling cremains across borders.
- **Reselling Czech seed beads (Preciosa/Jablonec).** This is a commodity with no handmade value added. CZ-based Etsy sellers such as ScaraBeads already ship 20 g bags from CZ with 4–8 day US delivery ([Etsy listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/250903979/20g-130-czech-glass-seed-beads-preciosa), [Etsy market page](https://www.etsy.com/market/preciosa_czech_beads)). Margins are thin per bag, and the "from Czechia" edge is already taken.
- **Reselling Rautis glass-bead Christmas ornaments.** There is a single UNESCO-listed producer, which sells directly through its own e-shop ([rautis.cz](https://www.rautis.cz/?lang=en)), and US importers already carry Czech glass ornaments ([europeanmarket.us](https://www.europeanmarket.us/blogs/meet-the-artisans/czech-glass-ornaments)). Reselling another maker's new goods is not allowed as "handmade" on Etsy. The business would be seasonal (Q4 only), and the only supply-problem signal found was a stale 2024 note.
- **Generic personalized name necklaces, laser-engraved boards and signs, wooden baby name puzzles.** Rejected on reasoning, **not verified this session** because the search budget was exhausted. These are the most saturated Etsy personalization categories, dominated by print-on-demand and factory sellers with free shipping. A CZ maker has no cost, access or language edge. They should be treated as rejected unless another hunter has contrary data.
