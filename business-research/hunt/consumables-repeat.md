# HUNTER: consumables-repeat

Lens: repeat-purchase consumables that one person can make or private-label with light EU/CZ regulation.

## Read this first: evidence limitations (2026-09-29)
- This hunter ran **9 web searches**. After that the session-wide WebSearch budget (200 calls, shared by the swarm) was exhausted, so no further searches were possible.
- **Every WebFetch attempt was blocked by the egress proxy**: etsy.com, ebay.de, reddit.com, heureka.cz, shop.foacreatives.com, etwool.cz, henliahandmade.com, ukzuz.gov.cz, eur-lex.europa.eu and en.wikipedia.org. So no marketplace sold-counts, Etsy shop sales numbers or Heureka rankings were read first-hand.
- Consequences:
  - Claims with a URL come from search-result snippets.
  - Claims marked **[unverified, background]** are the hunter's prior knowledge. They are *not* evidence under the brief and must be checked before anyone relies on them.
  - Every money figure marked **estimate** shows its reasoning.
  - Confidence scores are capped accordingly.
- Each candidate ends with a **verification checklist** for the dossier stage.

### 1. Hand-dyed mini-skein advent calendar, sold on pre-order (24 × 20 g sock/fingering)
- **What you sell / type:** Physical. A boxed set of 24 numbered 20 g mini skeins of hand-dyed superwash merino/nylon sock yarn, sold as a colour story (e.g. "Bohemian winter forest"), optionally with one pattern PDF. Pre-orders run June–September; the calendars ship in early November. The pre-order model means base yarn is bought *after* cash is collected.
- **Buyer & channel:** Knitters in DE/AT/NL/CZ/SK who make sock and shawl projects. Sold through an own shop (Shoptet/Shopify) plus Etsy, and promoted on Instagram, Ravelry groups and Czech/Slovak knitting Facebook groups.
- **Price (€):** €129–149 per calendar (**estimate**: I could not capture competitor prices; the dossier stage must verify this against 5 EU competitors).
- **Unit economics (estimate, at €139 via Etsy):**
  - Base yarn: 480 g × ~€6.5/100 g = ~€31. One data point: a premium merino/cashmere/nylon base sells at $47.75 per 5 × 100 g, about $9.55/skein ([search snippet, dyersupplier/indiedyer](https://dyersupplier.com/collections/featured/wholesale-bare-yarn)). Standard 75/25 SW merino/nylon, 400 m/100 g, is available from EU wholesaler Rooster Yarns ([roosteryarns.com](https://roosteryarns.com/undyed-yarn-superwash-sock)) and KNOMAD ([knomadyarn.com](https://www.knomadyarn.com/)). The €6.5 volume price is an estimate.
  - Dye, citric acid and energy: ~€2.
  - Box, 24 bags and fibre-composition labels: ~€8.
  - Extras (markers, printed card): ~€3.
  - **Materials total: ~€44.**
  - Fees: Etsy (6.5% transaction + ~4% payment + listing) ≈ €15. On an own shop with a card gateway, ≈ €4.
  - Shipping is paid by the buyer (a Packeta/Zásilkovna parcel to DE is roughly €7–12, **estimate**).
  - **Net before labour and tax: ~€80 via Etsy, ~€91 via own shop.**
  - Labour: ~1.5 h per calendar when batched (dyeing, winding 24 minis, labelling, packing; **estimate**).
- **Demand evidence:**
  - Several 2025 advent calendars were sold on pre-order "until sold out": Etsy 24 × 20 g listing ([etsy](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1735985354/hand-dyed-yarn-advent-calendar-2025)), Holly Press Fibers ([link](https://hollypressfibers.com/products/forage-preorder-a-twelve-mini-skein-advent-calendar-for-2025)), Pancake & Lulu ([link](https://pancakeandlulu.com/products/2025-festive-folktale-yarn-mini-skein-advent-calendar-pre-order)), Southern Skeins/Vintage Lane on Etsy ([link](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1208504935/2025-hand-dyed-yarn-advent-calendar)).
  - Sweet Sparrow's 2025 advent is marked **SOLD OUT** ([link](https://sweet-sparrow.com/shop/sweet-sparrow-yarns-advent-calendar-2025)).
  - Rainier Roses ran its 2025 advent pre-order only "until April 30 or until sold out" ([link](https://www.rainier-roses-yarn-co.com/products/2025-advent-calendar-pre-order)).
  - Indie Untangled describes small-batch dyer updates that sell out in minutes and knitters who miss them ([indieuntangled.com/about](https://indieuntangled.com/about/)). This is qualitative only; I have no 2025 sell-out timings.
  - A caveat: nearly all of this evidence is **US-based**.
- **Supply gap / asymmetry evidence:**
  - The pre-order-then-sold-out pattern above suggests demand exceeds a single dyer's capacity. Mini-skein winding is labour-heavy, which caps each dyer's volume.
  - I found **no** EU/CZ-specific evidence of a gap.
  - The CZ market already has at least 8 indie dyers: [popletenemamy.cz](https://www.popletenemamy.cz/POPLETENA-RUCNE-BARVENA-KLUBKA-c15_0_1.htm), [lovespunning.cz](https://www.lovespunning.cz/100-merino/fingering/), [FÖA creatives (dyed in Braník)](https://shop.foacreatives.com/rucne-barvena-prize/ponozkova-prize/), [etwool.cz](https://www.etwool.cz/rucne-barvena-ponozkova-prize), [mountainfibres.cz](https://www.mountainfibres.cz/ice-flowers--dk/), [emiteriyarns.cz](https://www.emiteriyarns.cz/), [divokeklubicko.cz](https://www.divokeklubicko.cz/ponozkove-prize) and [decida.cz](https://www.decida.cz/osobni-udaje-informacni-povinnost). **Whether they sell advent calendars, and whether those sell out, is unknown.**
- **Edge for a CZ-based solo founder:**
  - EU-internal shipping with no customs. UK dyers selling to the EU face import VAT/IOSS friction after Brexit **[unverified, background]**.
  - Czech labour cost and cheap Packeta EU shipping.
  - CZ/SK-language marketing.
  - Overall the edge is weak to moderate: nothing a German dyer can't copy.
- **Startup cost (€) & hours/week:**
  - Startup: ~€1,500–2,500 (**estimate**). That covers dye pots, scale, acid dyes (~€150), swift and ball winder (~€80), a niddy-noddy/skein winder, 10–15 kg of base (€650–1,000), labels, photo setup and a shop subscription.
  - Only ~€600–800 is at risk before pre-orders come in (samples plus a photo set).
  - Hours: ~5 h/week Jan–May (design, samples), ~8 h/week during pre-order, and **~15 h/week in Sep–Oct** (production). About 100 calendars is the solo ceiling (100 × 1.5 h = 150 h).
- **Realistic monthly profit after 12 months (averaged over the year, after ~€600/yr fixed costs, before income tax; estimates):**
  - Pessimistic: €120 (25 calendars).
  - Realistic: €400 (60 calendars + a few sock sets).
  - Optimistic: €750 (100 calendars at the capacity cap).
- **CZ/EU legal/regulatory notes (light):**
  - No CPNP, no food/feed rules, no CLP label on the finished yarn.
  - The Textile Fibre Names Regulation (EU) 1007/2011 requires fibre-composition labelling, in Czech for CZ consumers **[unverified, background; eur-lex fetch blocked]**.
  - GPSR (EU) 2023/988 requires manufacturer identification and safety info on the product and in online listings.
  - REACH Annex XVII azo-dye restriction: use reputable acid dyes (Jacquard/Ashford/Dharma).
  - Selling to DE requires German VerpackG/LUCID packaging registration plus system participation (~€50–100/yr) **[unverified, background]**.
  - Once cross-border EU B2C sales exceed €10k/yr, destination VAT/OSS applies.
  - Volná živnost is enough.
- **Key risks:**
  - The US-heavy evidence may not transfer to the EU.
  - CZ already has 8+ dyers.
  - Seasonality: about 80% of profit arrives in Q4.
  - The craft market may be contracting: US chain Joann liquidated in 2025 ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2025/02/28/nx-s1-5308188/joann-closing-customers)), and a 2025 YouTube video explains why indie yarn shops are closing ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mGgSj_Nt1W8)).
  - Dyers report thin per-skein margins and seven-day weeks ([search snippet, henliahandmade](https://henliahandmade.com/how-much-money-indie-dyers-make/)).
  - Colourway photography is a real skill.
- **Confidence: 3/5.** The pre-order model limits risk and there are several 2025 sold-out or pre-order-closed signals. But the evidence is US-centric, prices are unverified, and the CZ supply is crowded.
- **Verification checklist:**
  - Etsy "yarn advent calendar 2026" filtered to EU sellers: prices, review counts, "only X left".
  - Check whether the CZ dyers above ran 2025 calendars and whether those sold out.
  - Instagram #adventskalenderwolle / #wolladventskalender.

### 2. Self-striping sock yarn club (quarterly, paid upfront), from the same dye studio
- **What you sell / type:** Physical subscription. Every quarter the subscriber gets 3 × 100 g of self-striping superwash merino/nylon sock yarn in exclusive colourways, billed upfront for each quarter. Single skeins of past colourways go on general sale. This uses the candidate-1 studio in Jan–Aug, when advent work is quiet.
- **Buyer & channel:** CZ/SK/DE sock knitters. Own shop with a recurring-billing plugin; the Czech/Slovak "ponožkování" Facebook groups; Ravelry.
- **Price (€):** €24–27 per 100 g skein; club price €70 per quarter for 3 skeins (**estimate**; not verified against competitors).
- **Unit economics (estimate, per skein at €25 on own shop):**
  - Base €6.5, dye €0.5, label and bag €1, gateway fee ~€0.8.
  - Club packages ship at ~€4–6 buyer-paid.
  - **Net ≈ €16/skein.**
  - Labour: ~25 min/skein, because self-striping needs a long-warp skein-winding set-up (**estimate**).
- **Demand evidence:** Only indirect. Sock yarn is the core product at most CZ dyers listed under candidate 1 (e.g. [etwool.cz ručně barvená ponožková příze](https://www.etwool.cz/rucne-barvena-ponozkova-prize), [divokeklubicko.cz ponožkové příze](https://www.divokeklubicko.cz/ponozkove-prize)). I have **no sell-out or subscription numbers**.
- **Supply gap / asymmetry evidence:** None verified. The hypothesis is that self-striping yarn is technically harder than speckled or tonal dyeing, so fewer small dyers offer it and industrial brands (Opal, Regia) dominate **[unverified, background]**.
- **Edge for a CZ-based solo founder:** Same as candidate 1: a CZ/SK-language niche and cheap EU shipping. It also shares equipment with candidate 1, so no extra capex.
- **Startup cost (€) & hours/week:** An extra ~€150 on top of candidate 1 (warping board, long-skein set-up). 5–8 h/week.
- **Realistic monthly profit after 12 months (estimate):**
  - Pessimistic: €80 (15 members).
  - Realistic: €250 (40 members plus loose skeins).
  - Optimistic: €600 (100 members, which is near the labour cap).
- **CZ/EU legal/regulatory notes:** Same as candidate 1 (textile labelling, GPSR, packaging EPR). For subscriptions, EU consumer rules need clear cancellation terms, and the 14-day withdrawal right applies to each shipment.
- **Key risks:** Subscriber churn; crowded CZ sock-yarn supply; colour-repeatability issues; no hard evidence of demand.
- **Confidence: 2/5.** It is plausible and low-risk as an add-on, but no demand evidence was captured.
- **Verification checklist:**
  - Count CZ/SK/DE dyers that offer self-striping yarn.
  - Check sold-out ratios on their product pages.
  - Check existing "sock club" offers and whether their waitlists close.

### 3. Leather strop paddle + chromium-oxide stropping compound refills (sharpening consumables)
- **What you sell / type:** Physical. A hand-assembled strop paddle (vegetable-tanned leather on a beech or oak paddle) sold as a starter kit, plus the consumable: 40–50 g bars of green chromium-oxide stropping compound. A camellia or food-grade blade oil can be added as a second consumable. Assembly takes ~20 min per paddle.
- **Buyer & channel:** Spoon/green-wood carvers, whittlers, knife enthusiasts and straight-razor shavers in DE/AT/CZ/PL. Channels: Etsy, Allegro.cz/pl, Kaufland.cz/de marketplaces, and woodcarving forums and courses.
- **Price (€):** Kit €29–39; compound refill bar €8–12 (**estimate; not verified**).
- **Unit economics (estimate):**
  - Paddle kit: leather offcut €2–4, wood blank €1.5, glue and finish €0.5, one compound bar €1.5, box €1.
  - Fees: ~12% on Etsy (€4). Shipping buyer-paid.
  - **Net ≈ €20 per €34 kit.**
  - Refill bar: materials €1.2 + packaging €0.5 + fees €1.2 ≈ €3. **Net ≈ €7 per €10 bar.** It fits a letter-post envelope.
- **Demand evidence:** **None captured this session** (Etsy, Allegro and eBay fetches were blocked). The hypothesis is that strops and compound are a staple Etsy/Amazon category for woodcarvers **[unverified, background]**.
- **Supply gap / asymmetry evidence:** None verified. The category is probably competitive.
- **Edge for a CZ-based solo founder:** Weak. Czech/Central European wood and leather are cheap, and CZ/PL/SK marketplaces offer a local-language listing advantage.
- **Startup cost (€) & hours/week:** ~€400–700 (leather hide, wood stock, bandsaw access or pre-cut blanks, compound raw materials, molds, packaging). 4–6 h/week.
- **Realistic monthly profit after 12 months (estimate):**
  - Pessimistic: €50.
  - Realistic: €200 (≈8 kits + 10 refills per month).
  - Optimistic: €500.
- **CZ/EU legal/regulatory notes (very light):**
  - GPSR only.
  - Chromium(III) oxide is generally not CLP-classified, so a wax-bound compound likely needs no CLP label **[unverified, background; check the ECHA C&L inventory and the supplier SDS]**.
  - Use vegetable-tanned leather to stay clear of the REACH Annex XVII entry 47 Cr(VI) limit for leather in skin contact.
  - Packaging EPR applies for DE.
- **Key risks:** A crowded category with Amazon/AliExpress price pressure. Strops are durable, so repeat purchases rely on compound bars with a low ticket price. No demand evidence was gathered.
- **Confidence: 2/5.** Regulation is light and it is solo-feasible, but demand and gap are unproven.
- **Verification checklist:**
  - Etsy "leather strop" filtered to EU sellers: sales counts of the top 5 shops.
  - Allegro.pl/cz sold counts ("sprzedano") for strop kits.
  - Amazon.de BSR for "Abziehleder".

### 4. Aquarium & terrarium botanicals / leaf litter from Czech forests (alder cones, oak, beech, walnut leaves)
- **What you sell / type:** Physical. Cleaned, boiled and dried Central European botanicals sold as blackwater/biotope aquarium décor and naturalistic terrarium substrate in 20–100 g packs and mixed "biotope boxes". Raw material is collected in autumn with landowner permission, so it costs almost nothing.
- **Buyer & channel:** Shrimp, betta and blackwater-aquarium keepers and isopod/dart-frog keepers in DE/AT/NL/CZ/PL. Channels: eBay.de, Kleinanzeigen, Etsy, Allegro, and hobby Facebook groups.
- **Price (€):** €4–8 per small pack; €12–20 per mixed box (**estimate; not verified**).
- **Unit economics (estimate):**
  - Raw material ~€0; bag and label €0.4; fees ~12%.
  - Shipping is the constraint: letter or small-parcel post from CZ to DE costs roughly €3–6 (**estimate**).
  - **Net ≈ €3–5 per pack, €8–12 per box.**
  - Processing is batch work in October–November; packing takes about 5 min per order.
- **Demand evidence:** **None captured** (the search budget ran out on this query). The hypothesis is that "Erlenzapfen", "Seemandelbaumblätter" and "Laub für Asseln" are established eBay.de/Amazon.de listings with repeat buyers, because leaves decompose and need constant replenishment **[unverified, background]**.
- **Supply gap / asymmetry evidence:** None verified.
- **Edge for a CZ-based solo founder:** Real but small: free raw material in CZ forests, and labour to collect and process it that costs more in DE/NL.
- **Startup cost (€) & hours/week:** ~€200–400 (drying racks, pot, dehydrator, bags, scale). About 10 h/week in collection season, then 2–3 h/week packing.
- **Realistic monthly profit after 12 months (estimate):**
  - Pessimistic: €40.
  - Realistic: €150 (≈30 orders/month).
  - Optimistic: €400.
  - A low ticket price caps the upside.
- **CZ/EU legal/regulatory notes:**
  - If marketed as *food* for isopods or shrimp it is feed. That requires ÚKZÚZ feed-business registration: the fee is 5,000 CZK and unregistered production is fined up to 500,000 CZK ([portal.gov.cz](https://portal.gov.cz/sluzby-vs/registrace-krmivarskeho-provozu-pro-vyrobu-krmiv-S15034)).
  - Marketing it strictly as décor/substrate is a grey zone, because the animals eat it.
  - Commercial collection needs forest-owner consent under the Forest Act 289/1995 **[unverified, background]**.
  - Dried leaves likely need no plant passport intra-EU, but non-EU export (UK, CH, US) needs phytosanitary certificates, so stay EU-only **[unverified, background]**.
  - Avoid health/antibacterial claims. GPSR applies.
- **Key risks:** Low price per order; the feed-classification grey zone; mould and pest contamination if drying is poor; the isopod-hobby boom may have peaked.
- **Confidence: 2/5.** Raw material is nearly free, but there is no demand data and the upside is small.
- **Verification checklist:**
  - eBay.de sold listings for "Erlenzapfen" and "Laub Asseln".
  - Kleinanzeigen listing volume.
  - Etsy EU shops selling "leaf litter isopods": sales counts.
  - Ask ÚKZÚZ whether "décor" labelling avoids feed status.

### 5. Handmade watercolour half-pans featuring Bohemian earth pigments
- **What you sell / type:** Physical. Hand-mulled watercolour half-pans (gum arabic, honey or glycerin, pigment) in curated 6- and 12-colour tins. The hero colours are Central European earths (Bohemian green earth, local ochres); the rest use standard non-toxic pigments. Refill pans are the repeat-purchase consumable.
- **Buyer & channel:** Adult hobby painters, urban sketchers and journalers in the EU and the US. Channels: Etsy, an own shop, Instagram.
- **Price (€):** €7–10 per half-pan; €45–60 for a 6-tin, €80–110 for a 12-tin (**estimate; not verified**).
- **Unit economics (estimate):**
  - Pigment and binder €0.5–1.5 per pan, empty pan €0.2, tin €3–5, fees ~12%.
  - **Net ≈ €5–7 per pan, ~€35 per €55 6-tin.**
  - Labour: ~10–20 min of mulling per pan, which makes the product labour-bound.
- **Demand evidence:** **None captured this session.** Handmade watercolour is a large Etsy category with several high-volume shops **[unverified, background]**.
- **Supply gap / asymmetry evidence:** None verified. The category is likely crowded. The only asymmetry is Bohemian green earth, a historic terre verte from the Kadaň area that pigment houses such as Kremer list **[unverified, background]**. That is a story angle, and novelty earns zero points under the brief.
- **Edge for a CZ-based solo founder:** Access to local earth pigments and a Czech/Bohemian provenance story. Otherwise none.
- **Startup cost (€) & hours/week:** ~€500–900 (glass muller and slab, pigment set, gum arabic, pans, tins, printed swatch cards). 6–10 h/week.
- **Realistic monthly profit after 12 months (estimate):**
  - Pessimistic: €50.
  - Realistic: €200.
  - Optimistic: €600.
- **CZ/EU legal/regulatory notes:**
  - CLP classification depends on the pigment. Avoid cadmium, cobalt, chromium-yellow and lead pigments, and the paints can stay unclassified.
  - Preservatives such as clove oil can trigger EUH208 sensitiser labelling.
  - Label and market as "not a toy / adults 14+" to stay outside the Toy Safety Directive/EN 71.
  - GPSR applies.
  - Sourcing earth pigment yourself needs landowner consent and heavy-metal testing **[unverified, background]**.
- **Key risks:** A crowded category; labour-bound mulling; the angle relies on novelty; no demand data.
- **Confidence: 1/5.**
- **Verification checklist:**
  - Etsy "handmade watercolor" top-shop sales counts, and the number of EU sellers.
  - Price per half-pan.
  - Whether Bohemian green earth is actually obtainable in bulk from CZ.

## Rejected
1. **Carp boilies / hook baits (CZ carp scene).**
   - Saturated: CZ retailers carry at least 10–12 brands, including Carp Servis, Nikl, Starbaits, TB Baits, Mivardi, Nash, Nutrabaits, Sportcarp, Sticky Baits, The One and Vitalbaits ([parys.cz](https://www.parys.cz/boilies-mivardi/), [nikl.cz](https://www.nikl.cz/boilies/)). Local producers already operate, e.g. [navnadyproryby.cz (Brno)](https://www.navnadyproryby.cz/).
   - Prices are low: a premium brand sells at about €15–16/kg on Heureka (Karel Nikl 1 kg from 375–399 CZK, [heureka](https://navnady-a-nastrahy.heureka.cz/karel-nikl-ready-boilies-krill-berry-1-kg-24-mm/)), and the product is heavy to ship.
   - It is a feed-classification risk: ÚKZÚZ registration costs 5,000 CZK, with fines up to 500,000 CZK ([portal.gov.cz](https://portal.gov.cz/sluzby-vs/registrace-krmivarskeho-provozu-pro-vyrobu-krmiv-S15034)).
2. **Pet treats/chews and specialty wild-bird food.**
   - Pet food and dog chews require both ÚKZÚZ feed-facility registration and approval from the regional veterinary authority ([svscr.cz petfood](https://www.svscr.cz/krmiva-vzp-asanace/krmiva-pro-zajmova-zvirata/), [ÚKZÚZ](https://ukzuz.gov.cz/public/portal/ukzuz/krmiva/schvalovani-a-registrace-provozu/vyroba-krmiv-1)). Animal-origin chews add animal by-product rules, so a home kitchen won't qualify.
   - Bird food also needs feed registration, and it is heavy and low-value against supermarket and garden-centre competition **[unverified, background]**.
3. **Czech glass bead resale / repacking (Jablonec access).**
   - No scarcity: Preciosa Ornela produces around 3,000 t of seed beads a year and is actively launching products ([preciosa-ornela.com](https://www.preciosa-ornela.com/company)).
   - Jablonec-area sellers already occupy Etsy and are discounting, e.g. [czechandglassbeads](https://www.etsy.com/shop/czechandglassbeads) (30% off 300 items) and [BeadsFromCzech](https://www.etsy.com/shop/BeadsFromCzech).
   - The "local access" edge is already priced in.
4. **3D-printer filament niches.** A solo founder can't extrude, so it would be private label. Branded PLA/PETG from Bambu, Elegoo and Sunlu sells at roughly €10–18/kg on EU marketplaces **[unverified, background]**, which leaves a thin margin after shipping a 1.2 kg parcel. Commodity pricing collapses any niche once it proves itself.
5. **Candles / wax melts.**
   - Fragrance oils usually make the mixture CLP-classified (skin sensitiser H317, aquatic hazards). That brings full CLP labels, EUH208 allergen lines and, where classified, UFI/poison-centre notification **[unverified, background]**.
   - The category is hyper-saturated on Etsy and Vinted, and CZ craft fairs are full of it.
6. **Epoxy resin, mica and alcohol-ink craft supplies.**
   - Epoxy resin and hardener are classified mixtures (irritant/sensitiser, aquatic chronic). They need CLP labels, SDS and UFI/poison-centre notification. A private-label seller becomes the "supplier" under REACH/CLP **[unverified, background]**.
   - Amazon and AliExpress price competition leaves little margin.
7. **Specialty coffee / tea.** These are food: they need KHS/SZPI registration and HACCP-style hygiene. Roasting needs premises. The CZ specialty-roaster scene is already dense **[unverified, background]**. There is no supply gap and the regulatory load isn't light.
8. **Leather-care balm / boot wax.**
   - Repurchase is slow: one tin lasts about a year.
   - The category is brand-dominated (Saphir, Collonil, Bick 4, Otter Wax) **[unverified, background]**.
   - Solvent-based formulas (turpentine, naphtha) make the mixture CLP-classified.
   - Any "also for hands" claim turns it into a cosmetic, which means CPNP and a safety assessment.
   - I found no evidence of a gap.

## Also noted, not pursued
- **Wet-shaving soaps and balms:** these are cosmetics, which need CPNP notification plus a safety assessor's report (CPSR) per formula. Too heavy.
- **Saaz hops for homebrewers:** Czech hop certification rules apply **[unverified, background]**, and homebrewing has been declining since its pandemic peak.
- **Tungsten beads and fly-tying materials for Czech nymphing:** CZ brands (e.g. Hends) already dominate **[unverified, background]**.
