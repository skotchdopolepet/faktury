## DDR4 RAM shortage
- DDR4 chip prices hit a **record $42.50 in August 2026**. A 32 GB DDR4 kit cost $60–90 in Oct 2025 and $150–180 in Jan 2026. DDR4 spot prices were 172% above contract prices in Jan 2026. Commentators recommend the used and refurbished market.
  - Sources: https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/ram/ram-price-index-2026-lowest-price-on-ddr5-and-ddr4-memory-of-all-capacities, https://www.techpowerup.com/345717/ddr4-prices-skyrocketing-amid-dram-shortage-crunch, https://tech-insider.org/dram-ram-price-crisis-2026/

## EU lead ban on fishing tackle
- The REACH Committee adopted the restriction on **29 April 2026**. Sales bans follow on this schedule:
  - fishing wire and drop-in sinkers: after 6 months
  - sinkers ≤50 g: after 3 years
  - sinkers 50–140 g: after 5 years
- It's a sales ban, not a ban on anglers using lead. Entry into force follows a 3-month scrutiny period, so late 2026.
  - Sources: https://www.eftta.com/news/article/reach-committee-adopts-eu-lead-restrictions-for-fishing-tackle, https://www.birdlife.org/news/2026/04/30/press-release-eu-takes-major-step-toward-lead-ban-in-fishing-weights/, https://angling-international.com/2026/05/03/lead-ban-restrictions-to-effect-sales-not-use-by-anglers/
- Demand is forced but slow. Big tackle brands will fill it, and a CZ carp-scene angle is possible.
