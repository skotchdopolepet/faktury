# Review 05 (ECONOMICS lens): flash-modded iPod Classic from broken CZ donors

Reviewer lens: unit economics and time. Date: 2026-09-29. FX 24.5 CZK/EUR.
Tags: **[verified: URL]** = search snippet seen (this session or in `02`/`06`); **[knowledge]** = unverified background; **[estimate]** = my arithmetic, reasoning shown.
One WebSearch was used (CZ Bazoš modded listings). Its result is cited in §2.

---

## 1. Verdict

**WEAK. Score 4/10.**

- The test is cheap and honest, and the demand is real, so this is not a KILL.
- The dossier's €94/unit CZ net is an **early-month number**. By month 12 three things push it towards **€45–50**:
  - realised prices converge with the Bazoš modders already present, AliExpress refurbs and DE asks;
  - donor and microSD costs rise, which the dossier itself forecasts;
  - small costs it left out (platform share, tools, spare parts) add up.
- At 7 units a month and ~2.7 h per unit all-in, that is **≈ €300/month before levies at ≈ €11/h**, or **≈ €9/h after levies**.
- The DE/Etsy expansion adds **roughly €0–40 a month** once compliance is costed at the real volume.
- This is a small paid hobby, not the asymmetric bet the brief asks for.

## 5. Sell-through, cash and seasonality

- **Sell-through** [estimate]: 3–6 weeks median at 4,500–5,000 Kč on Bazoš and Aukro.
  - There is **no CZ sold-price data** at all. Every price in the dossier is an ask.
  - The stage-1 test falls in **Nov–Dec (the gift season)** if you register in October. That flatters sell-through, so re-check in Jan–Feb before stage 2.
- **Cash tied up at 7 units/month:** about **€1,800–2,000**, plus about €250 of tools.
  - ~10 donors: €360
  - 7 part kits: €520

- **Downside is well bounded.** CZ-only exposure never exceeds about €2,500, and parts are liquid.

  - ≈ **€2,800 pre-tax** over ≈ **290 h**
  - ≈ **€10/h pre-tax**, or ≈ **€7.5/h after 21.75% levies** [estimate]

## 6. Fatal flaws (economic)

1. **No price floor, and a margin that is compressing.**
   - The competitors can already reach the same buyers: CZ modders on Bazoš, AliExpress new-shell refurbs at ~€130–190 landed, and DE sellers at ~€200 including VAT.
   - Inputs are rising at the same time: cards +124% on the NAND shortage [verified: https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/storage/memory-cards-and-flash-drives-prices-rocket-124-percent-some-products-peak-at-261-percent-jump-increases-from-2025-driven-by-ai-chip-shortage-across-a-range-of-formats-and-capacities], and donors are drifting up.
   - A ~€145 cost base under a ~€190 ceiling leaves a **€45 spread that each new modder erodes further**.
2. **The ceiling is low, and it is set by the tax cliff and the donor supply, not by the founder's effort.**
   - CZ donor flow caps volume at about 8–10 units a month.
   - At €45–70 net, that is €300–700/month pre-tax.
   - Under the side-activity regime, take-home **plateaus at ≈ €313/month** for any profit between €400 and €503 a month.
   - The "optimistic" €670 pre-tax is only ≈ €417 after levies.
3. **The expansion path destroys value.**
   - DE/Etsy costs €1,200–1,700 a year in fixed compliance to add 2–3 units a month.
   - It brings 24-month DE warranty exposure, Abmahnung risk, monthly identified-person returns and the €10k OSS cap.
   - The upside is **≈ €0–40 a month**. The dossier half-admits this, but still books DE units into its realistic M12.

These aren't instant killers, because the test is cheap. They do mean that **a pass is worth only ~€300/month**.

## 7. Fixable issues and fixes

| Issue | Fix |
|---|---|
| Asking-price anchors | During the test, record **sold** prices only: Aukro ended auctions, and Bazoš listings that vanish after a buyer confirms. Run an A/B at **4,490 vs 4,990 Kč**, not 4,990 vs 5,490. Plan on 4,490 Kč. |
| The kill bar is measured at the best moment | Keep "net ≥ €55" but **re-test it in February**, after Christmas and with current card and donor prices. If it fails then, stop at stage 1. |
| DE/Etsy stage 3 | **Delete it.** Stay CZ-only, and SK via Packeta only after the SK WEEE and battery registration costs are known. No Etsy, no eBay.de. That also avoids identified-person status entirely, provided you run no Meta/TikTok ads. |
| The social cliff dead zone | Either keep annual profit **≤ ~117k CZK** (≈ 5–6 units/month at the corrected net) or clearly above ~€6k/yr. Never sit between. Time purchases of stock and tools at year-end to manage it. |
| Hours creep | Batch in fours. Buy donors as lots from eBay.at/.de to cut pickups. Standardise on one model family (6G/7G, or 5G/5.5G for easier opening). Ship a printed and QR-video sync guide to cut support. Sell through one or two channels only. |
| Input inflation | Default to 128 GB and 256 GB, offer a "bring-your-own-card" SKU at −900 Kč, and never pre-buy more than one month of cards. |
| Paušál temptation | Use actual expenses. The 60% paušál roughly doubles the taxable profit here. |
| Premium line | Build S3 only against a deposit or a waitlist in CZ. Don't build it to stock. |

## 9. Would I tell a friend to do this?

**Only if** they already enjoy opening iPods and would do it as a hobby anyway.

If so:
- run the €900 CZ-only test;
- price at 4,490–4,990 Kč and log **sold** prices;
- never spend a crown on DE or Etsy compliance;
- stop if the February re-test shows a net under €55 per unit.

As a way to "actually make money" with asymmetric upside, **no**. Once the price catches up with the competition, it pays about €9–11/h, roughly what a DPP side shift pays (≈ 200–300 Kč/h [knowledge]), with inventory, warranty and compliance risk on top.
