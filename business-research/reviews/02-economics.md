## 1. Verdict

**WEAK: 4/10.**

- **What the dossier gets right:**
  - The market is liquid.
  - Stock bought at ≤ 30% of exit price can be liquidated near cost.
  - Little capital is at risk, and the maximum drawdown is ≈ €2.5–3k. This is not a money-loser.
- **Its arithmetic is internally correct but rests on optimistic inputs:**
  - asking prices;
  - €7.5–9.5 postage to DE;
  - a 3% ad rate for a zero-feedback seller;
  - no time for duds, unsold stock or returns;
  - €60/month of fixed costs that cannot cover the DE WEEE representative it budgets.
- **Its own headline is inconsistent.** "€15–20/hour" does not follow from its own figures: €600 ÷ 42–47 h = **€13–14/h**.
- **Rebuilt, the realistic month-12 result is ≈ €280/month before levies at ≈ €7/h,** or ≈ €220 and ≈ €5.3/h after tax and health insurance. That is about the Czech minimum wage.
- **With the fixes in §8 it reaches ≈ €390/month at ≈ €9/h:** no DE WEEE registration, electronic compacts sold in CZ only, and bare lenses instead of adapter kits.
- **Hard ceiling: ≈ €700–750/month before levies, ≈ €450–500 after.** Three limits meet there:
  - the 15 h/week cap, which allows ~20 units a month;
  - the social-insurance dead zone at €400–505/month of profit;
  - the €10k EU distance-sales VAT threshold, which the realistic case reaches in year 2.
- **Year 1 pays ≈ €3–4/h**, once the 70–140 h of unpaid skill ramp are counted.

## 2. Web check (1 search, as allowed)

Query: eBay.de business-seller fees in 2026 for cameras, and the international fee for sellers from other EU countries. The search surfaced the fee reform that took effect on **1 July 2026**:
- The commission is now category-based, at **7–14% of the total including shipping**, plus **€0.35 per order (€0.45 in some categories)**.
- The **"Foto" technology group pays 7%**, and **"Kamerazubehör" (camera accessories) pays 12%**.
- **Certain used or refurbished listings pay 5%.**

Sources: [onlinemarktplatz.de](https://onlinemarktplatz.de/267961/neue-ebay-gebuehren-fuer-gewebliche-verkaeufer-ab-1-juli-2026/), [ezfox.ai](https://ezfox.ai/de/blog/ebay-gebuhren-fur-gewerbliche-verkaufer), [Händlerbund](https://ohn.haendlerbund.de/themen/marktplaetze/ebay-aendert-gebuehren-teurer-guenstiger), [eBay.de rate card](https://www.ebay.de/verkaeuferportal/news/seller-news/2026-januar/gebuehrenaenderungen).

The search did **not** settle three things:
- whether used cameras get the 5% rate;
- whether lenses and adapters fall under the 12% accessory rate;
- the cross-border fee for a CZ-registered seller. The dossier's 1.6% remains [knowledge, unverified].

I use 7% as the base case. The sensitivities are about +€3/unit if the 5% rate applies, and **about −€8/unit on lens kits** if they are charged at 12%.

## 7. Fatal flaws

Nothing here loses serious money. Two economic problems disqualify it against the brief ("actually make money" and asymmetry at 10–15 h/week):

1. **It is paid labour at about minimum wage.**
   - Every unit needs 2–4 h of skilled handwork plus sourcing.
   - Realised nets are €28–49, and 2 of the 7 variants in §4a do not pay at all.
   - There is no leverage: one more € of profit takes the same hours again.
   - The best-€/h line (bare CZJ/Meyer glass at ≈ €18/h) is also the most supply-limited in CZ, and German private sellers set its price (demand review).
   - The 2026 CZ minimum wage is ≈ 22,400 CZK/month, **≈ €5.5/h gross** [knowledge]. The realistic after-levy rate is ≈ €5.3/h.
2. **A low ceiling that three limits enforce together.** Even with perfect execution, the business tops out at **≈ €450–500/month after levies**:
   - the time cap at ~20 units a month;
   - the social-insurance dead zone (profit of €400–505/month buys nothing extra);
   - the €10k EU distance-sales threshold. At 12 units × ≈ €135 × ~80% non-CZ ≈ €15k/yr in year 2, destination VAT (19/119 of the price ≈ €21/unit in DE) would wipe out most of the ≈ €37 contribution. §90 would cost €10–17/unit. Only the EU SME cross-border exemption keeps it cheap (§8).

## 10. Would you tell a friend to do this?

**Only if** they already shoot film or like fixing mechanical things, so that ≈ €5–9/h after levies works as a paid hobby. They should also:
- run the free sold-comp and buy-side checks first (the demand review's gates);
- start with the fixed plan: bare CZJ/Meyer glass and mechanical SLRs to eBay.de, electronic compacts in CZ only, no DE WEEE registration;
- cap stock at €1,500–2,000;
- stop at day 90 if logged profit ÷ all hours is under €8/h.

**As the brief's "actually makes money, asymmetric" side business: no.** The market is liquid and the downside is small. But once fees, postage from CZ, compliance costs and ~3.5 h per camera are counted, the spread pays about the minimum wage, and it cannot get past ≈ €500/month after levies within 15 h/week.
