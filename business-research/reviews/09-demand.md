# Review 09, DEMAND lens: A2 permanent-residence exam prep pack (C08)

Reviewer lens: demand and competition skeptic. Date: 2026-09-29.
Tags:
- **[verified: URL]**: a web-search snippet (the dossier's, the orchestrator's, or the one search I ran this session).
- **[knowledge]**: my background knowledge, not checked this session.
- **[estimate]**: my own arithmetic, with the reasoning shown.

---

## Verdict: **WEAK, score 3/10**

- **Demand for exam *prep* is real, forced and growing, but demand does not exceed supply.**
  - The scarce resource is **exam seats** (5–6 month waits). The founder can't sell those.
  - **Prep material is abundant, and the best of it is free and official.**
- **There is no evidence that anyone sells a self-study pack in volume.** Every price point in the dossier is an asking price, and every proven sale is a *service* (a course or a lesson), which the brief excludes.
- **The "new format" gap has largely closed already, and the official side is still adding material.** This session's search found a free **interactive online new-format model test** on the official portal, on top of the 2 PDF model tests and the handbook.
- **The founder's edge is only being a native Czech speaker.** The natural winners here are bilingual UA/VI teachers of Czech and schools, who already have both the language pair and the community trust. The founder would be a late, outside entrant into closed communities.
- **Realistic month-12 profit: about €270/month before levies, not €700.** That run-rate (about 18 sales/month) would fail the dossier's own day-90 kill rule, so the most likely real outcome is a freeze, with €50–150/month passive.

## 2. Refuting "demand exceeds supply"

**The dossier confuses two different scarcities.**
- **Exam capacity** is scarce: 5–6 month waits, and capacity is at its maximum. This is a state bottleneck, and no prep product can monetise it.
- **Prep supply** is plentiful. I count **at least 20 named suppliers** across the dossier, the orchestrator's verification and this session's search.

| Tier | Suppliers | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Official, free** | NPI/cestina-pro-cizince.cz: 2 new-format model tests + an interactive online new-format test + a handbook + audio, also in Ukrainian | Free |
| **Digital, free or freemium** | CzechReady (free reading and listening; AI-graded writing and speaking in premium), cestinaexam.cz, YouTube playlists, teacher blogs (e.g. jitkapourova.cz, found this session), integration-centre materials | Free / freemium |
| **Digital, paid** | ExamOnline (1,900 Kč), a2zkouska.cz (price unknown), studyfun.cz, Autentická Čeština (Patreon) | Mostly unknown |
| **General AI** | ChatGPT, Gemini and similar: unlimited A2 writing prompts, corrections and voice conversation practice | Free / €20 subscription [knowledge] |
| **Courses (service)** | ICJ, EduJoy, UJOP, Solaris, Language Atelier, JC Correct, SJS, MUNI CJV, KCV JŠ, ÚBN FF UK (found this session), plus Vietnamese-community in-language courses [knowledge] | 1,580–5,400 Kč |
| **Grey** | Telegram-shared PDFs of paid materials; "real recalled questions" (Patreon) | Free / cheap |

**Mapping the dossier's three residual gaps against this supply:**
1. **More new-format mocks.**
   - Partly real: only 2 official *full* new-format mocks plus the interactive test.
   - But it matters only for writing and speaking, since reading and listening are unchanged.
   - Any teacher, school or CzechReady can add mocks cheaply, and schools with courses need them anyway.
2. **UA/VI explanations.**
   - Real, but the people best placed to supply them are bilingual UA/VI teachers of Czech, many of whom already run courses and channels.
   - A native-Czech founder paying freelance proofreaders is structurally worse placed than them.
3. **Speaking drill audio for the 2×4 format.**
   - Real but thin. Free AI voice mode and YouTube walkthroughs are good-enough substitutes for most A2 candidates [knowledge].

**Conclusion.** Demand for prep is broad, but the product-shaped gap is narrow and closing. It fails the brief's core test ("demand clearly bigger than supply ... sellers who can't keep up"). **Nobody here is failing to keep up.**

## 3. Sold prices vs asking prices

- **Sold data: none.** No sales counts, review counts, subscriber counts, or Gumroad or app-store ratings exist for any A2 self-study product. The one thing that would have moved my score, CzechReady's premium price and user count, is still unknown (dossier §15, claim 1, unverified).
- **Asking prices:** ExamOnline 1,900 Kč. That is **probably old-format** ("new questions from 2023") [verified: examonline.cz], so it's a stale anchor, not proof of what a new-format pack sells for.
- **Proven sales exist only for services** (courses and consultations). The brief rules those out.
- **The Patreon "real questions" model is a demand signal that cuts against the dossier.** What buyers seem to pay for is *leaked, real* items, which the founder legally cannot and should not offer. A clean original mock competes against a grey product that buyers perceive as better.
- **Base rate** [verified: hunt/base-rates.md, citing insightraider.com/en/state-of-gumroad-2026]: the median Gumroad creator makes ≈ $72/month, and under 5% make more than $1k/month. Forced demand lifts this idea above the generic info-product base rate. The absence of any sold evidence keeps it well below "proven".

## 5. Trend: is it rising or fading?

- **Buyer flow is rising.** About 2,500 passed in 2020 and 12,000+ in 2024 [verified: msmt.gov.cz]. But growth is **capped by exam capacity**, not by demand, so the 2025–27 flow is roughly flat at capacity (≈14–16k/yr) [estimate].
- **The value of paid prep content is falling.**
  - The free official offering is expanding: interactive tests and a Ukrainian UI.
  - General AI keeps getting better at A2-level writing feedback and speaking practice [knowledge].
  - CzechReady chose a freemium model, which hints that pure paid packs are hard to sell [estimate; motive unknown].
- **The nationality mix is unverified and probably less favourable than assumed.**
  - Most Ukrainians under temporary protection are **not yet eligible** for PR (the ZDP route is ≈2028+).
  - Today's UA candidates are mainly pre-2022 labour migrants.
  - The Vietnamese community is long-established, with many members already holding PR, and it has its own in-language prep ecosystem [knowledge].
  - The VI and RU language versions may earn less than their translation cost.
- **Net:** a stable, forced, but capacity-capped buyer pool, with a falling price ceiling for self-study content.

---

## 6. Are new entrants succeeding?

- **No evidence either way for digital entrants.** CzechReady, a2zkouska.cz, studyfun.cz and the Patreon series all exist, but none shows traction data.
- **The visible survivors are course providers.** They monetise through teaching hours and ad budgets, and they use free mocks and blog posts as lead magnets. That lead-magnet economy is exactly what makes a 590 Kč pack hard to sell: the founder's product is someone else's free funnel content.
- Survivorship caution: an absence of visible sellers of a thing can mean a gap, or it can mean the thing doesn't sell. With free official material this close to the product, I read it as the latter until a pre-sale proves otherwise.

**About 15 A2 + 3 B1 sales/month, or ≈1.2% of candidates. The dossier assumes 2.5% (32 + 8).**

Net per sale:
- Lower organic reach pushes the paid share of sales to ~55% (CAC 363 Kč incl. reverse-charge VAT) and the Complete (B) share down to ~30%.
- **Blended A2 net ≈ 405 Kč ≈ €16.5** [estimate: A = 590 − 18 − 18 − 30 − 200 = 324 Kč; B = 890 − 24 − 27 − 45 − 200 = 594 Kč; blended at 70/30].

**Month-12 run-rate:**
- 15 × €16.5 + 3 × €24 − €50 fixed costs ≈ **€270/month before levies**.
- About €225 after levies (≈8.7% of ≈€510 revenue).
- **That is below the dossier's own day-90 kill threshold (≥25 sales/month).**

## 8. Fatal flaws (demand lens)

1. **No supply gap.** The brief requires demand clearly bigger than supply. Here supply is abundant, much of it free and official, and still expanding. The asymmetry is ≈2/10, not the dossier's 4/10.
2. **There is no evidence that anyone buys this product type.** All the money-changing-hands evidence is for services, and the one paid pack found is probably stale and has no visible volume. This matches the founder's documented failure mode ("sounded good, didn't sell"), which scorer A flagged.
3. **The founder has no distribution edge, and plausible competitors do.**
   - The buyers sit in closed UA/VI/RU communities, often gated by agencies and schools that sell competing courses.
   - A native-Czech outsider pays for trust that bilingual teachers already own.
   - This is fatal **unless** the founder has pre-existing trusted access, such as an admin role, a partner inside a community, or an employer channel.

## 9. Fixable issues, with fixes

| Issue | Fix |
|---|---|
| The €1,000 stage-1 spend comes before any demand signal | Run a **€0–100, ~20 h smoke test first**: a lead magnet ("New 2026 writing and speaking: 3 templates + 1 drill") in UA and EN, a landing page with a *priced* pre-order button at **590 Kč, not 390 Kč**, and 5 admin-approved community posts. Proceed to stage 1 only if you get ≥200 organic sign-ups and ≥10 real pre-orders in 2 weeks. |
| The price test at 390 Kč flatters demand | Test at the real price. A 390 Kč "Starter" that converts doesn't prove 590/890 will. |
| The scope targets a gap that closed (the full 8-mock pack) | Cut to **what changed**: a new-format writing and speaking kit (templates, model answers, a drill audio bank) at 290–390 Kč, built in ~30–40 h. Point candidates to the free official mocks for reading and listening instead of competing with them. |
| Distribution is assumed, not secured | Before building, sign **one** distribution partner: a school, agency or community admin who agrees in writing to promote the product, or to license the mock bank as white-label course material (a product licence, not a service). No partner means no build. |
| Translating into 4 languages up front | Start with UA + EN only. Add VI or RU only if sign-up data shows demand. |
| Kill rules come too late | Move the gate earlier. If week-2 smoke-test pre-orders are < 10, stop at ≈€100 and ≈20 h sunk, instead of ≈€1,000 and ≈60 h. |
| The B1 + reálie upsell is assumed | Drop it from the projections unless the A2 cohort explicitly asks for it. |

## 11. Would you tell a friend to do this?

**No, unless** the friend already has trusted access to a UA (or VI) community of PR applicants: an admin seat, a teacher partner, or an employer or agency that will push it. Even then, only as the €100 / 20-hour smoke test above, at full price, before any build. Without that access, this is a well-researched version of the "clever digital product that didn't sell" the founder has already lived through.
