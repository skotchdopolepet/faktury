# Review 09: A2 permanent-residence exam prep pack (C08). EXECUTION lens

Reviewer lens: execution, legal load and platform risk (CZ). Date: 2026-09-29.
Read: `00-brief.md`, `dossiers/09-a2-exam-prep.md`, `02-main-loop-evidence.md`, `06-verification.md`, `cz-legal-tax-quickstart.md` (§0–3, §5–6), `scores/scorer-A/B/C.md`, `01-longlist.md` (C08 block, traps), `hunt/demand-signals.md` and `hunt/cz-eu-edge.md` (C08 origin).
Web: I ran 1 WebSearch, on the policy-change risk (results below). Other claims are tagged **[verified: URL]** (from the files), **[knowledge]** or **[estimate]**.

---

## Verdict: **WEAK, 4/10**

On the legal and platform axes this is the cleanest candidate on the shortlist. It has no goods, so none of GPSR, WEEE, EPR, hallmarking, AML, US tariffs or Etsy suspensions apply. The cash at risk to test it is small (≤ €1,000).

It fails on **executability by this founder**. The plan assumes three professional skills the brief never grants:
1. Writing test items calibrated to CEFR A2. Being a native Czech speaker is not the same thing.
2. Building a small web app (auto-scoring, streamed audio, in-browser recording, per-buyer watermarking) in 20 h.
3. Trusted access to closed Ukrainian and Vietnamese communities.

On top of that the plan runs 6–7 contractors across 4 languages. The 12-week plan is overbooked by about 40–60% at 10–15 h/week, and its gates are timed inconsistently.

Corrected realistic M12: about **€320/month before levies (≈ €265 after)**, conditional on passing the stage-1 gate. The fully loaded return over 24 months is about **€3/hour**, which is below the Czech minimum wage.

## 2. Can a normal employed person execute the 12-week plan? **No, not as written.**

| Phase | Dossier hours | Realistic hours | Weeks at 12.5 h/week |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stage 1: study, lead magnet ×5 languages, landing, checkout, legal docs, contractor hiring, pilot mock, templates, drills, first outreach | ~40 (MVP) + ~14 (spec study, landing) | **65–75** | 5–6 (pre-sale opens in **week 6**, not week 4) |
| Weeks 5–12 in the plan: mocks 2–4, web-app scoring and watermarking, phonetics drills, Anki, full proofreading coordination, 5 SEO articles ×5 languages, affiliate setup, 3 short videos, ads tuning, support, testimonials, B1 outline, VAT/admin | not totalled; implied ~100 | **140–160** | 11–13 |
| **12-week total** | ~150 (fits by construction) | **≈ 210–235** | **≈ 17–19 weeks** |

In short, the plan needs **17–20 weeks**, or 18–20 h/week, to hit its milestones. The build is also not the only load: at the same time the founder has to manage 6–7 contractors (2–3 voices, 1 teacher, UA/RU/VI proofreaders), each with a licence or performer's-rights contract. Recording sessions with drama students must fit around a day job.

**Skill ramp (the real bottleneck):**
- **Item writing.** Native speakers write reading texts and dialogues that are too long, too idiomatic and too fast for A2. Distractor design and level control are learned skills. The dossier's mitigation (3 h of teacher review per mock) catches errors but doesn't teach the craft. Expect 2–3 rewrite cycles on mock 1.
- **Phonetics drills for the new 3-point criterion.** This is a CzFL-teacher or phonetician's job, not a layperson's.
- **Audio production.** Editing multi-voice dialogues to exam-like quality in Audacity is learnable, but the first mock's audio realistically takes 8–10 h, not 4.
- **Marketing in 4–5 languages** to communities the founder is not part of is the hardest ramp. It depends on AI translation plus people the founder can't quality-check, especially for Vietnamese.

## 4. Fatal flaws

1. **No founder–market access to the buyers.** Nothing in the brief says the founder has a foothold in UA or VI communities. Without a trusted insider, the channels in order are: admins who say no, ads that are either restricted (Meta) or too expensive per sale (Google at a 590 Kč ticket), and SEO that takes 6–12 months. This is fatal unless it is fixed *before* week 1 (see F1). It is the same "clever idea without distribution" profile that the brief says has already burned this founder.
2. **The plan depends on skills the founder probably lacks and doesn't budget to buy:** CzFL item writing, a web-app build and phonetics. Each alone is fixable with money (F3, F4). Together, and un-budgeted, they make the stated 12-week, 155-hour plan infeasible.

Neither is a *legal* or *platform* flaw. On those axes this idea is unusually safe.

## 5. Fixable issues and fixes

| # | Issue | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | No community access | **Sign 1–2 native ambassadors before building anything.** Look for a UA and/or VI person who already admins a group or runs a channel. Offer 30–40% revenue share on a coupon code, since 25% is too thin. Also get written "yes, you may post" from 5 admins. If neither happens in 2 weeks, stop at €0 spent. |
| F2 | Pre-sale legal and exam-date exposure | Sell the **Starter kit as an immediately delivered product**: mock 1, writing templates and speaking drills on day 1, with mocks 2–4 as "free updates as released, target date X". The withdrawal waiver then applies, nobody's exam date is left hanging, and the refund promise is limited to undelivered items. |
| F3 | Item-writing skill ramp | **Buy it.** Commission the mocks from a CzFL lektor (AUČCJ, UJOP, MUNI) on a work-for-hire/exclusive-licence contract. Budget about 6,000–8,000 Kč per mock (≈ €250–330) × 4 in stage 2, ≈ €1,200, which still fits under €8,500. The founder keeps production, audio and distribution. This cuts ~50 build hours and most of the quality risk. |
| F4 | Web app in 20 h | For V1, **drop record-and-compare, the date-driven email plan and custom auto-scoring.** Use a Czech checkout plus membership tool (SimpleShop/FAPI) with a quiz plugin, streamed audio and stamped PDFs. Build the web app only after the day-90 gate, and only if the founder codes. Budget 40 h, not 20. |
| F5 | Four explanation languages at launch | **Launch in UA + EN only.** UA is the largest cohort, and many Ukrainians also read Russian. Add VI only when a VI ambassador (F1) exists, and RU only if demand appears. This cuts proofreaders from 3 to 1 and removes ~40% of the coordination load. |
| F6 | Gates mis-timed | Re-index every gate to calendar weeks from the actual pre-sale date (e.g. pre-sale in week 6, so day 30 = week 10, day 60 = week 14, day 90 = week 19). Put the dates in the calendar on day 1. |
| F7 | CAC unmeasurable | Give every group, ambassador and affiliate its own coupon code; use UTM links for ads and SEO. Judge channels on at least 10 sales each, not on a €124 test. |
| F8 | Ad-policy risk | On Meta, frame ads as a "Czech language exam course", never "immigration" or "residence permit", or skip Meta. On Google, never imply official status or registration ("not affiliated with NPI ČR" on the landing page). Bid on exam-prep terms, not "registrace" terms. |
| F9 | Contractor admin and VAT | Prefer contractors who invoice with an IČO. Paying students on a DPP makes you an employer with monthly ČSSZ reporting [knowledge], so use a licence or performer's-rights agreement instead. Budget +21% on any foreign freelancer, SaaS or AI tool (identified person). |
| F10 | Expense method | Keep actual-expense records (daňová evidence) for the build year; switch to the 60% paušál once revenue outgrows costs. Spend one paid hour with a daňový poradce (quickstart §6). |
| F11 | Digital-content obligations | Put the access period and update policy in the T&Cs. Process speaking recordings **locally in the browser only**, so no voice data is stored (GDPR). If TTS or AI images are used, add a short disclosure (AI Act Art. 50 transparency applies from Aug 2026) [knowledge]. |
| F12 | Service creep | Write canned replies in all languages: "no individual correction; see the rubric; for a tutor, here is partner school X (affiliate)". Hold the line: the brief excludes services. |

## 6. Corrected projections (month 12, monthly) and €/hour

Assumptions [estimate]:
- Launch slips about 6 weeks.
- The channel mix leans more on paid than the dossier assumes, so blended net is ~€17 per A2 sale (the dossier has €19).
- The B1 pack ships around month 9–10.
- Fixed costs are €55/month.
- Levies are 8.7% of revenue (paušál from year 2).

| Scenario | M12 sales/month | M12 profit before levies | After levies | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pessimistic | Killed at the day-30/60 gate; passive trickle of 2–4 sales | **€0–40** | €0–35 | Most likely single branch (40–50%) |
| **Realistic** (conditional on passing stage 1) | 18 A2 + 3 B1 | **≈ €320** | **≈ €265** | ~1.4% of ~1,250 candidates a month |
| Optimistic | 50 A2 + 12 B1 + occasional B2B | **≈ €1,150** | ≈ €1,000 | Requires F1 to work well. The dossier's €2,300 (75 + 25 sales, ~6% share for a part-time newcomer) is not credible. |

The probability-weighted M12 is only about €150–200/month.

**€/hour:**
- **Run phase at M12:** €320 ÷ ~32 h/month (7–8 h/week) ≈ **€10/h before levies (≈ €8–9/h after).**
- **Fully loaded over 24 months:** the realistic ramp (≈ €0 → €100 → €200 → €300 → €320) sums to about €5,600 before levies. Subtract ~€3,000 of one-off cash (voices, review, proofreading at the corrected cost, mic, B1 production) and levies, and about **€2,200 net** remains. Hours: ~230 (A2 build) + ~60 (B1) + ~560 (run) ≈ 850. That gives **≈ €2.6–3/h**, below the Czech 2026 minimum wage of ~134 Kč/h ≈ €5.5/h [knowledge].
- **Ceiling:** about €1,000–1,500/month at 10–15 h/week. The candidate pool (~1,250 a month) and the free substitutes cap it. Growth past that needs a school or agency partnership, which drifts toward a service.

## 7. Would I tell a friend to do this?

**Only if** all three of these hold:
1. The friend is a native Czech speaker **and** either has CzFL teaching experience or pays a lektor to write the items (F3).
2. They already have, or sign before spending anything, a trusted **insider in the UA (or VI) community** who will sell for them (F1).
3. They run it strictly as the **≤ €1,000, ~70-hour Starter-kit test** with honest, correctly dated gates (F6), as a secondary bet next to a resale candidate, not as the main project.

Otherwise, **no**. Legally it is the easiest idea on the list, but it is a distribution and skill problem dressed as a content project. The founder's realistic return is about €3/h fully loaded for about a year of evenings.
