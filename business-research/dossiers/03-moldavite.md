## 1. Verdict in 3 lines

- **Conditional go, but only as a small add-on line (ideally in the same Etsy shop as C02). It is a no-go as a standalone business.** Run a ≤€1,000 test and scale only if the day-60 gate passes.
- **Realistic month-12 profit: about €360/month before tax and levies** (about €280 after roughly 22% levies), at about 7 h/week, which is roughly €11–12 per hour.
- **Biggest risk: the headline spread (€4–8/g finder price against $50–100/g US retail) is not available to a legal newcomer.** Moldavite is a state-owned reserved mineral, and cheap "direct from finder" supply is largely illegally dug. Clean, invoiced supply costs 250–1,000 CZK/g, so the spread you can actually capture is about 2×, not 10×.

## 2. The exact offer

"Verifiable, not just certified" Czech moldavite. Every stone gets:
- a public ID number;
- weight and specific gravity (SG) measured in-house;
- a micrograph of its bubbles and lechatelierite inclusions;
- its locality, with the supplier's invoice reference on file;
- a 60-day guarantee: if an accredited lab says it isn't moldavite, the buyer gets a full refund plus up to €50 of the lab fee.

Descriptions stay geology-only (a tektite from the Ries impact, about 15 million years old [knowledge]), with **no metaphysical or healing claims** (see §11).

## 4. Supply gap, asymmetry and the newcomer's edge

**Geology creates a real and durable supply limit.**
- Moldavite is found essentially only in South Bohemia and Moravia [knowledge].
- It is a **reserved mineral (vyhrazený nerost)**, and its natural deposits are **state property**, extractable only by authorised mining [verified: https://www.mallorn.cz/news/legislativa-mineralogicke-cinnosti/, https://cnn.iprima.cz/kopace-vltavinu-neodradi-pokuty-ani-hrozba-vezeni-policiste-jich-letos-chytili-pres-tricet-414224].
- The Besednice deposit is a natural monument where "any ground intervention is prohibited", and a court blocked exploration there [verified: https://moderniobec.cz/besednicke-vltaviny-neuzavreny-problem-jiznich-cech/, https://ceskokrumlovsky.denik.cz/zpravy-region/vltaviny-tezba-kopaci-besednice/].
- Legal extraction happens at the Chlum sand pit and in the museum-run "experiential mining" near Besednice [verified: iprima URL above].

**The limit protects incumbents, not a newcomer.** The finder-to-dealer channel (buyback at 100–200 CZK/g) belongs to established dealers: topvltaviny.cz, vltaviny-obchod.cz, topminerals.cz and Granát Turnov [verified: 02-main-loop-evidence URLs]. Much finder supply is illegally dug:
- 45 illegal diggers caught in 3.5 months, with 600,000 CZK in fines [verified: https://ceskokrumlovsky.denik.cz/zpravy-region/vltaviny-kopaci-pokuta-besednice-netolice-policie/];
- more diggers caught in July 2026 [verified: https://www.novinykraje.cz/2026/07/31/hon-za-vltaviny-zanechava-skody-policiste-pristihli-dalsi-kopace];
- Seznam Zprávy asks why "the state can't stop the leaks from the moldavite business" [verified title: https://www.seznamzpravy.cz/clanek/zelene-jihoceske-prokleti-proc-stat-nezastavi-uniky-z-kseftu-s-vltaviny-74560].

**A newcomer's honest edge is therefore narrow:**
1. **Against non-EU sellers** (US resellers, Asian fakes) selling to EU buyers: EU-internal shipping with no import VAT and no €3 EU low-value duty (from 1 Jul 2026 [verified: https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/12/12/customs-council-agrees-to-levy-customs-duty-on-small-parcels-as-of-1-july-2026/]), plus German-language listings. This edge is real but modest.
2. **Against CZ incumbents:** a channel gap on Etsy. The biggest CZ-resident Etsy shop found has only **533 sales** [verified: https://moldavitestones.etsy.com/], and Etsy demand is mostly served by US "American Seller" resellers [verified: https://www.etsy.com/listing/1340419357/]. Execution adds per-stone verifiable data, which beats paper certificates that fakers also print [verified: https://ancientenergy.com/blogs/knowledge-center/real-vs-fake-moldavite-complete-guide].
3. **Durability:** the geology lasts for years, but **the newcomer's edge can be copied in a weekend** by any incumbent that opens an Etsy shop. The small size of existing CZ Etsy shops also signals limited demand.

## 7. Sourcing plan in CZ

- **Where:**
  - IČO-registered dealers in South Bohemia (Český Krumlov and České Budějovice area) that buy from the Chlum operator;
  - wholesale packs from naturshop.cz and similar;
  - mineral fairs (burzy) in Prague, Brno and Turnov [knowledge; check dates];
  - old estate mineral collections via Aukro (C02 sourcing overlap);
  - Moravian material, which costs about twice as much [verified: 02-main-loop-evidence].
- **Target prices:**
  - 300–450 CZK/g for 0.5–2 g grade B+ with an invoice;
  - 500–700 CZK/g for grade-A 2–5 g, against about 940 CZK/g at retail;
  - **do not plan on 100–200 CZK/g.** That price is reachable only by buying from diggers.
- **Rules for every purchase:**
  1. an invoice with IČO, locality and weight;
  2. a signed declaration of legal origin;
  3. no anonymous "fresh from the field" Bazoš lots;
  4. every stone tested (SG + loupe);
  5. the first lot sample-checked at ČGL.
- **Realistic volume:** 30–60 g/month (30–50 stones). **Demand, not supply, is the constraint.**

**Proving authenticity.** Certificates alone prove nothing, because fakers print them too.
- **In-house tests:**
  - SG by hydrostatic weighing on a 0.01 g scale: moldavite ≈2.32–2.38, against ≈2.45–2.55 for common soda-lime fakes [knowledge];
  - 20–40× inclusions: round or elongated bubbles and **lechatelierite "worms"**, which are strongly diagnostic [knowledge];
  - natural etched sculpture rather than repeated moulded patterns [verified: https://www.granat-shop.cz/blog/jak-poznat-pravy-vltavin--5-testu--ktere-zvladnete-doma/].
- **UV is not diagnostic**, so drop the longlist's "UV photo" [knowledge].
- **Independent lab:** the Česká gemologická laboratoř (ČGL) charges **800–2,500 CZK** for an expertise (worthwhile for stones over 5,000 CZK), and a basic identity check costs "hundreds of CZK" [verified: https://www.eppi.cz/napoveda/drahokamy/vltavin].
- **Calibration:** buy 2–3 known fakes as negatives.

## 11. CZ/EU legal checklist

| Topic | What applies |
|---|---|
| **Živnost** | Volná "Výroba, obchod a služby…", field *Velkoobchod a maloobchod*; 800 CZK online (quickstart §1). **Do not set stones in silver, or wire-wrap in silver, yourself**: that is the řemeslná licence *Zlatnictví a klenotnictví* [verified: quickstart §1.2; wire-wrap is my interpretation]. Stainless-steel "cage" pendants are volná |

| **Hallmarking** | **Notify the Puncovní úřad before selling any silver.** Silver over 3 g needs a hallmark; under 3 g needs a fineness mark plus a responsibility mark; receipts must state type, price, weight and fineness [verified: 02-main-loop-evidence, http://www.puncovniurad.cz/cz/faq.aspx]. Keep P1 settings under 3 g. UK: silver over 7.78 g needs a UK hallmark |

## 12. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Illegal-origin supply** (the biggest reputational and legal exposure) | Buy only from IČO-registered sellers with invoices and origin declarations; no digger or anonymous Bazoš lots |
| Buying fakes | Test 100% (SG + inclusions); ČGL for the first lot and for grade A; keep reference fakes |
| The real spread is only about 2× | Push R2/P1 and multi-stone orders; drop singles under 0.8 g |
| Fad softness at the low end | Stay in EU geology/collector positioning; keep stock small and turn it fast |
| "It's fake" disputes and chargebacks | Public per-stone data, a lab-refund guarantee, a 5% allowance |
| US friction (duty, fees, refusals) | US only for items over €100, prepaid DDP |
| Etsy policy (claims, creativity standards) | Geology-only copy; list as handpicked craft supply; mirror the listings on eBay.de |
| Liquidation at buyback (≈30–50% of cost) | Cap stock at 2–3 months of sales |
| Incumbents copy the format | Accept it; this is an add-on line, not a moat |
