## 3. Why it makes money (demand evidence)
- **Mandatory, capacity-bound flow of buyers.**
  - 2023: 8,819 candidates sat 9,441 exams; 86.2% passed within the year and 80.5% of sittings passed. [verified: https://www.auccj.cz/zkouska-z-cestiny-pro-trvaly-pobyt-aktualni-podoba-a-moznosti-pripravy-uchazecu/]
  - 2024: more than 12,000 people passed. The Interior Ministry expected up to 30,000 permanent-residence applications in 2025, and capacity was set to rise by about 50%. [verified: https://msmt.gov.cz/zajem-o-zkousku-z-cestiny-pro-trvaly-pobyt-roste-stat-jiz]
  - Waits are about 5–6 months, and capacity "appears to have reached its maximum". [verified: https://registrace.cestina-pro-cizince.cz/, https://www.npi.cz/aktuality/79098-byla-vytvorena-online-registrace-ke-zkousce-z-cestiny-pro-trvaly-pobyt]
  - **Estimate: 14,000–18,000 candidates a year, or about 1,200–1,500 a month.** Reasoning: 12,000 passes ÷ 0.86 ≈ 14,000 candidates in 2024, plus the capacity increase.
- **Failing is expensive, which raises willingness to pay.**
  - The fee is **3,200 Kč**, and since the end of 2023 there is **no free first attempt** (the state vouchers ended). Candidates get at most 3 attempts per calendar year. [verified: https://ct24.ceskatelevize.cz/clanek/domaci/zadatele-o-trvaly-pobyt-si-zaplati-uz-prvni-pokus-zkousky-z-cestiny-ta-podrazi-1962, https://blog.zamestnavamecizince.cz/trvaly-pobyt-pro-vase-zamestnance-z-ciziny-jazykova-zkouska-zdarma-konci-v-prosinci-2023/]
  - **This refutes scorer C's assumption that the state pays for the first attempt.**
  - A fail costs 3,200 Kč plus another months-long wait. A 590 Kč pack is under 20% of one re-sit.
- **The pain sits exactly where the product focuses.**
  - Writing is the weakest skill, averaging **65.9%**, against reading 82.8%, listening 86.9% and speaking 77.2%. [verified: auccj.cz, above]
  - The 11 Apr 2026 change touched **only writing and speaking**: new task structure and point distribution. Speaking now has 2 topics × 4 questions instead of 1 topic × 10 questions, plus a 3-point phonetics criterion. [verified: https://cestina-pro-cizince.cz/trvaly-pobyt/zmena-zkousky-a2/, https://czechbuddy.cz/new-format-of-the-a2-exam-for-permanent-residency-2026/, https://aroundprague.cz/en/guides/czech-language-exam-for-permanent-residency-a2-new-format-from-11-april-2026]
- **People already pay for prep:**
  - **ExamOnline sells a digital pack (3 sample tests + audio + transcripts) for 1,900 Kč.** [verified: https://examonline.cz/en/cestina-trvaly-pobyt/]
  - CzechReady puts writing, speaking and full mocks behind a **premium plan**. [verified: https://czechready.cz/]
  - EduJoy sells a 12-hour hybrid course at 3,990 Kč (2,990 Kč early-bird). [verified: https://edujoy.cz/ua/ (UA blog page on the A2 prep course)]
  - ICJ charges 5,400 Kč for 40 h. [verified: https://icj.cz/en/preparation-for-the-a2-exam-for-permanent-residence-new-format-2026/]
  - UJOP charges 1,580 Kč for 90 min. [verified: https://ujop.cuni.cz/UJOPEN-74.html?ujopcmsid=68:individual-preparation-for-the-exam-for-permanent-residence-applicants]
  - **What's still missing:** no sales counts for any digital pack. Scorer A's objection that "nobody has shown a sale of a self-study pack" is only half answered: paid packs *exist*, but their volume is unproven. The stage-1 pre-sale exists to measure it.
- **Durability of demand.**
  - Ukrainians on the new special long-term residence (ZDP) can apply for permanent residence after 5 years. ZDP time counts in full and earlier temporary protection at half its length. [verified: https://ipc.gov.cz/zvlastni-dlouhodoby-pobyt/, https://www.pravniprostor.cz/clanky/spravni-pravo/prodlouzeni-docasne-ochrany-zvlastni-dlouhodoby-pobyt-obcanu-ukrajiny]
  - [estimate] A large refugee cohort becomes eligible around 2028–2030, so demand grows after the 12-month window.

[verified: https://ct24.ceskatelevize.cz/clanek/domaci/zajem-o-zkousky-z-cestiny-byl-loni-rekordni-jsou-treba-pro-zisk-obcanstvi-360097]

## 4. Supply gap and asymmetry (honest read: weak to moderate)
- **The "format change" gap is smaller than the longlist implied.**
  - Total points and the 60% pass thresholds are unchanged; only the internal structure of writing and speaking changed. [verified: cestina-pro-cizince.cz, above]
  - Old reading and listening material still works.
  - The official side already covers the change: **two free new-format model tests with answer sheets and keys** (Oct and Dec 2025). [verified: https://www.npi.cz/images/isbn/978-80-7578-169-7.pdf, https://www.npi.cz/images/isbn/978-80-7578-175-8.pdf]
  - The NPI "učební materiál" handbook (ISBN …-176-5) carries the ISBN right after model test 2, so **it is probably also a new-format edition** [estimate: ISBN sequence]. That contradicts the hunter's "2022 old format" claim.
  - The official site is also in Ukrainian. [verified: https://cestina-pro-cizince.cz/?hl=uk_UA]
- **What remains as a gap:**
  - **Volume:** only 2 official new-format mocks exist, and people want 6–10.
  - **Explanations in UA and VI** of *why* an answer is right, plus writing model answers in the new task types.
  - **Structured speaking drill audio** for the 2×4 format and phonetics.
- **Why others can't copy it instantly:** they can, fairly easily. Any Czech teacher or school can produce the same pack. The only frictions:
  - multi-voice native audio;
  - translation into 4 languages;
  - **trust inside closed communities**, which accrues to whoever gets there first and gathers testimonials.
- **Durability:**
  - The format lasts about 5 years (the previous change was 2021; this one 2026). A built pack therefore stays sellable until about 2030 with minor updates.
  - The *competitive* gap may close within 6–12 months, as NPI adds free material and CzechReady and schools expand.
  - Asymmetry score: **4/10**. The upside is a cheap test with a long product life, not scarcity.

## 7. Production plan in CZ (a one-off build; no inventory)
- **Items.** The founder writes them to the official A2 specification and the 2 official mocks' task types, with original texts and pictures.
  - A **qualified teacher of Czech as a foreign language** reviews the difficulty of each mock: about 3 h per mock at 500–700 Kč/h [estimate: typical CZ lektor rates, knowledge].
  - Find reviewers through AUČCJ members or UJOP/MUNI lecturers.
- **Audio: yes, native voices are needed, as a one-off cost.**
  - Listening tasks are dialogues, announcements and messages, so they need 3–4 distinct native voices: both sexes, different ages.
  - Record the founder plus 2–3 paid friends or drama students (DAMU/JAMU/conservatoires) at 500–1,000 Kč/h on a USB condenser mic (about €100–150) in a furnished room. Edit in Audacity or Reaper.
  - Budget: about 2 h of finished audio for 8 mocks ≈ 8–10 h of sessions ≈ **€300–500 in total** [estimate].
  - Get a **signed performer's licence** from every voice (performers' rights, Czech Copyright Act 121/2000 Sb.) [knowledge].
  - Neural TTS (Azure cs-CZ, ElevenLabs) is acceptable for vocabulary and Anki audio only. Dialogues sound flat and buyers notice [knowledge].
- **Pictures for the speaking tasks:** your own photos, licensed stock, or AI images under a tool's commercial terms. Never the official pictures.
- **Translations:**
  - Draft the explanations (about 6,000–8,000 words) with AI.
  - Have native UA, RU and VI proofreaders edit them at about 150–250 Kč per standard page (normostrana) [estimate]. That comes to about €250 per language.
  - Take a licence assignment in every contract.
- **Platform:**
  - If the founder codes: a static web app with auth and Stripe.
  - If not: a Czech checkout and member area (SimpleShop or FAPI + WordPress). Czech SaaS avoids some identified-person triggers [knowledge; verify prices].
- **Throughput:** about 1 mock a week at 12–15 h/week.

## 11. CZ/EU legal checklist
- **Živnost:**
  - Volná, with the fields "Nakladatelské činnosti…" (publishing) and "Poskytování software…" (software) [quickstart §1.1].
  - Don't tick "Mimoškolní výchova a vzdělávání, pořádání kurzů" (courses); you don't run courses.
  - Use the 60% flat-rate expenses (paušál).
- **VAT:**
  - Buyers live in CZ, so the place of supply for e-services is CZ. As a non-payer you charge no VAT until 2M Kč turnover. OSS isn't relevant [quickstart §3.1–3.5].
  - Google Ads, Meta Ads and foreign SaaS make you an **identified person**: register within 15 days, then file a monthly return with 21% VAT on those fees [quickstart §3.3].
- **Margin scheme, GPSR, EPR/packaging, WEEE, hallmarking:** not applicable to pure digital content [knowledge]. A later printed edition would need EKO-KOM packaging registration.
- **Consumer law** [quickstart §5.3]:
  - Business terms (obchodní podmínky), a complaints procedure and a GDPR notice.
  - A checkout **checkbox waiving the 14-day withdrawal right** for immediate digital delivery (§1837 l OZ), confirmed by email.
  - Add the online withdrawal function required from 19 June 2026 anyway.
  - **No "pass guarantee"**: it's misleading and invites disputes.
- **Copyright and IP:**
  - NPI model tests, the handbook, pictures and audio are protected works. Free to download does **not** mean free to reuse. Link to them; never copy items, texts, images or audio.
  - The exam *format* and task types are ideas and methods, not protected expression [knowledge]. Imitate the structure only.
  - **Don't publish "real exam questions" recalled by candidates** (the Patreon model). It risks infringement and the NPI's hostility, and it poisons trust.
  - Take licence assignments from translators, reviewers and voices (performers' rights). Use licensed images and fonts.
- **Trademark and unfair competition:** never say "official". Don't use NPI, MŠMT or UJOP logos. Describe the product as "prep for the A2 exam for permanent residence (format from 11 Apr 2026); not affiliated with NPI ČR". [knowledge]
- **Other:**
  - GDPR: a processor agreement with the email tool. If AI writing feedback is added later, disclose that texts go to a processor.
  - DAC7 applies only if you sell through platforms.
  - The EAA exemption for microenterprises applies.
