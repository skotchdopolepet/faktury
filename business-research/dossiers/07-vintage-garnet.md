## 1. Verdict in 3 lines
- **Conditional go, but only as a sub-line inside C02 (the vintage-glass picker shop), EU-first. As a standalone business it's a no-go.** The spread is real only on mispriced private listings, not on quality antique pieces at dealer prices.
- **Realistic month-12 profit: about €375/month** before income tax and health insurance (8 sales × ~€50 net, minus ~€25 fixed). Pessimistic €115, optimistic €1,095.
- **Biggest risk:** the spread is much thinner than the longlist's "2–4×". The closest like-for-like pair in the evidence is a c.1890 tombak brooch asking 5,200 CZK (€208) on Aukro against an antique pyrope brooch on Etsy at $231–260 (€196–221). That's roughly 1×. ## 2. The exact offer
One Etsy shop, shared with C02 and positioned as "Czech vintage: Bohemian glass and garnet". Every piece is ≥20 years old (Etsy's vintage rule), tested, weighed, with its hallmark photographed and translated.

| Tier | What | Price (EU buyer, free shipping) | Share of sales |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: filler | 1960s–90s Granát Turnov / družstvo pieces in gilt tombak or gilt 900 silver: brooches, earrings, small pendants | €60–110 | ~50% |
| B: core | Interwar–1950s Czech-hallmarked silver (800/835/900) brooches, bracelets and necklaces; tombak pieces from before 1920 in good condition | €150–350 | ~40% |
| C: occasional | Gold (≥585) rings and brooches with a valid Austro-Hungarian or Czechoslovak hallmark | €500–1,500 | ~10% |

- **Buyers:** EU collectors and wearers of antique jewelry in DE, AT, NL, FR and IT, and the UK via the marketplace. Also January-birthstone gift buyers and the Czech/Slovak diaspora.
- **US collectors come later, and only for items ≥ €250 shipped DDP.** The US carries a 15% tariff on EU goods, and postal service was suspended [verified: 02-main-loop-evidence "US import after the end of de minimis"; https://euperspectives.eu/2025/08/european-postal-services-suspend-parcel-shipments-to-us/].
- **Channels:**
  - Etsy Vintage is the primary channel.
  - eBay.de/.at auctions are for clearing tier A.
  - Aukro is for liquidating misbuys to Czech buyers.
  - Ruby Lane and 1stDibs are only considered after 40+ listed pieces and a working US route. Ruby Lane charges a monthly shop fee; 1stDibs vets dealers [knowledge].
- **Dropped from the hunter's plan:**
  - **Stone replacement.** It is precious-metal repair, which needs a craft (řemeslná) licence, so it is outsourced to a licensed goldsmith (zlatník) or the piece is sold "one stone missing".
  - **The ultrasonic cleaner.** It loosens pitch- or glue-set stones and ruins the foil backing in closed-back antique settings [knowledge]. Use a soft brush, lukewarm water with a drop of detergent, and isopropyl alcohol.

- **The real edge is a mispricing edge, not an origin edge.** Private sellers on Vinted CZ, Bazoš and Sbazar, auctions that close below the asks above, and mixed "po babičce" boxes regularly price pieces at 300–1,500 CZK [knowledge; the Bazoš listing page exists at https://ostatni.bazos.cz/inzeraty/cesky-granat/]. **Durability:**
- Pre-1945 supply is finite, but a century of Bohemian production means it is not rare in CZ households [knowledge].
- Mid-century pieces are continuous with Granát Turnov's current production, which it sells new on its own e-shop [verified: https://www.granat.cz/en/brooches/]. That caps tier-A prices.
- Czech media are telling households that grandmother's garnet is valuable (fajntip, 2026), so private asks will creep up.
- **Estimated durability [estimate]:** the low-end mispricing gap lasts for years but cannot scale. The quality-piece gap is effectively closed already.

**Telling Czech pyrope from glass and from other garnets (tools ≈ €120–200, skill ≈ 15 h):**

| Tool | Cost | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| 10× triplet loupe + LED penlight | €25–40 | Glass shows round gas bubbles, swirl lines, mould marks, rounded facet edges, orange-peel surface and shell-like chips. Garnet shows crisp facet junctions and natural needles or crystals [knowledge] |
| N52 neodymium magnet | €10 | Pyrope–almandine garnets "drag" (almandine strongly, Bohemian pyrope weakly to moderately). Glass and paste don't react. Test loose stones or use the pendulum method, and watch for steel pins [knowledge] |
| 0.01 g scale | €15 | Needed legally for receipts (weight and fineness), and to apply the 3 g / 0.5 g hallmark thresholds |
| Gold/silver acid kit + touchstone | €25 | Tests the metal where the hallmark is worn |
| Handheld spectroscope (optional) | €40–80 | Almandine shows strong iron lines at ~505/527/576 nm. Pyrope shows a chromium band with weaker iron lines [knowledge] |
| Reference set: 1 marked Granát piece + 1 Jablonec glass imitation | €40 | Trains the eye |

**Checks for tricky cases:**
- **Doublets** (a garnet cap on glass) show a junction line or a change of lustre at the girdle [knowledge].
- **Colour:** Czech pyrope is small (mostly under 5 mm), rose-cut and a deep "fire" red with no purple tint. Larger purplish stones or cabochons suggest almandine; modern Asian pieces suggest Indian or African garnet [knowledge].
- **Refractometers** are of little use on mounted rose cuts, because there is no flat table facet to read [knowledge].
- **How to describe:** use attribution language such as "Czech (Bohemian) pyrope garnet, attributed from maker's mark, hallmark and style; stones tested: natural garnet (magnet, loupe)". Don't claim a lab certificate you don't have.
- **Learning:** Turnov's museum (Muzeum Českého ráje) garnet exhibits, and the Puncovní úřad's official table of old recognised marks [verified: https://punc.gov.cz/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Stare-puncovni-znacky-uznavane-jako-platne-2025-V1.pdf].

## 11. CZ/EU legal checklist for this product
**1. Živnost (trade licence)**
- A volná živnost is enough to trade. Tick **"Velkoobchod a maloobchod"** and **"Zastavárenská činnost a maloobchod s použitým zbožím"** [knowledge: the field exists in the MPO list; verify].
- **No special živnost is needed to trade precious-metal jewelry** [knowledge]. Repairs, soldering and stone setting are the **řemeslná "Zlatnictví a klenotnictví"** licence [verified: quickstart §1.2, BusinessInfo], so outsource them.

**2. Puncovní úřad (Assay Office)**
- **Notification:**
  - Register as an obchodník with precious metals within **15 days** of starting (§ 35 of Act 539/1992).
  - Notify the start of sales at an establishment **or an "elektronická adresa"** (your Etsy/eBay shop URL) **at least 3 days in advance**.
  - You then appear in the public register [verified: https://portal.gov.cz/sluzby-vs/vedeni-registru-vyrobcu-a-obchodniku-s-drahymi-kovy-S1688, https://www.puncovnictvivpraxi.cz/vyrobce-a-obchodnik/23-zakladni-povinnosti-vyrobcu-a-obchodniku-se-sperky].
- **Hallmark thresholds:**
  - Gold or platinum over **0.5 g** and silver over **3 g** per piece must carry a valid hallmark. Lighter silver needs only a fineness mark [verified: 02-main-loop-evidence; https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/1992-539].
  - **Old marks count:** Austro-Hungarian (1866–1921), Czechoslovak, Protectorate and Slovak State marks are recognised as valid (for gold, down to 585/000) [verified: punc.gov.cz PDF above; https://www.puncovniurad.eu/puncovni-znacky-cz-stare-platne-zlato/]. Check silver finenesses against the same PDF.
  - **Antique pieces without a valid mark** can be submitted to the office. They are **not stamped**; instead they are "released to trade by an expert opinion (odborný posudek)" [verified: snippet of 539/1992 via https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/1992-539]. That's useful: no new stamp damages the piece.
  - **Rule:** buy unmarked silver or gold only at a price that absorbs the posudek trip.
- **Receipts:** every sale must come with a receipt showing type, price, weight and fineness [verified: search snippet]. Generate it in Fakturoid or iDoklad and put it in the parcel.
- **Tombak (gilt brass) is outside the Hallmarking Act.** Never call it "gold". "Gilt tombak" or "gold-washed brass" is fine [knowledge].
- **Export sales:** whether sales to foreign buyers are exempt is **unverified**, so assume the rules apply to everything sold from CZ.
- **EU mutual recognition:** a valid Czech hallmark eases sales into FR and NL, which have mandatory hallmarking (Reg. 2019/515) [knowledge].

**3. AML**
- Traders in precious metals and stones are obliged persons only for transactions **≥ €10,000**, cash or cashless [verified: https://www.puncovniurad.eu/uncategorized/povinnosti-dle-aml-zakona-pro-obchodniky-s-drahymi-kovy-a-drahymi-kameny/, https://www.fau.gov.cz/cs/rozcestnik/legislativa-a-metodika/informace-pro-povinne-osoby/povinne-osoby-z-nefinancniho-sektoru/obchodnik-s-drahymi-kovy-a-drahymi-kameny-828]. This business stays far below that.
- Still, **log every private seller's name, date, item and price**. A seller-ID record duty for used-goods and precious-metal buying is plausible [knowledge, unverified; see claim 4], and the log is your provenance defence.

**4. Cultural goods (Act 71/1994)**
- An item counts as a "předmět kulturní hodnoty" only if it is significant **and** meets the criteria in Annex 1. Export needs a certificate from the designated institutions in Annex 2 [verified: https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/1994-71, https://mk.gov.cz/zakon-c-71-1994-sb-o-prodeji-a-vyvozu-predmetu-kulturni-hodnoty-v-platnem-zneni-cs-1610].
- Folk jewelry is covered from **100 years** old, and sacred items from 50 years [verified: search snippet, npu.cz].
- **Rule:** no export of museum-grade or pre-1926 pieces over ~€1,500 without asking the Uměleckoprůmyslové museum or NPÚ. Mass-produced post-war Granát pieces are clearly outside the law.
- The EU export licence (Reg. 116/2009) for goldsmiths' work only starts at €50,000 [knowledge].

**5. VAT**
- You stay a non-payer (neplátce).
- The **identified-person** trap applies from the first Etsy fee: register within 15 days, then file a return by the 25th each month.
- The €10,000 EU B2C threshold for OSS applies.
- **Margin scheme (§ 90):** irrelevant unless you register for VAT. Useful if you ever do, because margin-scheme goods fall outside the OSS distance-sales rules (quickstart §3.4).

**6. GPSR**
- Antiques are excluded; vintage, second-hand pieces are in scope, with you as distributor [knowledge].
- Fill the GPSR fields: Granát Turnov as manufacturer where it is marked, otherwise your own details.

**7. REACH**
- The lead and cadmium limits exempt older jewelry first placed on the market before the restriction dates [knowledge].
- Tombak (copper–zinc alloy) carries low nickel risk.

**8. EPR (packaging)**
- CZ: keep records of packaging placed on the market.
- **DE: register in LUCID and license packaging with a dual system before the first DE parcel.**
- Skip FR and AT until the marketplace handles EPR.

**9. Consumer law**
- The 14-day withdrawal right applies.
- The used-goods warranty may be shortened to 12 months; state this in your terms.

**10. WEEE:** not applicable.

**11. IP**
- Use "Granát Turnov" only descriptively, and only for pieces carrying its mark. No logos [knowledge].

**12. UK and US**
- **UK:** VAT is collected by the marketplace up to £135. UK hallmarking has pre-1950 antique exemptions, so keep UK sales to marketplaces [knowledge].
- **US:** only DDP, only ≥ €250 (quickstart §5.5).

## 13. Kill criteria
- **Day 30:**
  - Fewer than **6** pieces found that satisfy the buy rule (≤ 50% of a documented sold comp) → stop; the buy-side gap isn't there.
  - Any fake bought unknowingly → pause and retrain.
- **Day 60:**
  - Fewer than **4** sales, or an average net below **€25** → stop buying and fold the stock into C02.
- **Day 90** (any one of these fails → stop, liquidate within 60 days and keep C06 only as an opportunistic C02 add-on):
  - Fewer than **10** cumulative sales.
  - Trailing-30-day net below **€150**.
  - Monthly sell-through below **15%** of listed stock.
  - Returns above **10%**.
  - More than **2.5 h** per sold unit including sourcing.
