## 1. Verdict in 3 lines
- **Conditional go, but only as a sub-line inside C02 (the vintage-glass shop), selling to the EU first. As a standalone business it's a no-go.** The spread exists only on mispriced private listings. It doesn't exist on quality antique pieces at dealer prices.
- **Realistic month-12 profit: about €375/month** before income tax and health insurance (8 sales × ~€50 net, minus ~€25 fixed costs). Pessimistic €115, optimistic €1,095.
- **Biggest risk:** the spread is far thinner than the longlist's "2–4×". The closest like-for-like pair in the evidence is roughly 1×: a c.1890 tombak brooch asking 5,200 CZK (€208) on Aukro against an antique pyrope brooch on Etsy at $231–260 (€196–221). There is no sold-price data on either side.

## 2. The exact offer

- **Channels:**

  - eBay.de/.at auctions clear tier A.
  - Aukro is for liquidating misbuys.
  - Ruby Lane (monthly shop fee) and 1stDibs (vetted dealers only) come only after 40+ listings and a working US route [knowledge].
- **Changes to the hunter's plan:**
  - **No stone replacement.** Replacing a stone is precious-metal repair, which needs a craft (řemeslná) licence. Outsource it to a licensed goldsmith (zlatník), or sell the piece as "one stone missing".
  - **No ultrasonic cleaner.** It loosens stones set in pitch or glue and ruins the foil backing in closed-back antique settings [knowledge]. Use a soft brush, mild soapy water and isopropyl alcohol instead.

- **Quality-matched spread: about 1–1.8× on asks.** 5,200 CZK (€208) compares with $231–260 (€196–221), and 3,000–3,200 CZK (€120–128) with $280 (€238). After 25–30% friction for fees, shipping and returns, little is left [estimate].
  - The hunter's 2–4× compared low-end CZ pieces with high-end foreign pieces. All three scorers flagged that both sides were asking prices, and they were right.
- **The real edge is mispricing, not origin.** Private sellers on Vinted CZ, Bazoš and Sbazar, Aukro auctions that close below the asks, and mixed "po babičce" boxes often price pieces at 300–1,500 CZK [knowledge; https://ostatni.bazos.cz/inzeraty/cesky-granat/ exists]. That supply is fragmented, Czech-language and slow to work, and that is the moat.
- **Durability:**
  - Pre-1945 stock is finite, but a century of production means it is common in CZ households [knowledge].
  - Mid-century pieces compete with Granát Turnov's current production, which it sells new online. That caps tier-A prices [verified: https://www.granat.cz/en/brooches/].
  - Czech media now tell households that grandmother's garnets are valuable (fajntip), so private asks will creep up.
  - **Net:** the low-end mispricing gap will last for years but cannot scale; the quality-piece gap is already closed [estimate].

- **Buy rule:** landed cost of at most **40% of the median foreign ask** or at most **50% of a documented foreign sold comp**. Never buy from dealers.
- **Realistic volume:** 3–5 qualifying pieces a week, which is more than the 8–10 sales a month the sell-through allows.
- **Synergy with C02, the main reason to do C06 at all:**
  - It shares the searches, trips, Etsy shop, light box, and GPSR/LUCID/identified-person setup.
  - Jewelry is small and unbreakable, so it avoids glass's breakage cost, and it lifts the shop's average order value.
  - Pieces that test as **glass** are still Czech vintage costume jewelry. Sell them honestly in the C02 line ("Bohemian glass, garnet-style", €25–60), which caps the loss on misbuys.
  - The added overhead is one Puncovní úřad notification plus weighing and receipts.

**Telling Czech pyrope from glass and other garnets (tools ≈ €120–200; about 15 h of self-training)**

| Tool | € | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| 10× triplet loupe + LED penlight | 25–40 | **Glass:** round gas bubbles, swirl lines, mould marks, rounded facet edges, orange-peel surface, shell-shaped chips. **Garnet:** crisp facet junctions, natural needles or crystals [knowledge] |
| N52 neodymium magnet | 10 | Pyrope–almandine garnets "drag" toward it (almandine strongly, Bohemian pyrope weakly to moderately); glass and paste don't. Watch for steel pin parts [knowledge] |
| 0.01 g scale | 15 | Legally needed for receipts (weight and fineness) and for the 3 g / 0.5 g hallmark thresholds |
| Gold/silver acid kit + touchstone | 25 | Tests the metal where the hallmark is worn |
| Handheld spectroscope (optional) | 40–80 | Almandine shows strong iron lines at ~505/527/576 nm; pyrope shows a chromium band with weaker iron lines [knowledge] |
| Reference set: 1 marked Granát piece + 1 Jablonec glass imitation | 40 | Trains the eye |

- **Doublets** (a garnet cap on a glass base) show a junction line or a change in lustre [knowledge].
- **Czech pyrope** is small (mostly under 5 mm), rose-cut and deep "fire" red with no purple tint. Large purplish stones or cabochons suggest almandine, and modern Asian settings suggest Indian or African garnet [knowledge].
- A **refractometer** is of little use, because mounted rose cuts have no flat table facet to read.
- **Wording:** "Czech (Bohemian) pyrope, attributed from maker's mark, hallmark and style; stones tested as natural garnet (loupe, magnet)". Never imply a lab certificate you don't have.
- **Study aids:** the garnet exhibits at the Turnov museum, and the official table of recognised old marks [verified: https://punc.gov.cz/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Stare-puncovni-znacky-uznavane-jako-platne-2025-V1.pdf].

## 11. CZ/EU legal checklist
**Trade licence (živnost)**
- A volná živnost with the fields **"Velkoobchod a maloobchod"** and **"Zastavárenská činnost a maloobchod s použitým zbožím"** [knowledge; verify the field name].
- **No special živnost is needed to trade precious-metal jewelry** [knowledge].
- Repairs, soldering and stone setting fall under the craft licence **"Zlatnictví a klenotnictví"** [verified: quickstart §1.2], so outsource them.

**Puncovní úřad (Assay Office)**
1. **Register as a precious-metals trader** within **15 days** of starting (§ 35, Act 539/1992). Notify sales from premises **or an "elektronická adresa"** (your Etsy or eBay shop URL) **at least 3 days beforehand**. You then appear in a public register [verified: https://portal.gov.cz/sluzby-vs/vedeni-registru-vyrobcu-a-obchodniku-s-drahymi-kovy-S1688, https://www.puncovnictvivpraxi.cz/vyrobce-a-obchodnik/23-zakladni-povinnosti-vyrobcu-a-obchodniku-se-sperky].
2. **Hallmark thresholds.** Gold or platinum pieces over **0.5 g** and silver pieces over **3 g** need a valid hallmark. Lighter silver needs only a fineness mark [verified: 02-main-loop-evidence; https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/1992-539].
3. **Old marks remain valid.** Austro-Hungarian (1866–1921), Czechoslovak, Protectorate and Slovak-State marks are recognised (gold down to 585/000) [verified: punc.gov.cz PDF above; https://www.puncovniurad.eu/puncovni-znacky-cz-stare-platne-zlato/]. Check silver finenesses against the same PDF.
4. **Unmarked antique pieces are released by expert opinion, not stamped.** The office issues an **odborný posudek** instead of a hallmark [verified: 539/1992 snippet], so the piece isn't damaged. Buy unmarked silver or gold only at a price that absorbs the trip to the office.
5. **Receipts.** Every sale needs a receipt showing type, price, weight and fineness [verified: search snippet]. Generate it in Fakturoid or iDoklad and put it in the parcel.
6. **Tombak is outside the Act.** Never call it "gold"; "gilt tombak" or "gold-washed brass" is fine.
7. **Export exemption unverified.** Whether sales to foreign buyers are exempt is not known, so assume they are covered.
8. **EU mutual recognition** (Reg. 2019/515) means a Czech hallmark eases sales into FR and NL, where hallmarking is mandatory [knowledge].

**AML**
- Precious-metals traders become obliged persons only for transactions of **€10,000 or more**, cash or cashless [verified: https://www.puncovniurad.eu/uncategorized/povinnosti-dle-aml-zakona-pro-obchodniky-s-drahymi-kovy-a-drahymi-kameny/, https://www.fau.gov.cz/cs/rozcestnik/legislativa-a-metodika/informace-pro-povinne-osoby/povinne-osoby-z-nefinancniho-sektoru/obchodnik-s-drahymi-kovy-a-drahymi-kameny-828]. This business stays far below that.
- Even so, **log every private seller's name, date, item and price**. A seller-ID duty for used-goods buying is plausible [knowledge, unverified; see claim 4], and the log proves provenance.

**Cultural goods (Act 71/1994)**
- An item is covered only if it is significant **and** meets the Annex 1 criteria. Export needs a certificate from an institution listed in Annex 2 [verified: https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/1994-71].
- Folk jewelry is covered from **100 years** old, and sacred items from 50 years [verified: snippet, npu.cz].
- **Rule:** no export of museum-grade pieces, or pre-1926 pieces over ~€1,500, without asking UPM or NPÚ. Mass-produced post-war Granát pieces are clearly outside the Act.
- The EU export licence (Reg. 116/2009) only starts at €50,000 for goldsmiths' work [knowledge].

**VAT**
- You remain a non-payer (neplátce), but the first Etsy fee makes you an **identified person** (register within 15 days, then a monthly return by the 25th).
- The €10,000 EU OSS threshold applies.
- The § 90 margin scheme is irrelevant unless you become VAT-registered (quickstart §3.4).

**Other rules**
- **GPSR:** antiques are excluded; vintage items are in scope, with you as the distributor [knowledge]. Fill the marketplace GPSR fields: Granát Turnov where marked, otherwise yourself.
- **REACH:** older jewelry is largely exempt from the lead and cadmium limits [knowledge].
- **EPR:** keep CZ packaging records. **Register in LUCID and buy a dual-system licence before the first DE parcel.**
- **Consumer law:** the 14-day withdrawal right applies; the warranty on used goods can be 12 months.
- **WEEE:** does not apply.
- **IP:** name "Granát Turnov" only for pieces that carry its mark [knowledge].
- **UK:** sell only through marketplaces, which collect VAT up to £135 [knowledge].
- **US:** DDP only, and only for items of €250 or more.

## 13. Kill criteria
- **Day 30:** fewer than **6** pieces found that meet the buy rule → **stop** (the buy-side gap is absent). Any fake bought unknowingly → **pause and retrain**.
- **Day 60:** fewer than **4** sales, or average net below **€25** → **stop buying** and fold the stock into C02.
- **Day 90:** any one of the following → **stop** and liquidate within 60 days:
  - fewer than **10** cumulative sales;
  - trailing-30-day net below **€150**;
  - sell-through below **15%** of listed stock per month;
  - returns above **10%**;
  - more than **2.5 h** per sold unit.
