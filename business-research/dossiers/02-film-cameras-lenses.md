## 1. Verdict in 3 lines

1. **Conditional go, as a small, labour-bound resale business rather than an asymmetric bet.** Spend €990 on the stage-1 test, and scale only if sourcing yields ≥10 sellable units a month at ≥€35 net each.
2. **Realistic month-12 profit is about €600/month before tax** (16 units × €41 net − €60 fixed), which works out at about €15–20/hour. Once profit crosses the social-insurance threshold, that is about €370/month after levies.
3. **The biggest risk is sourcing yield.** Czech sellers already price the hyped names at or above EU exit prices. The Czech stock that is plentiful (Praktica, Zenit) sells for *less* on eBay.de than Bazoš sellers ask. The margin exists only in untested lots, Japanese bodies and better CZJ/Meyer glass, and all of those are thin on the ground in CZ.

**Where the real asymmetries are**
1. **The proof premium.** eBay is full of "as-is" listings [verified: https://www.ebay.com/itm/397104391593], and "serviced" asks run about 2× unserviced ones. Only one listing supports that 2× figure, so it is weak evidence.
2. **EU location against the dominant Japanese exporters.** An EU buyer pays 19–21% import VAT (IOSS at checkout for consignments ≤€150, at the border above that), plus customs handling and, since 1 Jul 2026, a €3/item duty on low-value parcels [knowledge + quickstart §5.7]. A Czech non-VAT-payer ships VAT-free (§11), which is a structural price advantage of about 20% over JP sellers at the same listing price. **This is the best edge in the case.**
3. **Untested lots priced by the box**, where a Japanese compact or a Pancolar hides among Smenas.
4. **Scarce repair capacity.** Few technicians are left, and no new bodies are made apart from niche reissues such as the Pentax 17 and Rollei 35AF [knowledge]. The working stock shrinks every year.

**Durability: 2–4 years** [estimate].
- The film revival has lasted ~10 years, and film prices are stable.
- The Contax T2 going from ~$250 to ~$2,000 looks like a top-of-cycle signal, so premium compacts carry the most downside.
- Mechanical SLRs and lenses have the most stable demand, because they are bought for use rather than as fashion items.

## 7. Sourcing plan in CZ

**Where to buy**
1. **Bazoš, Sbazar, Aukro and Vinted CZ.** Set keyword watchdog alerts (hlídací pes) for the brand names (Olympus, Pentax, Nikon FM, Canon AE, Minolta, Yashica, Contax, Rollei, Flexaret) and lens names (Pancolar, Flektogon, Trioplan, Biotar, M42). Add "pozůstalost", "sbírka fotoaparátů", "konvolut foto" and "staré fotoaparáty".
   - **Vinted CZ is a buying channel**: households list "starý foťák" for 100–500 Kč [knowledge].
   - Aukro auctions that end at night often close at the starting bid [knowledge].
   - Ask sellers to ship via Zásilkovna so you avoid trips.
2. **House-clearance firms (vyklízení bytů).** Make a standing offer to 5–10 firms in Prague and Brno of 500–1,500 Kč per box of camera gear [estimate]. **This is the only channel that can scale.**
3. **Flea markets and camera swap meets.** Examples are Bleší trhy Praha and Brno Olomoucká. Check the dates of the fotoburza meets [knowledge, unverified].
4. **Pawnshops and small-town bazaars** for Japanese SLRs at €40–80.
5. **Optional: Saxony and Bavaria on Kleinanzeigen.** Search "Kamera Konvolut" near the border; lots of GDR glass sell there [knowledge].

**Buying rules**
- Lots and untested units only.
- A maximum of 30% of the expected net exit price per sellable unit.
- Never buy hyped singles at named asks.
- Always inspect lenses with a torch for fungus and haze before paying when you collect in person.

**Realistic volume** [estimate]:
- Weeks 1–8: 3–5 sellable units a week while learning.
- Steady state: 4–6 a week, i.e. 16–24 a month.
- About 1 premium find a month.

Each sellable unit implies buying ~1.35 units because of duds.

## 11. CZ/EU legal checklist for this product

**Trade licence**
- Volná živnost, "Velkoobchod a maloobchod", as a side activity (800 CZK online).
- Registering in Oct 2026 is fine. The 2026 social threshold drops to 29,375 CZK, and Q4 profit will stay below it [estimate].

**VAT**
- The founder is a non-payer. The first eBay or Etsy fee (a foreign B2B service) creates **identified person** status: register within 15 days, file a monthly return by the 25th, and pay 21% on fees.
- **The €10k distance-sales trap:** B2C sales to other EU states above €10k/yr must carry destination VAT (DE 19% on the full price), which would wipe out the margin. A year-2 turnover of ~€15–20k, mostly to DE, crosses it.
- Options to settle in the week-1 advisor hour:
  - (a) Voluntary CZ VAT registration plus the **§90 margin scheme**. VAT is 21/121 of the margin, about €8–10 a unit, and margin-scheme goods fall outside the distance-sales rules [B, quickstart §3.4].
  - (b) The **EU cross-border SME exemption** in force since 2025, which covers EU turnover under €100k and DE sales under the €25k Kleinunternehmer limit [knowledge, verify].

**Warranty, B2C, used goods (the key cost)**
- *CZ law:* 24 months of liability for defects, which for used goods **can be shortened by agreement to 12 months**. Defects appearing within 1 year are presumed to have existed at delivery, and claims must be settled in 30 days (quickstart §5.3). A known defect, such as a dead meter, excludes liability only if the consumer was specifically told and expressly accepted it [knowledge; Directive 2019/771 art. 7(5)].
- *DE buyers:* under Rome I art. 6, German mandatory law applies to listings aimed at Germany [knowledge]. § 476(2) BGB allows the 1-year shortening for used goods only if the consumer was "eigens in Kenntnis gesetzt" (specifically informed) before ordering **and** it was "ausdrücklich und gesondert vereinbart" (expressly and separately agreed). OLG Köln rejected ordinary contract clauses on 9 Apr 2025, and online checkouts struggle to meet the test [verified: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__476.html, https://www.it-recht-kanzlei.de/neues-kaufrecht-verkuerzung-gewaehrleistung-bei-gebrauchtwaren.html].
- **So plan for 24 months of exposure to DE buyers.** After month 12 the buyer has to prove the defect existed at delivery, which is hard with a 40-year-old camera, so the practical risk sits in year 1.
- The 14-day withdrawal right cannot be excluded. A deduction for "use beyond testing" (shooting a roll) is hard to enforce.
- **Log serial numbers and take pre-ship photos** so a swapped return can be identified.

**WEEE**
- Electronic compacts and SLRs with battery-powered meters are very likely EEE. A distance seller placing them on the German market from abroad needs stiftung ear registration via an authorised representative, and this applies to used devices too [verified: https://www.it-recht-kanzlei.de/Elektrogesetz-WEEE-Nummer-Verkauf-gebrauchte-Elektrogeraete.html, https://www.haendlerbund.de/de/ratgeber/recht/4038-elektrogesetz-haendler].
- Out of scope: manual lenses, adapters, and bodies with no battery at all (Flexaret, Zenit E). The selenium-powered Olympus Trip is borderline.
- **Stage 1: sell EEE in CZ/SK only**, since reselling there doesn't count as a new placing on the market [knowledge]. Register in DE in stage 2.

**Batteries**
- Ship no batteries.
- Remove the date-back lithium coin cells that many 1990s compacts carry, to stay outside battery EPR.

**GPSR**
- GPSR applies to second-hand goods. Fill eBay's GPSR fields, naming the original maker and yourself as the EU operator.
- Include safety notes: flash high voltage, battery type, and a **thoriated-glass disclosure** for some Takumar and CZJ lenses [knowledge].
- Buy adapters from an EU distributor rather than directly from China, or you become the importer.

**Packaging EPR**
- LUCID plus a dual system before the first DE parcel (€30–100/yr); keep EKO-KOM records.
- Start with DE, CZ and SK, and add AT and NL after checking their representative rules (quickstart §4.3).

**Platforms**
- An eBay.de business account (Impressum, AGB, withdrawal notice). New-seller limits and payout holds apply for the first weeks [knowledge].
- Etsy is fine for vintage items (20+ years old).

**Not applicable:** hallmarking. On IP: never sell Zorki/FED "Leica" conversions as genuine Leicas.

## 12. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Sourcing yield (the main risk) | House-clearance standing offers, all-platform alerts, Kleinanzeigen lots near the border. Kill at day 30 if fewer than 8 sellable units |
| Hidden-fault returns and claims | Film-test every body; shoot 2 rolls for premium compacts; no electronic repairs; 5–10% reserve; serial log |
| The trend reverses (the T2 at $2k looks toppy) | Keep compacts under 40% of stock; turn stock within 60 days; weight towards lenses and mechanical SLRs |
| Beginner damage | Practise on €10 junk; buy JIS tools; stay at seal level in stage 1 |
| WEEE and the DE cease-and-desist (Abmahnung) culture | EEE sold in CZ/SK until registered; German legal texts with cease-and-desist protection; no "warranty" USP claims |
| Crossing the €10k VAT threshold | Track it monthly; choose margin scheme or SME scheme before the threshold is reached |
| Crowding on Helios kits | Lead with CZJ/Meyer glass; Helios only as serviced units |

## 13. Kill criteria

- **Day 30:** fewer than 8 sellable units sourced at ≤30% of expected exit price → stop buying compacts and test the lens line only.
- **Day 60:** fewer than 8 sold, **or** average net below €30, **or** returns plus claims above 12% → liquidate the stock and stop.
- **Day 90:** run rate below 10 sellable units a month, **or** net profit below €250/month, **or** hands-on time above 3 h/unit → stop scaling. Keep only the lens line if it clears €30/h.

## 14. 12-week launch plan

1. File the JRF (trade licence). Book 1 h with a tax advisor on the VAT options. Set the Bazoš, Sbazar, Aukro and Vinted alerts. Order tools.
2. Buy 5 junk bodies and 3 junk Helios lenses. Learn diagnosis and seals. Build the bulk-film, home-dev and scan workflow.
3. Open the eBay.de business account and register as an identified person. Set up LUCID, the dual system, German legal texts and the GPSR insert. Log 20 eBay.de *sold* comps for each of the 15 target models.
4. Buy the first €400 of stock: 2 estate boxes, 6 compacts, 4 CZJ/Meyer lenses. Contact 5 house-clearance firms.
5. Test and clean. List the first 8: lenses on eBay.de, compacts on Bazoš/Aukro (CZ/SK).
6. Source batch 2. Run film tests. Answer offers, and adjust prices using watcher counts.
7. List batch 2 (8 units). Put 3 items above €150 on Etsy.
8. **Day-60 review against the kill criteria.** Decide on stage 2.
9. Start the DE WEEE representative registration. Do your first helicoid service on junk Helios lenses.
10. Flea market or camera swap meet. Buy 10–15 units and move to 5 listings a week.
11. Launch a serviced-lens pilot (3 units) to measure the "serviced" premium in *sold* prices.
12. **Day-90 review.** Check the €10k and VAT-ID bookkeeping. Set the month 4–6 targets.
