## 1. Verdict in 3 lines

1. **Conditional go, as a small, labour-bound resale business rather than an asymmetric bet.** Run the €990 stage-1 test, and scale only if sourcing yields ≥10 sellable units a month at ≥€35 net.
2. **Realistic month-12 profit is about €600/month before tax** (16 units × €41 − €60 fixed), which is about €15–20/hour. That becomes about €370/month after levies once profit crosses the social-insurance threshold.
3. **The biggest risk is sourcing yield.** Czech sellers already price the hyped names at or above EU exit prices. The abundant Czech stock (Praktica, Zenit) sells for *less* on eBay.de than Bazoš sellers ask. The margin exists only in untested lots, Japanese bodies and better CZJ/Meyer glass, and all of those are thin in CZ.

### 8a. Skill ramp for a beginner [knowledge]

| When | Skill | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Weeks 1–2 | Diagnosis: meter vs. a phone app, a €20 DIY optical shutter tester, torch check for haze/fungus/separation, cleaning battery corrosion | Practise on 5 junk bodies at €10 each. Diagnosis decides what you buy |
| Weeks 2–6 | **Light seals and mirror bumper**: scrape with a wooden stick and naphtha, fit pre-cut kits or 1–3 mm adhesive foam. 20–45 min per body once practised | The first 10 bodies take 2–3× longer. Budget 10–20% damaged as tuition |
| Weeks 6–12 | Oily aperture blades and helicoid regreasing on M42 lenses; the Canon AE-1 "squeal" fix; cleaning the Olympus Trip shutter | Mark the helicoid entry point. **JIS screwdrivers only** |
| Months 3–12 | CLA on cloth and leaf shutters (Zenit, Canonet), using Tomosy's repair books and Chris Sherlock's "Fix Old Cameras" | **Never repair electronic compacts** (mju, T4, T2): the ~300 V flash capacitor, fragile flex cables, no parts. Sell failures "for parts" |

**Time to competence:** ~20–40 h for diagnosis, seals and lens cleaning; another 50–100 h for CLA [estimate]. So stage 1 sells "tested and cleaned" units, not "fully serviced" ones.

## 11. CZ/EU legal checklist for this product

**Trade licence and timing**
- Volná živnost, "Velkoobchod a maloobchod", as a side activity (800 CZK online).
- Registering in Oct 2026 is fine. The 2026 social threshold drops to 29,375 CZK, and Q4 profit stays below it [estimate].

**VAT**
- The first eBay or Etsy fee triggers **identified person** status: register within 15 days, file monthly by the 25th, and pay 21% on fees.
- **The €10k trap:** EU B2C distance sales above €10k/yr must carry destination VAT (DE 19% on the full price), which erases the margin. Year 2 crosses it.
- Settle this in the advisor hour:
  - (a) Voluntary CZ VAT registration plus the **§90 margin scheme**: 21/121 of the margin (≈€8–10 a unit), with margin-scheme goods outside the distance-sales rules [B, quickstart §3.4].
  - (b) The **EU cross-border SME exemption** in force since 2025, which covers EU turnover under €100k and DE sales under €25k [knowledge, verify].

**Warranty for used goods sold B2C (the key cost)**
- *CZ law:* 24 months of liability, shortenable by agreement to **12 months for used goods**. There is a 1-year presumption that a defect existed at delivery, and claims must be settled in 30 days (quickstart §5.3).
- A known defect, such as a dead meter, is excluded only if the buyer was specifically told and expressly and separately accepted it [knowledge; Directive 2019/771 art. 7(5)].
- *DE buyers:* German mandatory law applies to listings aimed at Germany (Rome I art. 6) [knowledge]. § 476(2) BGB allows the 1-year shortening only if the consumer was "eigens in Kenntnis gesetzt" (specifically informed) before ordering **and** it was "ausdrücklich und gesondert vereinbart" (expressly and separately agreed). OLG Köln rejected ordinary contract clauses on 9 Apr 2025 [verified: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__476.html, https://www.it-recht-kanzlei.de/neues-kaufrecht-verkuerzung-gewaehrleistung-bei-gebrauchtwaren.html].
- **So plan for 24 months of exposure to DE buyers.** After month 12 the buyer must prove the defect existed at delivery, so the practical risk sits in year 1.
- The 14-day withdrawal right cannot be excluded. A deduction for "shot a roll" is hard to enforce.
- **Log serial numbers and take pre-ship photos** so a swapped return can be identified.

**WEEE**
- Electronic compacts and SLRs with battery meters are very likely EEE. Distance-selling them into DE from abroad needs stiftung ear registration via an authorised representative, and used devices are included [verified: https://www.it-recht-kanzlei.de/Elektrogesetz-WEEE-Nummer-Verkauf-gebrauchte-Elektrogeraete.html, https://www.haendlerbund.de/de/ratgeber/recht/4038-elektrogesetz-haendler].
- Out of scope: manual lenses, adapters, and bodies with no battery at all (Flexaret, Zenit E). The selenium-powered Trip is borderline.
- **Stage 1: EEE goes to CZ/SK only** [knowledge]. Register in DE in stage 2.

**Batteries**
- Ship none.
- Remove the lithium coin cells in 1990s date backs.

**GPSR**
- Fill eBay's GPSR fields, naming the original maker and yourself as the EU operator.
- Include flash high-voltage and thoriated-glass notes for some Takumar and CZJ lenses [knowledge].
- Buy adapters from an EU distributor, or you become the importer.

**Packaging EPR**
- LUCID plus a dual system before the first DE parcel (€30–100/yr); keep EKO-KOM records.
- Ship to DE, CZ and SK first.

**Platforms and IP**
- eBay.de business account (Impressum, AGB, withdrawal notice). New-seller limits and payout holds apply [knowledge].
- Etsy vintage means 20+ years old.
- Hallmarking does not apply. Never sell Zorki/FED "Leica" conversions as genuine.

## 13. Kill criteria

- **Day 30:** fewer than 8 sellable units sourced at ≤30% of the exit price → stop buying compacts; test lenses only.
- **Day 60:** fewer than 8 sold, **or** average net below €30, **or** returns plus claims above 12% → liquidate and stop.
- **Day 90:** run rate below 10 sellable units a month, **or** below €250/month net, **or** above 3 h/unit → stop scaling. Keep the lens line only if it clears €30/h.
