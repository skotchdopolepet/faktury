# C01 dossier: flash-modded iPod Classic from broken CZ donors

Date: 2026-09-29. Tags: **[verified: URL]** means a search-result snippet seen this session or cited in `01`/`02` (no page was opened); **[knowledge]** means unverified background knowledge; **[estimate: …]** shows its reasoning. FX is 24.5 CZK/EUR (quickstart).

---

## 1. Verdict in 3 lines

1. **Conditional go, but only as a small, CZ-domestic business (5–10 units a month).** The pitch of "EU buyers at €190–300" doesn't survive the costs: DE WEEE and battery registration, 21% reverse-charge VAT on eBay and Etsy fees, and the €10k OSS threshold together cut the eBay.de net to ≈ €34 per unit, and to ≈ €0 once EU sales pass the €10k OSS threshold.
2. **Realistic month-12 profit: about €500/month** before income tax and levies, roughly €20/h. At that level the social-insurance cliff applies, so about €310/month is left after levies.
3. **Biggest risk: a margin squeeze with no moat.** German and Czech modders have the same donors, parts and YouTube tutorials. Meanwhile donor and microSD costs are rising: 512 GB cards roughly doubled to ~$70. That can push the CZ net from about €90 down to about €50.

- **Sold empty.** Each unit ships with a CS/DE/EN "how to sync (Finder / Apple Devices / Rockbox)" sheet [knowledge: the sync question is the main support load].
- **Service-line check.** Buying donors, modifying them and selling finished units is **retail of your own refurbished goods**, which is a product under the brief. It would become repair-on-demand if you offered "send me your iPod and I'll mod it", took customer-supplied devices, or did bespoke one-off builds.
  - **Rule:** decline all mod-your-device requests.
  - The legitimate substitute is **trade-in**: you *buy* the customer's dead iPod as a donor, and they buy a stock unit. That is two sales of goods.
  - Warranty repairs are a legal duty, not a service line.
- **Walkman add-on: don't make it a line.**
  - Belts are cheap, but idler tyres, motors, speed calibration and corroded contacts often push the work past 2 h per unit [knowledge].
  - New players cap the price for the casual buyer: FiiO CP13 at ≈ €110–130, We Are Rewind at ≈ €90–110 [knowledge].
  - No demand data was gathered (the hunter rated it 2/5).
  - Only sell Walkmans that arrive in donor lots and need under 1 h. They are EEE (WEEE applies) but hold no Li-ion cells.

## 4. Supply gap and asymmetry: honest assessment

- **In DE there is no supply gap.**
  - Kleinanzeigen has several iFlash builds, for example a 7G 512 GB with 2,000 mAh [verified: https://www.kleinanzeigen.de/s-iflash/k0].
  - eBay.de has further listings [verified: https://www.ebay.de/itm/168461378829, https://www.ebay.de/itm/376701255536, https://www.ebay.de/itm/357501277854].
  - DE repair shops sell SD conversions from 128 GB to 1 TB [verified: https://www.profi-repair.de/reparatur-apple-ipod-classic/].
  - German modders also have cheap donors: eBay.at shows **96 "iPod classic defekt" listings from €14.26** [verified: https://www.ebay.at/sch/i.html?_sacat=10542&_nkw=ipod+classic+defekt&_frs=1]. Scorer A is right that CZ donor access is **not** a cross-border edge.
- **The real asymmetry is the value step.** A €15–40 dead donor (the HDD is usually what died, and it is exactly the part the mod replaces) becomes a €160–300 product. Anyone with €100 of tools can capture it.
- **The CZ market is probably thinner on the supply side.** It has fewer semi-pro modders than DE [estimate: CZ has ~1/8 of DE's population and no CZ modder surfaced in any search this session; unverified]. Selling domestically also avoids cross-border EPR and VAT, which is the one structural edge a CZ founder has.
- **Durability: about 12–24 months.** Barriers are low, tutorials are abundant, and parts are on AliExpress, so expect price compression. Scorer B's call of "gap closes in 6–12 months" is plausible for DE; CZ should lag. Trade the trend with small, fast-turning stock. Don't build long-lived assets.

## 6. Unit economics (estimates per sold unit)

The table rests on these assumptions:
- **Donor:** €30 effective. That covers a €15–25 purchase, inbound postage, and 25–30% duds, which are parted out.
- **Adapter:** clone iFlash €18 landed. A genuine iFlash-Quad costs €50: $67.99 on Amazon [verified: https://www.amazon.com/iFlash-Micro-Adapter-Classic-MCMConcepts/dp/B0DW5Y7FKT], or from the UK plus import VAT.
- **Cards:** 256 GB €38 and 512 GB €72. The 512 GB EVO Select went from ~$35 to ~$70 and card prices are up 124% [verified: https://www.photoworkout.com/memory-card-prices-ai-shortage-2026/, https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/storage/memory-cards-and-flash-drives-prices-rocket-124-percent-some-products-peak-at-261-percent-jump-increases-from-2025-driven-by-ai-chip-shortage-across-a-range-of-formats-and-capacities].

- **Returns reserve.** It assumes 8–12% of units cause an incident in 12 months at about €40 each, plus a 3–5% withdrawal rate [estimate: no published data].
  - Known failure points: torn headphone-jack/hold-switch flex on 6G/7G, clone adapters dropping out, fake or failing cards, and batteries that won't charge. Third-party battery charge failures are reported [verified: https://www.iflash.xyz/3rd-party-extended-battery-guide/].
  - Germany adds cost: shortening the warranty for used goods needs a separate express agreement (§476 BGB), so **plan for 24 months on DE sales** [knowledge].
- **VAT trap for DE.** Once EU cross-border B2C sales pass **€10,000 a year** (about 45 units), you must charge 19% DE VAT via OSS. That costs about €35 per unit, which pushes S1 on eBay.de to **≈ €0** and S2 on Etsy to about €20 (quickstart §3.2).
  - The margin scheme doesn't rescue it: the donor cost is tiny, so VAT on the margin is almost VAT on the whole price.
- **Implication:** CZ direct is the only channel with a solid margin. DE only makes sense at **≥ €249** and under the €10k cap.

## 7. Sourcing and production plan in CZ

- **Donors.**
  - Set Bazoš watchdogs ("ipod classic", "ipod video", "nefunkční", "na ND") in both the elektro and mobil sections. Also watch Aukro, Sbazar, Vinted CZ, FB Marketplace, and estate or bulk lots from C02/C07 sourcing trips.
  - Broken-unit listings exist [verified: https://mobil.bazos.cz/inzeraty/ipod-classic/], but prices weren't visible. Aukro 99–469 Kč figures are probably auction **starts** [verified snippet, `01`].
  - **Expect 5–20 broken Classic/Video donors a month nationally**, contested by other flippers [estimate: 20–50 iPod listings a month across platforms × 25–40% broken; unverified].
  - Supplement with "working but tired" 80 GB units at 1,500–2,500 Kč (no dud risk) and eBay.at/.de "defekt" units from €14 plus €6–10 shipping.
  - The BankMyCell trade-in average for a 7G is $25.68, and broken units fetch ≤ $5 [verified: https://www.bankmycell.com/sell/ipod-classic-7]. That is a floor, and sellers' asks are drifting up.
  - **Budget donor cost rising from about €20 to €35–40 effective within 12 months** [estimate].
- **Parts.**
  - Genuine iFlash from iflash.xyz (UK) or resellers [verified: https://www.iflash.xyz/store/]. Use genuine for S3 and anything shipped to DE.
  - AliExpress clones for S1 are tested on arrival; expect some DOA [knowledge].
  - Cards: branded SanDisk/Samsung from Alza/CZC, each verified with H2testw/F3.
  - **Batteries: buy from an EU/CZ distributor that supplies a CE declaration**, such as appleking.cz or iFixit EU. That way *they* are the battery importer for CZ and you are not (see §11).
- **Realistic volume:** 2–3 units a week (8–12 a month). Donors and demand cap volume before hours do.

## 11. CZ/EU legal checklist for this product

| Item | What applies |
|---|---|
| **Živnost** | Volná živnost, fields *Velkoobchod a maloobchod* plus *Výroba elektronických součástek, elektrických zařízení a výroba a opravy … elektronických zařízení*. The device is low-voltage, so no vázaná electrical licence is needed [knowledge]. Check the §304 ZP non-compete clause. |
| **VAT** | Non-payer; CZ sales carry no VAT until 2M CZK turnover. **Identified person from the first eBay or Etsy fee**: monthly return, 21% on fees. **€10k cap on EU B2C** or 19%+ destination VAT via OSS [quickstart §3]. |
| **Margin scheme** | Only for VAT payers; negligible benefit here because donors are nearly free. Ignore. |
| **GPSR** | Replacing the battery with a higher-capacity cell is plausibly a **substantial modification**, which makes you the manufacturer [knowledge]. You need: a 1-page risk analysis; a serial number per unit; your name, address and email on the box; battery safety warnings in CS/DE/EN; a complaints log; and GPSR fields on each marketplace. |
| **WEEE** | **CZ:** reselling used EEE already on the CZ market makes you a distributor, not a producer [knowledge; confirm with MŽP/ASEKOL]. Keep CZ stock sourced in CZ. **DE:** a distance seller is the *Hersteller*, there is no de minimis, a Bevollmächtigter is required, and fines reach €100k [verified: https://www.get-e-right.de/leistungen/weee-bevollmaechtigter-in-deutschland/, https://www.provimedia.de/blog/elektrog-weee]. Used devices placed on the DE market for the first time are covered [knowledge; verify]. eBay.de checks WEEE numbers, and competitors send *Abmahnungen* [verified: https://www.it-recht-kanzlei.de/abmahnung-fehlende-registrierung-ear.html]. **SK, AT, FR and the others each need their own registration, and so do Etsy and Vinted cross-border sales.** Etsy is not a loophole. |
| **Batteries (Reg. 2023/1542)** | **CZ:** if you import the cells yourself (AliExpress), you are the CZ battery producer: register and join ECOBAT/REMA, and you carry the CE/labelling duties for non-compliant cells [knowledge]. Avoid this by buying from an EU distributor. **DE:** the BattDG has been in force since 7 Oct 2025, and foreign producers need a DE authorised representative [verified: https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/themen/battdg-in-kraft-getreten-uba-beleiht-erneut, https://www.stiftung-ear.de/themenwelten/hersteller/welche-pflichten-haben-hersteller-von-batterien/]. |
| **Packaging EPR** | CZ: EKO-KOM small-quantity relief, keep records. DE: LUCID plus a dual system before the first DE parcel. |
| **Warranty and withdrawal** | 24 months, or 12 for used goods by agreement in CZ; effectively 24 in DE. 14-day withdrawal. Don't sell "custom" builds hoping to escape withdrawal rights. |
| **Shipping** | Li-ion contained in equipment, UN3481 Section II (~11 Wh). Road via Packeta is fine; never ship swollen cells [knowledge]. |
| **IP** | Selling genuine Apple devices is fine, but disclose "modified with non-original parts; not affiliated with Apple". **Never fit AliExpress shells that carry Apple logos or engravings** (counterfeit marks); use original or logo-free shells. No Apple logo in branding. No preloaded music. |
| **Platforms and DAC7** | Vinted business sales require Vinted Pro (CZ availability unverified). Vinted CZ now trades cross-border with HR/HU/RO/SI [verified: https://www.penize.cz/nakupy/496859-vinted-dalo-cechum-pristup-k-novym-trhum-pribyly-ctyri-zeme], and those sales carry EPR duties there. Etsy: 5G/5.5G only. Expect DAC7 reporting. Hallmarking: N/A. |

## 12. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Price compression from DE/CZ modders with identical access | Win on trust: reviews, serial-numbered burn-in reports, 12-month warranty and a Czech-language sync guide. Hold ≤ 10 finished units. Reprice monthly. |
| Input inflation (NAND spike until ~2027; donors) | Make 128/256 GB the default. Offer a "card-ready" unit without a card. Don't pre-buy cards. |
| Compliance or Abmahnung in DE | CZ-only until registered. No eBay.de, Etsy or Vinted cross-border sales before registering. |
| Returns and failures | Genuine adapters for DE and premium builds. Test every card. A full charge cycle plus 2 h of playback. Log serials and photos per unit. |
| Fad reversal | Stock turns in < 30 days. Part out the remaining donors if you exit. |
| Service creep | Decline mod requests; offer trade-ins instead. |
| Li-ion incident | Quality cells only. Product-liability insurance at about 2–4k CZK a year [estimate]. |

## 14. 12-week launch plan

11. Decide on DE/Etsy (5G only), using the week-1 comps and the quotes.
