# Dossier C02: Vintage Czechoslovak glass, ceramics and Christmas-glass picker

Date: 2026-09-29. Evidence tags: **[verified: URL]** means a search-result snippet for that URL (pages could not be opened). **[knowledge]** means background knowledge not checked this session. **[estimate]** means my arithmetic, with the reasoning given. Exchange rate: 24.5 CZK/EUR.

## 1. Verdict in 3 lines
- **Conditional GO.** Start only as a 60-day, ≤ €1,000 test, sold to **EU buyers first** (Etsy EU + eBay.de + Catawiki). Make stage 2 conditional on the day-60 numbers in section 13.
- **Realistic month-12 profit: about €575/month** before income tax and levies (range €240–1,300). That is roughly 25 sales a month at €26 net, which works out to **€15–20 per hour**.
- **Single biggest risk: demand after the US shrank.** The CZ shops that prove this model were built on US buyers, whom Dwell puts at about 90% of sales. Since de minimis ended, every US parcel carries a prepaid 15% duty. How fast EU-only buyers absorb mid-tier pieces has never been measured. **Sourcing is not the binding constraint in year 1; sell-through is.**

  - Individual designer listings exist, e.g. Jurnikl Rosice #692 on 1stDibs [verified: https://www.1stdibs.com/furniture/decorative-objects/vases-vessels/vases/rudolf-jurnikl-sklo-union-rosice-692-pressed-glass-vase-czechia-1963/id-f_45947282].

## 4. Supply gap, asymmetry and durability
- **The asymmetry is origin-based.** The goods were made here in the millions and sit in Czech flats and estates, and Czech demand for mid-tier pieces is lower than Western demand. The flow is renewed by estates: the generation that furnished homes in the 1960s–80s is now clearing them. [estimate] That flow lasts 10+ years.
- **Where the gap has already closed:**
  - **Famous names.** Czech collectors bid up Jurnikl, Vízner and Zemek on Aukro (scorer C [knowledge]).
  - **Czech clickbait media inflating private sellers' expectations:**
    - "a vase with this pattern… collectors pay up to 78,000 Kč" [verified: https://www.tiscali.cz/kdo-ma-doma-starou-sklenenou-vazu-s-timhle-nenapadnym-lomenym-vzorem-sedi-na-malem-jmeni-sberatele-za-ni-davaji-i-78-000-kc-723304];
    - "a box of ornaments after grandma for 12,000 Kč" [verified: https://extrafit.cz/nemate-doma-po-babicce-stare-vanocni-ozdoby-za-jednu-krabici-dnes-sberatele-nabizeji-i-12-000-kc/].
  - **Ornaments at Bazoš asking prices.** Boxes run 500–1,000 Kč, and a 1963 set of 6 costs 400 Kč [verified: https://ostatni.bazos.cz/inzeraty/vanocni-ozdoby/, https://ostatni.bazos.cz/inzeraty/stare-ozdoby/]. That is about €2.7 per piece against $3.75 abroad, so **there is no spread at Bazoš asks**.
- **Where the gap persists:** unidentified pieces in mixed lots ("pozůstalost", "vyklízení", "krabice skla"). The edge is identification work (Czech catalogues, factory knowledge), not mere access. Example: a Rosice vase listed at **€20** on bazos.sk [verified: https://ostatne.bazos.sk/inzerat/195937716/vaza-zo-serie-sklo-union-rosice.php] against €40–120 exits.
- **Durability:** moderate and long. Other CZ/SK pickers compete, but fragmented supply that needs local labour can't be industrialised.

**What this means:**

- After month 6, cut sourcing to 3–4 h/week and **raise the bar** to an expected sale of at least €45.
- The true bottleneck is *high-value* finds (lines B and E), which come at roughly 1–3 a month. That matches the 02-evidence view that "sourcing speed is the bottleneck", but only for the profitable top end.
- **Walk-away rule:** do not chase named pieces on Aukro against collectors.

## 11. CZ/EU legal checklist (product-specific)
- **Živnost:**
  - Volná, with the field "Velkoobchod a maloobchod". Also tick **"Zastavárenská činnost a maloobchod s použitým zbožím"** if it is on the current list [knowledge; verify on the MPO obory PDF].
  - Register in **Oct 2026**. Q4 deemed profit must stay under the prorated 2026 social threshold of about 29,375 CZK, which is easy at test scale.
  - Keep a **purchase log**: date, source, seller, price and item. It serves tax, returns and any future § 90 records.
- **Expense method:** compare the 60% paušál with actual expenses. Buy costs (~12%) plus shipping, fees and packaging come to roughly 50% of revenue, so the two are close. Actual expenses allow the year-end stock lever.
- **VAT: what actually matters at this scale:**
  1. **Identified person** from the first Etsy, eBay or Catawiki fee: register within 15 days, then file a monthly return by the 25th with 21% on the fees, ≈ €1.2–1.9 per order (included above).
  2. **The €10,000/yr EU B2C distance-sales threshold** (all other EU states combined, *including shipping charged*). The realistic month-12 run rate (25 × 55% EU × €62) ≈ **€10.2k/yr**, right at the line. Above it you charge destination VAT on the **full price** via OSS: DE 19%, which is ≈ €10.9 on a €68 order, about 35% of unit net. Sales to CZ, the UK (the marketplace collects VAT), the US and CH do not count toward the threshold.
  3. **Margin scheme (§ 90):** **irrelevant in year 1.** It is only for VAT payers, and the payer threshold of 2M CZK (~€80k) is far away.
     - It matters only if EU sales pass €10k. Margin-scheme goods are excluded from distance-sales rules [verified: https://www.du.cz/33/90-zdph-zvlastni-rezim-pro-obchodniky-s-pouzitym-zbozim-umeleckymi-dily-sberatelskymi-predmety-a-starozitnostmi-uniqueidmRRWSbk196FNf8-jVUh4Ep4e0Vmy5oaCuF3mPlrgfE0CD-vMUnwPlw/], so VAT stays Czech 21% on the *margin*.
     - Buy prices are tiny (margin ≈ 88% of price), so § 90 VAT is ≈ €10.4 per €68 order, or ≈ €6.4 after deducting ≈ €4 of input VAT on shipping, packaging and fees. A non-payer on OSS pays ≈ €12.3 (€10.9 + €1.4 of non-deductible fee VAT).
     - The ≈ €6 saving per EU order roughly equals the extra accountant and domestic-VAT burden at 14 EU orders a month [estimate].
     - **Year-2 rule:** keep EU B2C under €10k by routing volume to UK, US and CZ; consider voluntary registration plus § 90 only above ~30 EU orders a month.
- **GPSR:**
  - Second-hand consumer goods are in scope; antiques are excluded [knowledge]. As a distributor, fill the marketplace GPSR fields.
  - Describe glass and ceramics as **decorative**, and state "vintage glaze/glass, not tested for lead or cadmium release; not for food use", because Reg. 1935/2004 food-contact rules otherwise attach [knowledge].
  - Disclose uranium glass.
- **Packaging EPR:**
  - CZ: EKO-KOM records only below ~300 kg/yr.
  - **DE: LUCID registration plus a dual-system licence before the first DE parcel** (€30–100/yr).
  - FR: sell only via marketplaces or skip it.
  - Watch the PPWR (applies from 12 Aug 2026).
- **WEEE:** none for glass and ceramics. **Do not sell wired lamps.**
- **Hallmarking:** none for glass. Only the C06 add-on triggers it (section 7).
- **IP and attribution:**
  - Use designer and factory names only descriptively.
  - Write "attributed to" unless the piece is marked or in the catalogue.
  - Never reuse other sellers' photos.
  - Misattribution counts as "not as described", so the buyer gets a refund.
- **Consumer law:**
  - 14-day withdrawal right.
  - Shorten the used-goods warranty to 12 months in the T&Cs.
  - Impressum for eBay.de.
  - ČOI is the ADR body.
- **Cultural goods:** EU export licensing outside the EU sets thresholds far above these values [knowledge]. Check CZ Act 71/1994 only for pre-1950 pieces above ~€1,000.

## 12. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| EU-only demand too thin (the proven shops were US-driven) | Three EU channels from week 3 (Etsy, eBay.de, Catawiki); UK via marketplaces; US only for duty-inclusive items above €60; the 60-day test measures it before scaling |
| Slow sell-through, aging stock | Cap stock at €2,500 until month 6; re-price at 90 days; liquidate 180-day stock as Catawiki lots or on Aukro |

| €10k EU VAT threshold | Monthly tracker; channel routing (section 11) |
| US tariff or postal rules change again | Treat the US as a bonus, never as the base |
| Etsy account or algorithm risk | eBay.de and Catawiki live from week 3 |

**Scorer objections, answered:**
- **A (economics only 5: heavy, fragile, US duty, 35–90 min).** Confirmed. Net is €29–31 on mid-tier pieces and €16–17 on cheap pieces and ornament bundles, i.e. €16–25/h. The fix is a price floor, not volume.
- **B (low €/h, slow sell-through, unmeasured buy prices, many pickers).** Buy prices are now partly evidenced: a €20 bazos.sk vase, and ornament boxes at €2.7/pc, which kills ornament arbitrage at asks. Sell-through is still unmeasured, which is why the day-60 gate exists.
- **C (Aukro collectors price up famous names; US impaired).** Agreed. The plan buys mixed lots and mid-tier identified pieces, sells EU-first, and never bids against collectors on Aukro.
