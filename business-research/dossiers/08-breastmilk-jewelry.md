## 1. Verdict in 3 lines
- **NO-GO as the founder's main bet.** At most, run a capped CZ/SK-only test (≤ €1,000, steel settings, kill at day 60) if the founder actually wants to do resin craft.
- **Realistic month-12 profit: about €500/month before levies** (about €390 after the 22% set-aside). That is roughly **€14/h** once marketing and admin hours are counted.
- **Biggest risk:** there is no durable gap. In DE and PL the speed gap has closed, because a PL-based leader claims 100k+ orders, sells a patented DIY kit and competes with DE express tiers of 2–3 weeks. In CZ/SK, speed is a copyable feature in a trust-gated niche, and the work is custom on irreplaceable customer material, which is service-like and breaks the brief.

**Why the gap isn't durable:**
- The queues are **capacity queues, not technical ones.** Actual elapsed process time is 5–10 days (§8).
- Any CZ incumbent can add a paid express tier in a day, and some already offer rush "by agreement".
- Entry costs under €1,000, and the skill takes weeks to learn, so supply is elastic.
- The only real barrier is **trust and portfolio**, and that protects incumbents, not the newcomer.
- Your own speed promise decays as you grow: at about 25 pieces/month the founder's hours cap creates the same queue (§8).
- **Durability of the speed USP: roughly 0–6 months** [estimate].

## 7. Sourcing and production plan (CZ)
- **Settings:**
  - 316L bezel pendants, rings and chains from EU wholesalers or AliExpress: €2–6. Ask for nickel-release certificates. The €3/item EU duty on non-EU parcels applies from 1 Jul 2026 [verified: https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/12/12/customs-council-agrees-to-levy-customs-duty-on-small-parcels-as-of-1-july-2026/].
  - 925 settings ≤ 3 g, already fineness-marked, from a CZ findings wholesaler: €10–18 [knowledge/estimate].
- **Resin:**
  - For made-to-order pieces, use jewelry-grade epoxy with UV stabiliser/HALS; makers cite HALS as the anti-yellowing measure [verified: https://welly.it.com/understanding-why-does-breast-milk-jewelry-turn-yellow-your-guide-to-preventing-discoloration].
  - For the kit, use UV resin, bought in retail bottles already CLP-labelled for CZ/SK.
- **Kit and equipment:**
  - Preservation powder, or your own recipe (pasteurise, preserve, dry, grind, sieve), matching the process Deník describes [verified: denik.cz].
  - Equipment: a second-hand dehydrator, a 0.01 g scale, silicone moulds, wet-sanding and polishing, and later a pressure pot.
- **Practice milk:** ask 5–8 friends or family for spare milk. Cow's milk trains the resin skills but not the milk-fat behaviour [knowledge].
- **Realistic capacity:** 2–6 pieces/week in 1–2 evening batches, with a hard cap of about 25 pieces/month (§8).

## 8. Time model
| Step (made-to-order, batched 4–6 per session) | min/unit |
|---|---|
| Pre-sale messages (design, size, anxious questions) | 15 |
| Collection-kit dispatch; milk intake, log, freeze | 10 |
| Preservation (pasteurise, preserve, dehydrator, grind, sieve) | 12 |
| Resin casting (mix, degas, pour, inclusions) | 10 |
| Demould, sand, polish, dome coat | 20 |
| Setting into bezel, cure check | 7 |
| QC, photos (customer and portfolio) | 8 |
| Pack, certificate, label, drop-off | 10 |
| Status updates and review request | 8 |
| Remake allowance (7% × 80 min) | 6 |
| **Total** | **≈ 106 min (1.75 h)** |

**Is it ≤ 1–2 h?**
- Making alone is about 65 min. With messages it is 1.75 h, which is at the top of the brief's limit.
- For a beginner it runs 3–4 h in months 1–2 [estimate].

**Elapsed time:**
- Milk in transit 1–3 days; drying 1–2 days; epoxy cure 1–2 days plus the dome coat 1 day.
- A QC hold of 3–7 days is advisable, because preservation errors show as discoloration early [verified: https://www.galactajewelry.com/will-breastmilk-jewelry-turn-yellow].
- Shipping 1–3 days. **Total 10–18 days:** 3 weeks is feasible, but 2 weeks means skipping the QC hold.

**Kit:** about 22 min (pick, pack, support).

**Fixed weekly load:** content and community 2.5 h, admin and VAT 0.5 h, sourcing and R&D 0.5 h.

| Volume | h/week |
|---|---|
| Realistic M12: 10 MTO + 6 kits/month | ≈ 8 h (≈ €14/h effective) |
| Optimistic M12: 25 MTO + 20 kits/month | ≈ 14.5 h, at the cap, and the 21-day promise starts to break |

## 11. CZ/EU legal checklist (product-specific)
| Topic | What applies | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Trade licence (živnost)** | Base-metal jewelry falls under the volná živnost [verified: BusinessInfo via quickstart §1.1]. Making precious-metal jewelry is the craft licence "Zlatnictví a klenotnictví" (§1.2). Whether gluing resin into *finished* 925 settings counts is **unclear** | Start with 316L only. Before silver, get a written answer from the trade office (živnostenský úřad) or use a responsible representative (odpovědný zástupce) |
| **Product or service?** | Under Civil Code § 2085, a made-to-order contract counts as a purchase *unless* the customer supplies a substantial part of the material. Otherwise it is a contract for work (smlouva o dílo, § 2586 ff.) [knowledge] | By the brief's own test (repair-on-demand for clients is excluded) this is a **productised service**. Only the kit is a pure product |
| **Withdrawal / warranty** | Personalised MTO is exempt from the 14-day withdrawal right. The kit is not. 24-month warranty on the jewelry. The withdrawal button is required from 19 Jun 2026 (quickstart §5.3) | T&Cs must define a defect: yellowing from sun or care is not a defect; preservation failure means a remake |
| **Liability for the milk** | Compensation for sentimental value ("cena zvláštní obliby", § 2969) applies only to intentional or malicious damage [knowledge], so the legal exposure is a refund. The **real exposure is reputational** in small mothers' groups | Ask for 2× the milk and archive 5 ml for remakes (industry practice [verified: https://treeofopals.com/how-long-will-breastmilk-jewelry-last/]). Risk sits with the customer until receipt. Liability insurance |
| **VAT** | Non-payer. The first Meta or Etsy fee triggers identified-person status (register within 15 days; monthly return by the 25th). EU B2C sales below €10k carry no VAT | Register before the first ad |
| **GPSR** | The founder is the manufacturer: risk file, order/batch ID, name and address, CZ/SK/DE warnings | Include an infant choking warning ("not a toy, keep the pendant from the baby"), plus nickel and resin notes |
| **REACH Annex XVII** | Nickel release (316L), lead and cadmium in settings | Get supplier test certificates |
| **Hallmarking** | Notify the Puncovní úřad before trading silver. **Silver > 3 g needs a hallmark; ≤ 3 g needs only a fineness mark.** Gold > 0.5 g needs a hallmark. Receipts must state weight and fineness. AML duties for precious-metal traders [verified: http://www.puncovniurad.cz/cz/faq.aspx, https://www.puncovnictvivpraxi.cz/vyrobce-a-obchodnik/23-zakladni-povinnosti-vyrobcu-a-obchodniku-se-sperky] | Silver ≤ 3 g only, no gold. Check how AML applies |
| **Human biological material** | No licensing rule for keepsakes that I know of. The SoHO Regulation (EU) 2024/1938 covers milk *for human application* only [knowledge] | Handle milk as potentially infectious: gloves, pasteurisation, dedicated tools (not the family kitchen's) |
| **Ashes** | Most German states have a burial obligation for urns (Friedhofszwang); dividing ashes is legally risky there. Carriers often refuse human remains [knowledge] | **Exclude ashes** |
| **Carriers** | Packeta and Česká pošta restrict liquids, perishables and dangerous goods [knowledge; Packeta T&C unverified] | Collection kit with a leak-proof tube and absorbent pad, sent quickly, or have the customer pre-mix the preservative |
| **GDPR** | Baby's name and birth date; breastfeeding may count as health data | Collect minimal data; get explicit consent before using photos |
| **CLP (kit)** | Epoxy part A: typically H315/H317/H319/H411. **Amine hardener part B: often H314, which means child-resistant cap, tactile warning, and ADR class 8 (UN2735), so post and Packeta refuse it.** UV resin: typically H315/H317/H319(/H411–412), not class 8; small UN3082 quantities are ADR-exempt under SP375. A re-packer or re-brander must do the CLP label in the local language, SDS and the UFI/PCN notification. Revised CLP formatting (Reg. 2024/2865) is phasing in [knowledge] | Kit uses **UV resin only**, in the **manufacturer's retail bottle, CLP-labelled in CZ/SK with UFI, unopened**. That makes the founder a distributor, not a re-packer. Post the supplier's SDS online. Don't sell into DE/PL without German/Polish-labelled stock |
| **WEEE** | N/A, *unless* the kit contains a UV torch or lamp. That would trigger WEEE plus battery EPR in each country | No lamp. Tell buyers to cure in sunlight or with their own nail lamp |
| **Packaging EPR** | CZ: under ~300 kg/year only records are needed. DE: LUCID plus a dual system before the first parcel. SK/PL distance-seller registration [knowledge, unverified] | Start CZ(+SK); check SK first |
| **IP** | MILKIES claims an "internationally patented" preservation method and a patented DIY set (liquid milk in resin, no drying) [verified: https://milkies-diy.de/] | Use the drying/powder method, check Espacenet, and avoid "milk-ies"-style marks |

## 12. Risks and mitigations
| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| No durable gap: incumbents add express tiers; the PL giant expands | High | Test only CZ/SK; kill quickly |

**Scorer objections, addressed:**
- **A** says the gap has closed, it is a service in disguise, nobody trusts a zero-review shop, and silver needs a licence. **Confirmed** for DE/PL, and all four points stand.
- **B** says the capacity cap binds the founder too, and steel dilutes the premium. **Confirmed**: the realistic ASP in CZ is about €68, not the hunter's €130.
- **C** says €50/h, yellowing, 5+ CZ competitors, and CLP/Packeta problems. The rate is really **€24–33/h per unit and about €14/h all-in**. The competitors are **8+ plus a Fler category**. The CLP/Packeta problem is solved only by using UV resin in retail bottles.
