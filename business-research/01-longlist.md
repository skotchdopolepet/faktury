## How to read this file
- **Evidence tags**
  - **[verified: URL]** means a hunter reports that the claim appears in a web-search result snippet for that URL. **No hunter could open any page**, because WebFetch was blocked for every marketplace, forum and government site, and the curator did no web research. "Verified" therefore means "URL-cited snippet", never "read first-hand". No sold count, Etsy sales counter or Heureka rank was read directly anywhere in this swarm.
  - **[knowledge]** means hunter or curator background knowledge that was not checked this session.
  - **[estimate]** means arithmetic or inference. The reasoning is given inline or in the hunter file.
- **Evidence grade**
  - **A:** several URL-cited signals that money is changing hands for this offer or a near-identical one (sales or review counts, sold-outs, paid rush tiers, rating counts on paid products, sold listings), **plus** URL-cited evidence of a gap (lead times, waitlists, a price spread with both sides cited, poor incumbent ratings, or absence in a crawled dataset).
  - **B:** URL-cited paying demand, or a strong verified demand driver alongside existing paid sellers, but the gap is inferred or only one side of it is measured.
  - **C:** mostly assertion or background knowledge, or "shops and listings exist" with no sales signal and no gap evidence.
- **Ranking:** IDs follow the curator's quick judgement of promise, most promising first. It weighs evidence grade, day-one CZ edge, money at risk, realistic profit and fit with 10–15 h/week. It is not a scored ranking.
- **Profit:** "12-mo profit" is monthly profit after about 12 months, as pessimistic / realistic / optimistic, before income tax and social/health levies. All figures are hunter **estimates**. Set aside 22–38% of profit for levies (quickstart §2.4).
- **Hunter names** are the file names in `hunt/`. "#n" is the candidate's number in that file.

## c. Traps and rejected ideas (all hunters, deduplicated)

Each entry gives a one-line reason, then the hunters who rejected it and any evidence they cited.

### Structural business-model traps
- **Dropshipping (China → EU/US, Shopify + Meta ads).** Only 10–20% are profitable in year 1. The EU levies €3 duty per item on low-value parcels from 1 Jul 2026, and US de minimis ended on 29 Aug 2025. — base-rates. Evidence: https://trueprofit.io/blog/dropshipping-success-rate, https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/12/12/customs-council-agrees-to-levy-customs-duty-on-small-parcels-as-of-1-july-2026/
- **Generic print-on-demand (t-shirts, mugs, posters).** Redbubble marketplace revenue fell 19% in FY25 with 542k selling artists, and there is no edge. — base-rates. Evidence: https://eightx.co/blog/asx-teardown-redbubble-2026, https://www.retailbiz.com.au/latest-news/articores-net-loss-widens-as-revenue-dips-in-fy25/
- **AI / low-content KDP books.** Uploads are capped at 3 titles a day, AI content must be disclosed, and the royalty on paperbacks under ~$9.99 fell from 60% to 50%. — base-rates. Evidence: https://authorsguild.org/news/amazon-adds-to-kdp-generative-ai-policy-caps-daily-self-publishing-uploads/
- **Amazon FBA private label in commodity categories.** Roughly half of sellers make ≤$1k a month and 45% quit in year 1; FBA costs are 20–30% above 2022 levels, and EU EPR applies per country. — base-rates. Evidence: https://amzprep.com/amazon-seller-profit/, https://www.businessradar.org/markets/amazon-fba-margin-squeeze-2026
- **Generic micro-SaaS or "passive" info products with no forced-demand hook.** About 70% stay under $1k MRR, and part-timers grow 2.2× slower. This matches the founder's failure mode. — base-rates. Evidence: https://saasranger.com/blog/micro-saas-revenue-reality-what-1000-founders-actually-earn/
- **Amazon/retail returns pallets and B-stock lots.** Negative expected value: cherry-picked manifests, WEEE and waste disposal costs, storage. — refurb-arbitrage [knowledge].

### Zero-marginal-cost digital commodities hit by AI and oversupply
- **Generic Etsy/Gumroad digital goods in English:** planners, printable wall art, SVG/Cricut files, Canva/Notion templates, budget spreadsheets. There are 1.6M "planner" results, the median Gumroad creator makes $72 a month, and sellers report AI-driven drops of 50–98%. — base-rates, digital-products. Evidence: https://www.accio.com/business/etsy-digital-planner-sales-trend-statistics, https://insightraider.com/en/state-of-gumroad-2026, https://www.digitaltrends.com/computing/etsy-sellers-are-quitting-as-ai-slop-floods-the-handmade-marketplace/
- **Knitting and crochet patterns, including Czech amigurumi.** 72.3% of Ravelry designers earn ≤$50 a month, and AI-generated fake patterns erode trust. — digital-products. Evidence: https://www.mediaperuana.com/blog1/designerincome, https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/etsy-crochet-buyers-suspect-ai-made-images-used-sell-patterns-rcna145878
- **Czech PDF sewing patterns (střihy).** At least 7 established CZ shops already serve it, and it needs drafting and grading skill. — digital-products. Evidence: https://www.fazonetka.cz/, https://www.sijemehrave.cz/
- **US teaching resources on TPT.** Most sellers make $0–100 a month, AI-made material is spreading, and the founder lacks US-curriculum expertise. — digital-products. Evidence: https://seolumina.com/blog/how-much-do-tpt-sellers-make-in-2026-real-data-income-breakdown, https://www.chalkbeat.org/2026/08/03/ai-slop-on-teachers-curriculum-marketplace/
- **Czech teacher marketplaces as a standalone business.** Author pay is "symbolic" and free, publicly funded supply is heavy. They are kept only as a channel for C22. — digital-products. Evidence: https://uciteleucitelum.cz/faq
- **Generic fantasy/D&D miniature STL Patreon.** Hundreds of competitors at ~$10 a month, and organic sculpting takes years to learn. — digital-products. Evidence: https://miniset.net/patreon/rating
- **Machine-embroidery files (Stickdateien).** AI auto-digitisers erode the skill moat, and custom digitising is a service. — digital-products. Evidence: https://absolutedigitizing.com/ai-embroidery-digitizer/
- **Fonts with CE diacritics.** Google Fonts is free, and a sellable family takes months to years of type-design skill. — digital-products [knowledge].
- **NFTs and digital collectibles.** Volumes are down more than 90%, and market cap fell to ~$4.9B by Oct 2025. — base-rates. Evidence: https://cryptonews.com/news/opensea-reinvents-itself-as-crypto-aggregator-amid-90-nft-volume-crash/

### Platform-rule and IP traps
- **Print farms reselling other people's STL designs, free or commercially licensed, on Etsy.** Since 10 Jun 2025, Etsy requires 3D-printed and laser items to be the seller's own design. Most 3D-print sellers make under $100 a year, and licensed characters invite IP takedowns. — base-rates, maker-fab. Evidence: https://www.tctmagazine.com/from-templates-to-originality-etsy-new-3d-printing-policy/, https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-creativity-standards-update-june-2025/, https://layermath.com/blog/how-profitable-is-selling-3d-prints
- **Gridfinity bins and baseplates.** Prices have collapsed to about $3.56 a bin, the design is open and free, and the buyers own printers. — maker-fab. Evidence: https://www.etsy.com/market/gridfinity
- **Physical tabletop wargaming terrain and GW "bits".** The Etsy own-design rule applies, Games Workshop IP brings takedowns, and the buyers own resin printers. — maker-fab. This does not apply to C26, which sells own-design files.
- **Board-game inserts.** Every game needs a new CAD project, the audience prints its own, and strong incumbents exist (Broken Token, e-Raptor). — maker-fab. Evidence: https://thetabletoptribune.com/insert-and-organizer-roundup/
- **FPV drone parts and mechanical-keyboard cases.** They sell to makers who print or CNC their own, against Chinese CNC group-buys. — maker-fab [knowledge].
- **Labubu / Pop Mart outfits and accessories.** IP exposure, a crowded handmade supply after the 2025 peak, and fad decay. — demand-signals.
- **Cosplay props.** Many hours per unit and heavy IP exposure. — hobby-niche.

## e. Cross-cutting insights

1. **The evidence base is thinner than it looks.** All 11 hunters worked without page access. Every "verified" item is a search snippet, and no sold count was read first-hand. The cheapest high-value next step is a direct check of eBay sold listings and Etsy sales and review counts for C01–C12 before any money is spent. Most candidates already name a "validate for €0–400" test.
2. **Most of the grade-A and top grade-B evidence comes from one pattern: margin locked in at purchase.** Buy under-priced Czech supply that has visible foreign price comps, add testing, cleaning or light modification, and sell across borders. This covers C01, C02, C03, C06, C07 and C12. It matches base-rates' finding that resale of used enthusiast goods beats "list and hope" models. These lines also **share one sourcing loop**: Bazoš, Aukro, Sbazar, estate (pozůstalost) and clearance lots, and flea markets such as U Elektry. A "Czech estate picker" feeding glass, garnet, cameras and lenses, and iPod donors from the same trips spreads the sourcing risk that every resale hunter names as the bottleneck.
3. **The second strong pattern is a forced supply cut or forced demand combined with a real product (not a service), where the buyer must act now.** Examples: Vorwerk ending TM31 parts (C11), the A2 format change (C08), the AMM rollout with an open HAN port (C10), and the DDR4 output cut (C12). The same logic fails when the deadline falls after the 12-month window (lead sinkers, C30), or when the regulation creates service work or platforms give the feature away (GPSR, EAA, withdrawal button, e-invoicing).
4. **The third pattern is capacity-constrained craft with long incumbent lead times.** Examples: breast-milk jewelry (C04), Kydex holsters (C09) and yarn advent calendars (C15). There the gap exists *because* the work is labour-bound, so the founder's 10–15 h cap binds too. The edge is turnaround speed plus a price-per-hour that works, not scale.
5. **CZ edges that recur across hunters:**
   - (a) Czech-language access to under-priced bazaar supply.
   - (b) Goods that only exist here: moldavite, Bohemian pyrope, Czechoslovak glass, ČSD prototypes, CZ pistols, Škoda.
   - (c) Czech-language and Czech-regulation digital moats: Shoptet with ~41k shops, the A2 exam, pre-school material, kniha jízd, HAN meters.
   - (d) Cheap intra-EU Packeta shipping, plus being an **EU-based** seller now that US de minimis has ended (29 Aug 2025), Brexit friction persists and the EU €3/item duty starts on 1 Jul 2026. This helps C01, C17, C18 and C35 against US and UK sellers.
   - (e) Prusa/Printables being headquartered in Prague.
6. **Traps that recur across hunters:**
   - (a) "CZ access" is worthless where makers or wholesalers already sell direct. Four hunters independently rejected Czech seed beads; Rautis, crystal, modrotisk and generic Jawa webshops fail for the same reason.
   - (b) Hyped used goods whose CZ asking prices already match exit prices: Mju II, CCD digicams, working iPods, ThinkPads.
   - (c) English, zero-marginal-cost digital goods crushed by AI supply.
   - (d) Etsy's 10 Jun 2025 own-design rule for 3D and laser items.
   - (e) Food, feed, cosmetics, electronics and live-goods compliance.
   - (f) Fads; C01, C03 and C07 all carry trend risk and should turn stock fast.
7. **3D-print and CAD ideas only work when selling physical-fit parts to non-makers.** Winners serve kitchen, van and car owners (C11, C14, C17, C18, C28). Makers print their own (Gridfinity, board-game inserts, TRX-4M, drone parts).
8. **Hunter conflicts resolved:**
   - Thermomix: demand-signals rejected it on food contact, maker-fab kept non-food SKUs → C14 without food-contact SKUs.
   - Wargaming terrain: maker-fab rejected physical prints, digital-products proposed an STL club → C26 as own-design files.
   - Jawa: cz-eu-edge proposed it, parts-b2b and maker-fab rejected webshops and repro plastics → C16 keeps only the eBay-channel and kit framing.
   - Game Boy: refurb-arbitrage proposed modded consoles, parts-b2b rejected the kit market → C29 at grade C.
   - Film cameras: base-rates proposed them but trapped hyped models, and demand-signals trapped CCD digicams → C07 buys untested lots and non-hyped bodies only.
9. **Legal cross-cuts (quickstart) that change the numbers for most candidates:**
   - The **first** Etsy, Meta or Google fee makes the founder an *identifikovaná osoba*: register within 15 days, then a monthly VAT return by the 25th with 21% VAT on those fees.
   - Social insurance is a **cliff** at 117,521 CZK of annual profit. Registering in Oct 2026 cuts the 2026 threshold to about 29,375 CZK, so consider starting on 1 Jan 2027.
   - Precious-metal jewelry *making* is a craft (řemeslná) licence (C03, C04, C24), so use partner workshops.
   - Electronics (C01, C10, C12, C28, C29) carry WEEE and battery EPR in **each destination country**, so start CZ(+SK)-only or budget for DE registration.
   - LUCID registration is needed before the first DE parcel.
   - Vinted requires Vinted Pro for business sellers.
   - The silver hallmark exemption conflicts between sources: ≤3.49 g per etsy-handmade's URLs vs ~1 g in the quickstart's recollection. Verify at puncovniurad.cz.
10. **Seasonality against the hours cap.** C15, C19 and C20 are Q4-heavy and exceed 15 h/week at the peak. Cap orders, or pair each with a counter-seasonal line (the sock club for C15).
