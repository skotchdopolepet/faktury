Killed by the orchestrator before a panel (see `06-verification.md`):
- **06 Shoptet add-on:** the gap is already filled (Shoptet's own iDoklad add-on and existing promo add-ons). The dossier forecast about €125/month.
- **08 Breast-milk jewelry:** a patented incumbent (MILKIES, 50k+ orders) plus 6+ CZ makers. The dossier verdict was no-go.
- **04 RAM part-out:** CZ sellers already price in the RAM value, and the price spike is topping out. The dossier forecast about €150/month.
- **03 Moldavite:** the cheap supply is illegally dug (a reserved mineral), and legal stock leaves only about a 2× spread. Viable only as an add-on line, at about €280–360/month.
- **07 Vintage garnet jewelry:** the spread is about 1× on like-for-like pieces, and hallmarking and AML duties apply. Viable only as a sub-line of 01, at about €375/month.

**Common findings across all reviewers:**
1. Every price the swarm saw was an **asking** price; the environment blocked sold-price data.
2. **Demand was real but supply was not short.** Competent sellers already exist in every niche.
3. Income is **bounded by the founder's hours**, so at 10–15 h/week the ceiling is roughly €500–700/month for hands-on resale.
4. There are EU cross-border traps: German WEEE registration for electronics, the €10k OSS threshold, reverse-charge VAT on platform fees (the identified-person trap) and Abmahnung risk. On top of that, US exports got 15% dearer after the end of de minimis, and EU sellers' US traffic on Etsy fell 20–70%.
