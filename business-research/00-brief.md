# Research brief: shared context for every agent in the swarm

## The founder (hard constraints)
- One person living in the **Czech Republic (EU)** with a full-time job. Available **~10–15 hours/week** (evenings plus some weekend time).
- Budget: **up to $10,000 (about €8,500 / 210,000 CZK)**, and ideally much less at risk before the idea shows traction.
- **NOT a service.** No selling hours: no freelancing, consulting, agency work, tutoring, repair-on-demand for clients or coaching. It must be a **product**:
  - **Physical**, where light manual work is fine (assembling, making jewelry, a small repair or refurb of roughly 1–2 hours per unit at most, finishing 3D prints), **or**
  - **Digital** (files, templates, software, assets), where manual work is mostly one-off creation.
- It must **actually make money**. The founder has already tried many ideas that sounded clever and novel and then didn't sell. Novelty earns zero points. **Proven, paying demand earns the points.**
- The target is an **asymmetric risk/reward profile**: demand clearly bigger than supply (supply-constrained, underserved, or sellers who can't keep up), plus some **edge from day one** (cost, location, access, skill, language or regulation). Loopholes are welcome but don't obsess over them. If it reliably makes money, it qualifies.
- Market: CZ and the rest of the EU first. Selling worldwide online is fine. Must be legally startable by a Czech resident as a side business while employed (vedlejší činnost).

## What "good" looks like
- A **specific** offer: a concrete product sold to concrete buyers on concrete channels at a concrete price.
  - Bad: "sell jewelry", "3D printing business", "digital products".
  - Good: "Czech-garnet minimalist stacking rings in 925 silver, sold on Etsy to US/DE buyers at €70–120, cast by a Turnov workshop, 15 min finishing and photography per piece."
- **Evidence that money is changing hands now.** Examples: marketplace sales or review counts, eBay *sold* listings, Etsy shop sales counts, Amazon BSR, Allegro/Heureka rankings, Kickstarter/pre-order data, public income reports.
- **Evidence of a supply gap.** Examples: sold-out or restock waitlists, long processing times at top sellers, second-hand prices above retail, "where can I buy X" threads, few sellers carrying many sales, a regulation that forces new demand.
- **Realistic numbers** with all costs included: platform fees, payment fees, shipping from CZ, packaging, returns under the EU 14-day withdrawal right, ads, taxes and VAT where relevant, and the founder's time.
