# Side-business research: final report

**Date:** 2026-09-29 · **For:** the founder in [`00-brief.md`](00-brief.md)

> **Bottom line**
> - **No candidate passed the bar.** Across 35 candidates, 10 deep dives and 15 adversarial reviews, nothing qualifies as a reliable, asymmetric €1,000+/month side business at 10–15 h/week.
> - **Best realistic outcome: ≈ €250–600/month by month 12, at ≈ €6–15/h.** That is a paid hobby, not an asymmetric bet.
> - **(A) If you start one thing:** one "Czech vintage & analog" EU shop (mid-century glass + mechanical cameras/lenses). Only if you enjoy flea-market hunting: the hunting *is* the job.
> - **(B) Cheapest, fastest test:** modded iPods, CZ-only, ≤ €500.
> - **(C) The only structural asymmetry found:** a pre-order yarn "drop" for the 2027 season. Least proven.
> - **(D) Optional:** 20 Czechoslovak LPs on Discogs, mainly to get real sold prices.
> - **Next 6 weeks:** A + B in parallel, ≤ €1,500 cash, hard kill numbers in §3.
> - **The real next step is yours:** answer the 10 questions in §7.

## 1. Verdict

No candidate passed the bar. The swarm ran about 70 agents: 11 hunters, then 35 candidates, 3 scorers, 10 dossiers, web verification, 15 adversarial reviews and a blind-spot critic. It found no idea that survives as a reliable, asymmetric €1,000+/month side business for an employed person working 10–15 h/week.

The best realistic outcomes are **≈ €250–600/month by month 12, at ≈ €6–15/h** before tax and levies. That is paid-hobby territory; at the low end it is close to the Czech minimum wage (≈ €5.3–5.5/h gross).

Reviewers cut every forecast they examined by 45–75%. Examples: glass €575 → ≈ €275, cameras €600 → ≈ €300, iPods €500 → ≈ €275. The main reason is that every price the swarm saw was an asking price, not a sold price ([`08-review-tally.md`](08-review-tally.md)).

What follows covers the cheap tests still worth running, what to avoid, why the search came up short, and what we need from you.

### A. Best bet to actually start: one "Czech vintage & analog" shop

**This is not asymmetric.** Demand, not supply, is the limit. The glass dossier sources ~40 saleable pieces a month but expects to sell only 15–25 ([dossier 01](dossiers/01-vintage-czech-glass.md) §7, [`reviews/01-demand.md`](reviews/01-demand.md)). You earn a buy-low spread plus your labour. **Only do this if you enjoy flea-market hunting, because the hunting IS the job.**

The shop has one storefront and sells to EU buyers first. Sourcing trips are shared across lines: pozůstalost (estate lots), vyklízení (house clearances), bleší trhy (flea markets) and Bazoš.

**What to expect.**
- Reviewers' realistic month-12 figures for each line on its own:

- Each line alone takes 8–11 h/week, so combining them does not double income. What it buys you is shared trips and the freedom to keep only the items with the best €/h.
- **Expect €300–600/month if it works.**
- The ceiling is ≈ €500–700 before levies, where three limits meet:
  - your hours;

### B. Cheapest, fastest test: modded iPods, CZ-only

**The offer** ([dossier 05](dossiers/05-modded-ipod.md)): buy broken iPod Classic/Video units in CZ as donors, fit flash storage and a new battery, and sell them with a 12-month warranty.

**What the reviewers found:**
- **Stay CZ-only for good.** All three reviewers agree. Selling in Germany or on Etsy would add ≈ €0–150/month but cost €1,200–1,700/yr in WEEE, battery and packaging compliance ([`reviews/05-execution.md`](reviews/05-execution.md)).
- **Plan on 4,490 Kč, not 5,290 Kč.** Modded units are already listed on Aukro and Bazoš, and AliExpress refurbs cap prices ([`reviews/05-demand.md`](reviews/05-demand.md)).
- **Realistic result: ≈ €220–300/month at €11–14/h.** It is capped by ≈ 5–8 good donors a month and by a fading fad.

**Before buying anything:**
- **Solve the battery source.** The reviewer found no CZ distributor of the 2,000/3,000 mAh upgrade cells, and importing them yourself makes you a battery producer.
- **Ship with a cable only**, no mains charger.

### C. The only structural asymmetry: a pre-order "drop"

**The offer** ([`01-longlist.md`](01-longlist.md) C15): a hand-dyed yarn advent calendar (24 × 20 g mini-skeins) plus a sock club. Customers pay before you make anything, and you buy base yarn only after the cash arrives.

**The evidence:**
- German indie dyers confirm "many hand-dyed calendars are already sold out, some already in summer pre-sale" ([`06-verification.md`](06-verification.md)).
- Hue Loco and Sweet Sparrow sold out in 2025 ([`02-main-loop-evidence.md`](02-main-loop-evidence.md)).

**Caveats:**
- **Less proven.** There was no dossier and no review, and it scored only 47.5 (rank 15).
- **It needs a craft skill:** dyeing and colourway photography. More than 8 Czech dyers and many German dyers already compete.
- **The 2026 window is closed.** Make samples over the winter and open the pre-sale in **June–July 2027**.
- **It is still capped by hours:** about 100 calendars at ~15 h/week in Sep–Oct. That peak collides with shop A's Q4.
- **Gate:** at least 30 paid pre-orders in 4 weeks ([`07-blind-spots.md`](07-blind-spots.md) A3); otherwise refund everyone and stop. The loss is capped at the samples (€600–800).

### D. Optional micro-test: Czechoslovak vinyl on Discogs

## 4. Legal minimum before the first sale

1. **Živnost** (volná, 800 Kč online, IČO in ≤ 5 working days).
   - Fields: *Velkoobchod a maloobchod* and *Zastavárenská činnost a maloobchod s použitým zbožím* (verify the exact name), plus the electronics production/repair field for the iPods.
   - You need your employer's consent only if you compete with them (§ 304).
2. **The identified-person trap (identifikovaná osoba).**
   - It is triggered by the first Etsy, eBay or Catawiki fee, and equally by any Meta or Google ad, foreign SaaS or AI tool.
   - You must register within **15 days**, file a monthly VAT return by the **25th**, and pay **21% on the fees**, with no deduction.
   - A CZ-only iPod line on Bazoš/Aukro with no paid ads avoids it.
3. **AML seller identification.**
   - A used-goods trader must identify the seller on **every** purchase, whatever its value, and keep the records for 5 years ([`reviews/01-execution.md`](reviews/01-execution.md)).
   - One purchase slip (name, date of birth, address, ID number, item, price, signature) covers both AML and tax evidence.
   - Pay Bazoš sellers by bank transfer, and confirm the scope with the advisor.
4. **Electronics stay CZ-only.**
   - Germany requires stiftung ear WEEE registration through an authorised representative (≈ €800–1,200 in year 1) and brings Abmahnung (legal warning letter) risk. SK and every other country need their own registration.
   - SLRs with battery meters count as electronics; manual lenses and bodies with no battery don't.
   - iPods: use CZ-sourced donors (or join ASEKOL/REMA/ELEKTROWIN) and CE-documented cells.
5. **Before the first German parcel:** LUCID + dual-system registration, and German legal texts (Impressum, AGB, Widerrufsbelehrung, the exact 12-month used-goods wording, a note that you are not VAT-registered). Disable Austria and France at first.
6. **GPSR and consumer law:**
   - Fill in the marketplace GPSR fields.
   - Describe glass as "decorative, not for food use".
   - iPods need a risk sheet, a serial number and your address.
   - Buyers have 14 days to withdraw. A 12-month used-goods warranty applies only if agreed; assume 24 months in Germany.
7. **Trackers:**
   - €10,000 EU B2C sales (OSS);
   - the 117,521 CZK social-insurance threshold;
   - DAC7 (≥ 30 sales or ≥ €2,000 per platform).

   Make no business sales from a private Vinted account.

## 6. Why no banger was found

1. **Efficient markets arbitrage away visible gaps.**
   - Czech asking prices for tested cameras, mju compacts and working iPods already match what they sell for abroad.
   - RAM is already priced at its used value.
   - Collectors outbid you for named glass, and incumbents own moldavite.
   - The swarm "measured supply and called it demand": listing counts show supply, not unmet demand ([`07-blind-spots.md`](07-blind-spots.md) §1).
2. **Hands-on resale is capped by hours.** ~52 h/month at €10–20/h caps profit at €500–1,000 whatever the idea, and reviewers measured €6–14/h. The OSS threshold and the social-insurance dead zone bite at the same level.
3. **US tariffs and lost Etsy traffic.**
   - US de minimis ended on 29 Aug 2025, with a 15% duty on EU goods.
   - Etsy's organic traffic for international sellers fell 20–50%; one UK seller reported −70%.
   - The Czech vintage shops that proved the model sold ~90% of their goods to the US.
4. **EU cross-border compliance taxes small sellers.** Each piece is small, but together they eat the margin on a €20–40 item:
   - WEEE and battery registration per country;
   - the OSS threshold;
   - packaging EPR;
   - GPSR;
   - monthly identified-person returns;
   - German legal warning letters (Abmahnungen);
   - AML duties.
5. **A methodology limit.** WebFetch was blocked and the search budget ran out. Every price was an asking price, and no sold count was read first-hand. That is the main reason reviewers cut forecasts by 45–75%.

## 7. How to actually find a banger: we need your input

Real asymmetry usually comes from an **unfair advantage you already have**: a skill, trade knowledge, a supply source, a community, a language, or insight from your employer's industry. Ideas that need no skill are crowded by definition, and the swarm never asked what you can do ([`07-blind-spots.md`](07-blind-spots.md) §1.4). Please answer:

1. What do you know better than 95% of people?
2. What does your day job expose you to (suppliers, recurring customer problems, B2B purchasing, regulation)? Could that industry be a B2B channel?
3. Which hobbies do you already spend money and weekends on, and what is hard to get there?
4. Which skills do you have now: soldering, sewing, woodworking, dyeing, CAD/3D printing, photography, coding (which languages; have you shipped anything)?
5. Do you have a workshop, storage, land/garden/chalupa, a car for pickups, or machines?
6. Do you have privileged supply: a family business, a manufacturer, wholesaler or clearance firm you know, or factory seconds?
7. Which communities trust you (a group you run, a club, an expat community), and how big are they?
8. Which languages can you sell in?
9. Would you accept a 6–12-month build at ≈ €0 income for a digital product that compounds?
10. What have you tried before, with numbers? What won't you do? And is the target €300/month of enjoyable work, or €1,000+/month even if dull?

**For the next run:**
- **Widen network access** so agents can read sold prices: eBay.de/.com sold listings, Etsy, Bazoš, Aukro ended auctions, Discogs, Catawiki, Kleinanzeigen, WorthPoint. Raise the search budget too.
- **Aim the swarm at your answers.**
- **Rank candidates in this order** ([`07-blind-spots.md`](07-blind-spots.md) §4–5):
  1. the mechanism that keeps supply short: skill, licence, biology, finite stock sold into a want list, or pre-sale;
  2. direct demand measures: deposits, want counts, the ratio of "Koupím" (wanted) ads to offers, waitlists;
  3. listing counts, last.

## Appendix C. Methodology

About 70 agent runs, in this order:
1. **11 hunters** ([`hunt/`](hunt/base-rates.md)), one lens each: base rates, consumables, CZ/EU edge, demand signals, digital products, digital software, Etsy handmade, hobby niches, maker/fab, parts/B2B, refurb arbitrage.
2. A curator merged them into **35 candidates** ([`01-longlist.md`](01-longlist.md)), alongside the legal baseline ([`cz-legal-tax-quickstart.md`](cz-legal-tax-quickstart.md)).
3. Web evidence gathered by the orchestrator ([`02-main-loop-evidence.md`](02-main-loop-evidence.md)).
4. **3 scorers** ([`03-scoring.md`](03-scoring.md)).
5. **10 deep dives** ([`dossiers/`](dossiers/01-vintage-czech-glass.md), [instructions](04-deepdive-instructions.md)).
6. **Web verification by the orchestrator**, which killed or downgraded 5 dossiers without a panel ([`06-verification.md`](06-verification.md)).
7. **15 adversarial reviews**, covering 5 dossiers × the demand, economics and execution lenses ([`reviews/`](reviews/01-demand.md), [instructions](05-review-instructions.md), [tally](08-review-tally.md)).
8. A **blind-spot critic** ([`07-blind-spots.md`](07-blind-spots.md)).
9. This synthesis.

## Appendix D. Limitations

- **No page access.** Every "verified" claim is a search snippet, and no sold count was read first-hand. **All prices are asking prices.**
- **The search budget ran out.** Each reviewer had at most one search.
- **Your skills and assets are unknown.** Ideas that need a skill were scored as risky, while those that need none proved crowded.
- **The lenses covered products and channels**, not buyers (municipalities, clubs) or mechanisms (licences, pre-sales, want lists).
- **Some numbers were never reviewed:** dossiers 03, 04, 06, 07 and 08, plus C15 and the vinyl idea.
- **Fees, shipping prices and many legal points are [knowledge]**, and US tariffs are volatile. Book the advisor hour before the first sale.
- **The exchange rate is assumed** at 24.5 CZK/€; some files use 25.
